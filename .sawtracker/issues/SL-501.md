**Found by:** std496 (SL-496). **Area:** typecheck, the declaration-site check for a public generic's inferred requirements.

**The rule:** LANGUAGE_SPEC, "A `public` generic must DECLARE what it requires":
> a `public` generic whose body needs the `Copy` tier says so in its signature, and the compiler asks for it at the declaration. Declaring a bound the body then EXCEEDS is also an error: `<T: ExplicitCopy>` licenses a spelled `.copy()` and nothing more.

**The defect:** sawc2 computes D27's inferred `Copy` requirement for a generic body (`implied_copies`, `copy_needs`) and checks it at each call. It never refuses a PUBLIC generic, function or method, whose body has such a requirement but whose signature doesn't declare it. The new std's `Vector.get` was written that way and passed.

**Owed:**
- At a public generic's declaration, an inferred `Copy` requirement on a type parameter must be refused unless the signature, or the enclosing bounded extension, declares `T: Copy`. The refusal names the body's copy site.
- The second half: a declared `T: ExplicitCopy` whose body silently copies is refused.
- Sweep public free functions, public methods in unbounded extensions, public trait default bodies, and requirements that arrive through a callee's inferred requirement (transitive).
- Census std/ and `compiler/` first. Stage 0 builds sawc2's own source, but its generics are mostly non-public; report any public one that trips.
