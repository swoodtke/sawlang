**Decided with the user (Oct 10).** This is a mechanical cleanup of `compiler/` and `std/`, queued for the gap after SL-505 and SL-456.p3 merge and before U7b or SL-513 dispatch, since it touches nearly every compiler file.

**1. Stage packages map to their `src/`.** Today `compiler/tools/build.py` maps `--module-path sawmir=compiler/mir`, one level above the source, so every import spells the directory: `import sawmir.src.api`. The spec's `--module-path NAME=DIR` makes DIR the source directory (`import NAME` reaches `DIR/lib.saw`, and `import NAME.sub` reaches `DIR/sub.saw`), and Blade passes `<dep>/src`.
- `STAGE_PACKAGES` maps to `compiler/<stage>/src`, including U7a's `mono`, `target` and `llvm`;
- every `sawX.src.Y` import becomes `sawX.Y`, and `sawlex.src.lib` becomes `import sawlex`;
- sources and the stages' `tests/` are both covered;
- every other place that writes those module paths follows: the std lane's `driver_arguments`, the `run.py` lanes, the cone tools, the READMEs, and `compiler/README.md`'s `<package>.src.<module>` paragraph.

**2. Field visibility is stated once, on the type.** The spec ("A field's default is its declaring type's visibility … The tier is stated once, on the type.") makes a field's written tier redundant when it repeats the struct's. Both compilers implement the inheritance (lead probe: a bare field of a `public struct` is readable and writable from another module, and a `private` one is refused by both).
- Drop the redundant marker: `public` on a field of a `public struct`, and `public(package)` on one of a `public(package) struct`. There are about 818 sites in 46 `compiler/` files, plus 7 in `std/`.
- Keep `private` narrowings (44).
- Leave test FIXTURES whose subject is syntax untouched: lexer and parser goldens such as `compiler/tests/lex/doc_comment_attachment.saw`, and the grammar corpus.
- `///` doc comments stay attached to their fields.

**3. Keep it that way:**
- a subset-checker rule (`redundant-visibility`) refuses a field whose written tier equals its struct's, over `compiler/` and `std/`, with a fixture per form;
- the saw-lang skill gains the idiom.

**Gate:**
- `compiler/tests/run.py`;
- `battery.sh compiler citations grammarcorpus astdiff`;
- the std lane's driver self-check, which must stay at zero refusals;
- `irdet` is not owed, since no IR change is expected.

Prove it mechanical: sawc2's `--emit-docs`, or the typecheck dump, of the driver build is identical before and after, apart from module names.

@claude-air-reviewer **SL-518.p1 is up for review: a mechanical cleanup** (sl518, 3 commits squashed: 131 files, +1865/−1812). It changes no behaviour, and both halves were decided with the user.

1. **Stage packages map to their `src/` (`2c955481`).**
   - `STAGE_PACKAGES` maps all eleven stages to `compiler/<stage>/src`.
   - 891 imports were rewritten in 108 files: `sawX.src.Y` becomes `sawX.Y`, and `sawlex.src.lib.{…}` becomes `sawlex.{…}`. Both compilers accept that spelling; sl518 probed `import sawlex.{lex, LexResult}` under each. Every import was selective, so no qualifier changed and no other code moved.
   - Followers: `grammar_tables.py` (the generated `grammar.saw` import), `subset_check.stage_files()`, both `frozen_check.py`, and the READMEs.
   - **Dump check:** typecheck, `--interfaces`, mir, borrowck and drops of the driver build are identical before and after, once the old module names are renamed. Resolve differs only by import-line columns, and the five lexer import records name `sawlex` instead of `lib`.
2. **A field states its visibility only to narrow it (`1d5e06b3`).** This is the spec's "The tier is stated once, on the type".
   - 894 `public` markers were dropped from fields of `public struct`s: 886 in `compiler/`, 8 in `std/`. The 64 `private` narrowings are kept.
   - **Proof:** with the markers blanked to same-width spaces, every dump is byte-identical. With them removed, only columns move.
   - Left as is, and listed in the report: 5 typecheck fixtures (their goldens would only move columns), `tests/corpus/`, and `prototypes/`.
3. **Subset rule `redundant-visibility` (`1f48215a`).** It reads Stage 0's record of whether a field's tier was written, and runs over `compiler/` and the `--std` profile. Its fixtures cover `public`/`public` (one per line and on one line) and `public(package)`/`public(package)`, plus the std profile, each with a `private` control and a bare-field control. The README rule table has its row. The saw-lang skill gains the two-line idiom.
