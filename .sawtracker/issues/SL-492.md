**Found by:** batch B2 (tcb2), as a sibling of SL-487. **Area:** typecheck, binding over an optional place.

**The defect:** `if var x = e`, where `e` is a conditional lend (`borrows -> &T?` or `&var T?`), is still accepted. SL-487 fixed the statement form of `borrow`, but the `if var` / `if let` binding over a conditional lend's payload is a separate mechanism: binding over an optional place. The spec's spelling for unwrapping a conditional lend is `if borrow let x = e` / `if borrow var x = e`. A plain `if var x = e` over a lend is either:
- a getitem-style copy of the payload, so it is refused for NoCopy and needs `.copy()` for ExplicitCopy, as D29 does for plain subscripts; or
- a write-capable binding through a possibly read-only lend.

**To settle first:** which reading applies, in two cases. When `e` is a conditional lend call written without `borrow`, the first reading follows from §5.1's "spelling picks the role". When `e` lends `&T` and the binding is `var`, writes to `x` must never reach the lent storage.

**Sweep (obligation 4):** `if let`, `if var`, `guard let`, `while let`, and `match` with a binding pattern, each over:
- a conditional lend through `&self`;
- one through `&var self`;
- a cell-carrying `&self` lend (`try_lock`).

Probe write-through and copy behaviour in each case, and compare with Stage 0.

The optional-place half is done on the tcb2b branch, commit "SL-492: a binding over a lent optional place copies…". The lead ruled option (a), so the plain-call half keeps the copy reading. The unenforced "only through `borrow`" rule is filed as SL-497.

- **Rule:** over a place rooted in a `borrow` binding (the binding, or an optional field of one), `if var`, `guard var` and `while var` are refused under the new rule `borrowing.var-unwrap`. The hint names `if|while borrow var x = e` and `if|guard|while let x = e`.
- **Copies:** `if let`, `guard let`, `while let` and `case Some(x)` copy the payload, and a NoCopy payload is refused under `transfer.implicit-copy` with a hint naming `borrow let`. Previously `case Some(x)` over a borrow binding aliased the payload.
- **Covered:** all three lends, the `&self`, the `&var self` and the cell-carrying one.
- **Fixtures:** `borrowing.var-unwrap` with the variants `.shared`, `.cell`, `.guard`, `.while` and `.field`; `transfer.implicit-copy.lent-if`, `.lent-guard`, `.lent-while` and `.lent-match`; and the golden `lent_unwrap`.
