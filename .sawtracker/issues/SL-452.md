Unit 6c of epic SL-398, the bootstrap slice's third semantic stage: MIR lowering (SL:architecture §3.5). The package is `compiler/mir/`, package `sawmir`. It depends on `sawtypecheck` and reads typecheck's side tables unchanged: transfers, adjustments, call targets, summaries. Its output is a new IR, MIR, per function (§3.0).

**Two units, landing in order,** each briefed as a comment here when the one before lands, and each a patch on this issue:
1. **U6c1, the MIR and its lowering:**
   - the data model: locals, basic blocks, places with projections, statements, terminators, place-based and not SSA, as ruled;
   - lowering of every expression, statement and pattern form in the slice;
   - the one operand funnel;
   - evaluation order, fixed once;
   - `match` as a decision tree;
   - explicit borrow windows (`window_open`/`window_close`, with the accessor as an opaque two-half callee), `ref` rvalues and closures as capture aggregates;
   - scope-end `drop`s;
   - suspension points and op-budget points (with their class);
   - `sawc2 mir --dump`, and the MIR verifier.
2. **U6c2, accessors and the interpreter:**
   - an accessor lowered as two halves around `lend`, with its state record (the shape §3.9 reuses);
   - a MIR interpreter scoped to the slice. It is the stage's oracle, since there is no code generation to run yet. Unit programs run on it, and their output is compared with the same program built by Stage 0. The interpreter is also the future `constexpr` evaluator (§3.10).

**Acceptance, for the stage:**
- every function of `compiler/`'s own source lowers, with the verifier passing;
- **the central invariant:** after this stage nothing about ownership is implicit. Every operand is `move`, `copy` or a constant, and every owned local's scope exits carry their drops;
- the compiler's unit programs run on the MIR interpreter with the output Stage 0's builds give;
- `tests/corpus/` coverage is reported, for information only.

**Scope:** the bootstrap slice, as before. A construct outside it is refused as `slice.not-yet`, and never lowered wrong. Coroutine lowering (§3.9) is not in it. Suspension points are marked, and nothing more, since `compiler/` is sync-only.

# U6c1: the MIR and its lowering (the first of two MIR units)

- **Epic:** SL-398, unit 6c. The issue's description gives the split and the stage's acceptance.
- **Base:** main after SL-447 (U6b, type checking) is complete.
- **Design:**
  - SL:architecture §3.0 (shared) and §3.5 (MIR lowering, all of it);
  - §3.6–§3.7, for what the borrow check and drop elaboration will read, since the MIR must carry it;
  - SL:borrowing §2 (the window forms) and §5 (subscript roles);
  - designs 127 (the op budget) and 219 (generic forwarding);
  - typecheck's READMEs, for its side tables: transfers, adjustments, call targets and roles, summaries.
- **Package:** `compiler/mir/`, package `sawmir`. It depends on `sawtypecheck`, is registered in `STAGE_PACKAGES`, and is written in the bootstrap subset, with the checker and markers.

## Decisions (the lead)
1. **The data model is §3.5's, exactly:**
   - a function is locals plus basic blocks;
   - a place is a local plus a projection path (field, index, deref, payload);
   - a statement assigns an rvalue to a place;
   - a block ends in exactly one terminator: goto, switch, call, return or unreachable.

   Types are typecheck's interned `TypeId`s, rendered by canonical spelling. Locals, blocks and statements are module-local typed ids (§3.0). A call's target is the symbolic declaration identity plus its instantiation.
2. **ONE operand funnel** builds every operand from typecheck's recorded transfer (brief obligation 1). Its docstring lists its entry points: constructions, arguments, returns, captures, assignments, and compiler-synthesized calls (operators, subscript roles, `for`'s `next`, conversions).
   - It never invents a move.
   - A named Copy place copies, calling its `copy()` hook if it declares one, even at its last use.
   - A generic `T` local's single-path read is typecheck's implicit `move` (the per-path rule, D20).
   - A transfer typecheck left unrecorded is an INVARIANT, never a guess.
3. **Adjustments lower to explicit rvalues or statements:** auto-wrap into `Some`/`Ok`/`Err`, `widen`, `adopt` (a constant), a slice, erasure, and the receiver's implicit `ref(shared | exclusive)`. After this stage, no conversion is implicit.
4. **The dump is text, one function at a time,** readable in the style of Rust's MIR, and specified in `compiler/tests/mir/README.md`. It shows:
   - locals with types;
   - blocks with statements and a terminator;
   - `move`/`copy` on every operand;
   - `drop` points;
   - window open and close, with their edges;
   - suspension points and op-budget points, with their class.

   Invocation is `sawc2 mir (--dump | --check)`, with the other subcommands' flags.

## Scope
**Lower** every expression, statement and pattern form the typecheck stage accepts in the slice:
- control flow as blocks and terminators, `if`/`while`/`for`/`loop`/`break`/`continue`/`return`/`guard`;
- **evaluation order,** fixed once as §3.5 lists it:
  - left to right;
  - a receiver and each key expression evaluated once, into temporaries;
  - an assignment's right side before its left side's borrow;
  - short-circuit operators, `??`, and `try`'s propagation as explicit branches;
- **`match`** as a decision tree: switches on discriminants, and tests on literals, ranges and guards;
- **borrow windows:** a `borrow` block is `window_open(accessor, args)`, with a present edge and, for a conditional lend, an absent edge; then the body; then `window_close` on every edge leaving it. The statement form closes as soon as its value is copied out. `for` is a window on its head plus a loop calling `next`. A plain `&x` argument is a `ref(mode, place)` rvalue. The accessor is an opaque two-half callee here, and its own split is U6c2;
- **closures** as aggregates of their captures: by-value captures move or copy per the funnel, and reference captures are `ref`s;
- **scope-end drops:** every exit of a scope, a `break`/`continue`/`return`/`?` included, gets `drop(local)` for each owned local in scope, in reverse declaration order, meaning "drop if still initialised" (§3.7 makes it precise). A panic aborts, so there are no cleanup edges;
- **suspension points:** a call whose callee may suspend is marked, using typecheck's summaries;
- **op-budget points** on every loop backedge of every function, each classed yield-capable or charge-only (inside a `borrows(sync)` window).

**Not this unit:** the accessor's two-half split and state record, and the MIR interpreter (U6c2); the borrow check, drop elaboration, mono and coroutine lowering (later units). A construct outside the slice is `slice.not-yet`, and never lowered wrong.

## Tests, test-first
- **Golden dumps** per aspect under `compiler/tests/mir/`, with a lane in `compiler/tests/run.py`. At least: operands (every funnel entry point), evaluation order, short-circuit and `??`, decision trees, windows (block, statement, conditional, `for`), closures, drops at every exit kind, and budget points.
- **The MIR verifier** runs on every lowered function:
  - every block has exactly one terminator, and every edge targets a real block;
  - every place and operand is typed and in range;
  - every operand is `move`, `copy` or a constant;
  - every `window_open` is matched by a `window_close` on every path leaving its body;
  - every owned local has drops at every scope exit;
  - every call names a target and its instantiation;
  - no conversion is left implicit.
- **Acceptance:** every function of `compiler/`'s own source (the sawc2 build and each unit program) lowers with the verifier passing, with no refusal and no invariant. Report `tests/corpus/` coverage for information.
- There is no frozen cross-check this unit: the interpreter in U6c2 is the stage's oracle. Keep the dump deterministic and stable, since U6c2 will build on it.

## Rules
- **Where to work:** `compiler/mir/`, `compiler/tests/mir/`, the driver, `compiler/tools/build.py`, `compiler/tests/run.py`, and `compiler/README.md` (its lists).
- **Typecheck gaps:** a missing recorded fact is reported, and fixed only with the lead's approval in chat, in its own commit with a pin.
- **`sawc/` is frozen.** A blocking Stage 0 bug stops that item, with a minimal repro, and any workaround carries a marker for a hazard SL:hazards names.
- **Decisions:** a reversible reading goes in the report, and a change of meaning stops that item.
- **Measured performance:** report the time to lower `compiler/`.
