## Description

**A temporary made by `(move r).w` is dropped at the end of the function, not at the end of its statement** (found by the lead's triage of the coroutine corpus's wrong rows, Oct 25).

`tests/corpus/conformance/V104_unbound_value_is_released.saw`, the MOVES row:

```saw
let r = Res(w: 4)
print("move {}", (move r).w)
```

The program expects `move 4` then `drop 4` on the next line. sawc prints `move 4`, then the whole rest of the sync block, and `drop 4` only at the end of `main`'s sync section, after `drop 8`. So the value is released once, which means no leak, but at the wrong point. V104's guarantee is that a value no binding holds is released at its consumption site.

**Mechanism (UNVERIFIED):** the moved-out value of a `move` expression used as a projection base is treated as still owned by `r`'s scope rather than as a temporary of the statement. The other producers in the row (a call, `try`, `!`, `if`, `match`, `??`, an init) all drop at their statement.

**Obligation 4 sweep:** `move x` as a projection base (`.field`, `.method()`, a subscript), as a scrutinee, as a discarded statement, and as an argument, in sync and driven bodies, with a NoCopy type that has a printing `deinit`. Check the driven half of V104 too: its driven rows have no `move` row today.
