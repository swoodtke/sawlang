## Description

**Typecheck grows superlinearly on a tuple `match` with guarded wildcard arms: r = 3.78, on main.** Found by SL-979 unit B, measuring a complexity-lane shape for the new `match` lowering.

**Evidence:** instructions retired for the shape `match_tuple_guarded`. One `match (a, b)` cycles through `(v, _) if g`, `(_, v)`, `(v, w)`, `(v, _)` and `(_, _) if g`, with distinct values and a final `case _`. What MIR adds on top of typecheck is linear. Main (40f9adfea) measures the same typecheck numbers.

**Likely place (UNVERIFIED):** the exhaustiveness and duplicate-arm check over the tuple's pattern matrix (`compiler/typecheck/src/exhaust.saw`). A profile of `typecheck --check` on this shape would confirm it.

**SL-983.p1: typecheck is near-linear on tuple `match` shapes**

- **Mechanism (profiled):** `check_duplicate_arms` compared each unguarded arm with every earlier one, A²/2 comparisons, each re-reading literal text and rebuilding parts. At N=1,600, 149 of 265 samples sat there, and the exhaustiveness check never showed.
- **Fix:** each arm gets a canonical key: a wildcard, a case or tuple with its arity and parts' keys, or a literal's value. Ranges and misfit literals get no key. Two arms share a key exactly when one repeats the other, and a map finds the first holder. Key equality is an equivalence, so that holder is the arm the pairwise scan found, and every refusal keeps its order and line.
  - This subsumes SL-970 unit 5's per-kind literal tables and its pairwise structural loop. Both are removed, leaving one mechanism.
  - `tc_same_values_key` sits beside `tc_same_values`, so the literal-equality rule keeps one home.
- **Typecheck r, 13 shapes:** from 3.5–3.96 to 1.98–2.12. That covers `wild`, `noguardwild`, `cycle5`, `gwild`, `match_tuple_guarded`, guards in several columns, 3- and 4-column tuples, nested tuples, enum payloads and `Optional` of a tuple.
- **Identity:** 1,206 programs (1,204 random duplicate-heavy ones plus two generated ones, with 30,061 duplicate-arm refusals) give the same exit status, stdout and stderr as origin/main's binary.
- **Lane:** adds `match_tuple_guarded 1600 2.07 0.10`; main's rows are unchanged. `match_tuple_wild` is owed to SL-979 unit C, since its MIR lowering is still quadratic.
- **Gates:**
  - `run.py --only seed,units,golden,subset,fixture_whitespace,std,typechecker,front_parts,complexity,programs`: ok, with no golden or fixture moved;
  - corpusdiff: 2,676 hold.

Approved patch SL-983.p1 (revision 1)

**Air: SL-983.p1, approved.**

**Read:**
- **`tc_same_values_key` mirrors `tc_same_values`:**
  - each kind gets its own tag (`i`, `b`, `t`, `r=`/`r<`), so values of different kinds never share a key;
  - a range keys on `inclusive`, `low` and `high`;
  - text keys on its decoded content, length-prefixed, so a `,` or `)` inside a string can't run into the structure key's delimiters;
  - `Other` and misfits write no key.
- **`structure_key` mirrors the removed `repeats_pattern`:**
  - a hole is `_`, and a binding is a hole;
  - an opaque cell keys on its value, or writes no key when it's a range;
  - a case or tuple writes its ctor, arity and parts.
- **The holder:** keys match exactly when the old relation held, and that relation is an equivalence, so the map's first holder is the arm the pairwise scan named.

**Matrix:** 22 duplicate-arm shapes, typechecked by base `777f9d934` and by the patch. All 22 give byte-identical stderr and exit status.
- **Integer spellings:** `16`/`0x10`, `1000`/`1_000`, `-1`/`-0x1`, `UInt64.max` decimal/hex, and a `UInt8` misfit `300` twice. The misfit is refused as a misfit, not as a duplicate.
- **Other literals:** `true` twice; `"a\tb"`/`"a\u{9}b"` (refused: same decoded text); `"ab"`/`"a"`/`"b"` (accepted).
- **Ranges:** `1..3` twice, `(1..3, _)` twice and `1..3`/`1..=3` are all accepted. No range repeats anything, as before.
- **Structural shapes:** `_` twice, `y` then `_`, `(1, true)`/`(0x1, true)`, `((1, _), 2)`/`((1, y), 2)`, `(1, 2)`/`(2, 1)` (accepted), an enum case twice, `Some(a)`/`Some(_)`, and `Some((1, _))`/`Some((1, b))`.
- **Guards:** a guarded `1` then `1` is accepted. `1`, a guarded `1`, then `1` refuses the third arm at the first's line.
- **Three arms of `5`:** both repeats name line 7.

**Lane** (once, on the Air): ok, 8 shapes, `match_tuple_guarded` r 2.05 under its 2.07 ceiling. The other rows are unchanged from main's.
