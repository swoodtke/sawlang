# Must-fix epic: soundness, liveness and ICE findings surfaced by design 275's landing

RULED by the user, Sep 23 2026, at the close of SL-318 (design 275). This epic is the ONE carrier for the correctness findings that epic's units and reviews produced but did not own. Membership = "a program that is accepted must not corrupt memory, hang, panic on valid code, or crash the compiler". Each member keeps its own issue (brief, evidence, landing note); this epic holds ORDER and STATUS only.

## Members, in the lead's recommended order (the user re-sequences at will)
1. **SL-355** — the park-word race (lost wake, MT hang). HIGH. IN PROGRESS: `agent:claude-air-sawlang` (Opus 5.5), Atomic + CAS latch per the user's ruling; carries **SL-354** (the arm reports a failed kevent). Lands after SL-350 (done) — its rebase onto 8d2c0563 is under way.
2. **SL-345** — a closure's captured reference-param root is not charged against the receiver of the call it is passed to: `v.with_var_ref(0, { e in try! v.push(k); e = 77 })` compiles and the write lands in freed memory. HIGH, soundness. Needs its fix brief (design-199 funnel: closure borrow captures join the call's access set). Unassigned.
3. **SL-340** — `Mutex(value: move v)` at a heap-owning payload frees the payload at construction (use-after-free); K141 is its XFAIL pin. Soundness. Unassigned.
4. **SL-349** — a DRIVEN derived shadow of a module static (`var ROOT = Owner(value: ROOT.value + 1)` after a suspension) panics `Slot.value: slot is empty` on valid code. Unassigned.
5. **SL-356** — ICE: the closure generator forgets the enclosing function's Void-typed locals (`void_variables` reset without save/restore). IN PROGRESS: `agent:claude-sl356` (one patch with **SL-362**, the pin for the generic-struct field retain SL-350 fixed as a side effect).
6. **SL-364** — ICE: an `Int??` parameter of a suspending function. Unassigned.
7. **SL-363** — an oversized CONSTANT fixed-array literal still reaches LLVM's 65535-leaf limit (SL-336's remaining sibling; a compiler abort on valid code). Unassigned.
8. **SL-348** — a SYNC abandoned call's already-moved argument is released by nothing (a leak, the orphan ordering; SL-339's sync twin). Unassigned.
