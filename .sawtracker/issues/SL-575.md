**`std.signal`** (`std/signal.saw`, new):
- `Signal` (7 cases), `Signal.send_to_self`, `SignalWatch.watch -> Result<SignalWatch, IoError>`, and `SignalWatch.next -> Result<Void, IoError>`, plus the `NoCopy` deinit, all over the runtime's `__saw_signal_*` shim.

- All 14 equivalence rows match Stage 0 with no exceptions. SL-366's shim hazard is untouched and not copied.
