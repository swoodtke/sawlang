Found by the SL-1049 spec review (F16). The spec ("`UnsafeMemory<T, Use>`", the `Normal` bullet) says a `Normal` view carries the region accessors `ptr()`, `len() -> Int` (the region's byte length) and `end()`. sawc accepts `ptr()` and `end()` on a `Normal` view of a struct, but refuses `len()` there: `memory.region-length`: "`len()` is the byte length of a region, a view of a fixed array, and `UnsafeMemory<Regs, Normal>` views `Regs`" (compiler/typecheck/src/bodies.saw, the `memory_len` branch). Probes: `.build/scratch/p1000o/p12_um_normal_struct.saw`, `p12_um_end_struct.saw`.

`end()` is `ptr()` plus the region's length, so the region has a byte length whenever `end()` answers. Refusing `len()` alone is inconsistent with that.

**Expected:** `len()` answers the viewed type's size on every `Normal` view, as the spec says. Check the three accessors together on an array view, a struct view and a scalar view.
