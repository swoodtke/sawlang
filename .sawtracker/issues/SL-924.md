## Description

The atomic intrinsics `__saw_atomic_*_i64*` (`__saw_atomic_add_i64`, `__saw_atomic_sub_i64_release`, `__saw_atomic_load_i64_relaxed`, `__saw_atomic_load_i64_seq_cst`, `__saw_atomic_store_i64_seq_cst`, `__saw_atomic_add_i64_seq_cst`, `__saw_atomic_cmpxchg_i64_seq_cst`) operate on a word, not on 64 bits. Typecheck types their operand `Int` (`atomic_intrinsic` in compiler/typecheck/src/bodies.saw), std calls them on `UnsafePointer<Int>` (`Atomic<Int>`, the String refcount, `Arc`, the task group), and on riscv32 `Int` is 32 bits. So the names are a misleading spelling.

SL-865 U6 keeps the names (Q5, lead ruling, Oct 25): renaming them is not in U6, and it likely touches the seed's lang table. U6b converts the lowering itself to the word (census rows in compiler/llvm/src/calls.saw).

Rename them to a word spelling, for example `__saw_atomic_add_word`, across every place they are spelled:
- resolve/src/prelude.saw (the builtin declarations);
- typecheck/src/bodies.saw (`atomic_intrinsic`);
- mono/src/worklist.saw (the builtin list);
- llvm/src/calls.saw (`atomic`, `seq_cst_atomic`);
- std (prelude.saw, taskgroup.saw, task.saw, arc.saw, string.saw);
- and the seed's lang table if it names them.

Census: .build/scratch/p865/u6/census.tsv (the ATOM_NAME rows).
