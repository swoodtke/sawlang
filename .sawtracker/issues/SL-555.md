**A3b stopped before implementation: the walking skeleton can't run without a child drive, and its loop needs A9's budget lowering.** Base: A3a (9aa65bac2) cherry-picked on 6415dbd74; probes run with that tree's Stage 2.

**1. `yield_main` reaches its park only through a child frame (A4's).** §3.1 says the std's park points, `yield_now` among them, "are std-internal lang functions that lower to" `park`. That reading made `main -> yield_now` a park inside `main`. A2's amendment (SL-553 c3) binds the role to `__saw_park_ready` instead, and `yield_now` is a std function whose body calls it. Neither A3b's block nor A4's covers the gap. `sawc2 coro --layouts` on the pin's shape (`while i < 3 { print(i); yield_now(); i = i + 1 }`):
```
frame #0: yield_main.main()  (size 16 align 8)
    suspend 1: bb4 call yield_now      <- a child drive (A4)
    suspend 2: bb5 budget              <- a live budget point (A9)
frame #5: std.task.yield_now()  (size 8 align 8)
    suspend 1: bb0 call __saw_park_ready   <- the only park(ready) A3b owns
```
So the only frame A3b can lower by itself is `yield_now`'s, and nothing can drive it until A4 lands. A user program can suspend only through a std wrapper, a test intrinsic (A4) or a `blocking` extern (offload, A14), so no user program can run on A3b alone.

**2. The pin's loop has a live budget point.** A suspending `main` is one of the three places where budget points stay live (§3.9). A3a counts a yield-capable point as a suspension (suspend 2 above), and its lowering (counter plus `park(ready)`) is A9's, which depends on A3b.

**The options I see** (the choice is the lead's; each is reversible):
- **(a) Inline a park-only wrapper.** A call to an item whose body is exactly one park call lowers as that park in the caller. This is Stage 0's INLINE (its ledger marks `yield_now` `no-frame-owed`, with the site `outcome=inline`). It goes against §3.1's "with none of Stage 0's inline special case", and A4 makes it unnecessary, so it's throwaway work unless kept as an optimisation.
- **(b) Move A4's smallest drive into A3b:** a child with no parameters and a `Void` result. That means a child slot in the layout, state reset to 0, the drive block, `Pending` passed through, and `Ready` continuing. A4 keeps parameter slots, the result move-out and the test intrinsics. Nothing is thrown away, but the work crosses into A3a's `frames.saw`/`layout.saw` ("child-frame slots are A4's") and A4's scope.
- **(c) Rebind the ready role to `yield_now` itself,** restoring §3.1's reading. That edits `resolve/src/lang.saw` and `std/task.saw` (A2's design, front track), and moves A2's `suspend_park_point` chain.
- **(d) Pin the skeleton on the test intrinsic `__saw_suspend()` called directly in `main`,** with A3b taking its `park(ready)` lowering from A4 (amendment SL-553). This is the smallest move, but the skeleton then has no `yield_now` and no corpus pin.

For gap 2, independent of the above: either `yield_main` is straight-line (prints and yields with no loop) until A9, or A3b also takes A9's budget lowering, which needs a frame-resident counter in A3a's layout. **My recommendation: (b), with a straight-line `yield_main`.** It's the only option that leaves nothing to undo and keeps `yield_now` as the pin. A9 then adds the loop form of `yield_main`.

**The brief's other two questions, settled:**
- **The corpus pins.** No `async_main_*` program qualifies: both (`async_main_nested_sleep`, `async_main_sleep_yield`) sleep, so they're A11's (A3c lowers the park). A Stage 0 ledger census over the 251 corpus programs naming `yield_now` finds 6 whose only framed function is `main` and whose only sites are direct `yield_now` calls. 4 of them reach only `coro.not-yet` (`main -> yield_now -> __saw_park_ready`) under sawc2 today, each with one yield and no budget point: `coro_conservative_generic_instance_not_promoted`, `crossmodule_embed_annotation_positions`, `crossmodule_embed_type_identity` and `crossmodule_embed_type_name_head`. They are A3b's corpus pins under (a), (b) or (c), and A4's under (d). The other two fail first on something else: `coro_conservative_free_callee_not_framed` on `Vector.each` and an untyped closure parameter, and `import150_std_qualified_positions` on `std.time`.
- **Which items lose `coro.not-yet`.** One predicate, `coro_frame_lowered(coro, k)` in `compiler/coro/src/lower.saw`, consulted by `coro_refuse_frames` in place of `needs_frame`: an item's frame is lowered when every suspension point in its frame run is a shape A3b lowers. Today that's a call whose target has the `ParkReadyFunction` role, plus the drive shape the ruling admits. A budget point, a park of any other role, or any other suspending call keeps the refusal and its cause chain, so the backend only ever meets lowered frames. The SL-555 c2 copy-hook refusal is separate, keyed on `coro_item_suspends`, and lands first.

I'm holding the unit until you rule. Nothing is committed beyond the cherry-pick.

On the a3b agent's stop (Air): I agree with its recommendation, option (b) plus a straight-line `yield_main`. Some reasons the other options are worse, for the lead's ruling:
- **(c), rebinding the ready role to `yield_now`, doesn't generalize.** `sleep(duration:)` computes before it parks (`__saw_park_timer(timer_nanos(duration))`, with the saturation, soon a `UInt64` per A3c), so it can't be a role-bound park point itself. A std wrapper around a park intrinsic is the general shape A2 chose, `cancelled()`'s non-park included. The drive is needed for every wrapper, so moving it earlier is work A4 would do anyway, never undone.
- **(a), inlining a park-only wrapper, is exactly the special case §3.1 rules out.** It would also need its own invariant to stay correct as wrappers grow bodies (`sleep`'s conversion is already one).

On the predicate: `coro_frame_lowered` as the one gate `coro_refuse_frames` consults is the right shape. It's obligation 1's funnel for 'which frames the backend may see'. Two asks for it:
- it names its entry points;
- an injection pin: a frame with one lowered park and one unlowered suspension (a `blocking` extern call beside the `yield_now`) must keep `coro.not-yet` naming the unlowered one.

Without that pin, a predicate that checks only 'has a ready park' would pass every listed pin and let a half-lowered frame reach the backend.

**A3b, one file-list crossing to rule on (c4 understood; work continues on the in-list parts meanwhile).**

The C entry is to call std's `__saw_exec_run_main(desc, frame)`, but nothing makes that function a mono item. Mono's roots are `main`, the `@export`s and the lang functions in `worklist.saw:add_roots`. An `@export` can't carry a `__saw_*` or `saw_*` symbol (`export.reserved`), and finding the declaration by name is what the lang funnel forbids. So the backend has no body to call.

**Options:**
- **(i) A new lang role `ExecRunMainFunction`** (`std.task`, `__saw_exec_run_main`), rooted like the panic sink. That's one row each in `resolve/src/lang.saw` (the enum, the row, the role list) and `typecheck/src/lang.saw` (its shape, an exhaustive match), plus `mono/src/{worklist,program}.saw` (the root and an `exec_item` field). Two of those files are front-track and two are back-track outside my list. A7b needs exactly this seam to replace the loop, so nothing is thrown away.
- **(ii) The backend emits the minimal loop in the C entry itself,** so `std/task.saw` isn't touched. This stays inside `compiler/llvm/`, but it contradicts the plan's "std's `__saw_exec_run_main`", and A7b would then have to edit `llvm/src/program.saw` to hand the loop to std.

**I recommend (i).**

Independent of the choice: the C entry, the frame descriptor constant and the descriptor-call helper go in `compiler/llvm/src/program.saw`, beside `entry_point`. The list names `{bodies,places,module}`, but the C entry lives in `program.saw`, which SL-594 also edits. Resuming through the descriptor needs a helper, since Saw can't call a raw code pointer. The backend defines `__saw_frame_resume(desc, frame, cx) -> Int32`, and `std/task.saw` declares it in an `extern "C"` block, the way `__saw_rt_get_argc` is defined by the backend and declared by std. A7b's executor calls frames through the same helper.
