**p712forward: forwarding lands in the branch; one cell needs a ruling.** (Commits on `worktree-agent-a04a1627afe7b2089`, not submitted.)

**The rule, as built:** a method of a `Box`'s or an `Arc`'s *struct* payload that the pointer doesn't declare is called through the pointer as `p.value().m()` (Box) or through the new private shared accessor `Arc.payload` (Arc). Only a `&self` method forwards. A method taking `&var self`, or a consuming one, is refused (`forward.exclusive-receiver`), as the spec's Box and Arc forwarding text and Stage 0 both say. A `borrows` payload method forwards too, `Arc<Mutex<T>>`'s `lock()` included. One lookup (`forward_accessor`) decides what forwards, and one funnel (`call_receiver`, with its entry points named) gives every typecheck and MIR site the receiver node.

**Needs a ruling: does a `String` payload forward?** `Box<String>.len()`: the spec's text says "a `&self` method on the payload struct", and it presents `String` as a builtin type, not as a struct. Stage 0 forwards nothing to it ("type `Box` has no method `len`"). The new std declares `String` a struct. I stopped that cell on Stage 0's side: it's refused as `member.unknown` (fixture `member.unknown.forward-string`). If the answer is "forward it", the change is one condition in `forward_accessor` plus the fixture.

**Reversible decisions:**
- A `@synthesize(shared)` accessor declared `&var self` doesn't forward, even for a read: the declared receiver decides, as the spec's "a `&var self` payload method is rejected" reads.
- Enum, type-parameter and nested (`Box<Box<P>>`) payloads don't forward, as Stage 0 has it.
- `Box.value` gets the `@synthesize(shared)` twin the spec already declares, which is SL-502's first item.
