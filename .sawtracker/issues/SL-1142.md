## Description

**The Vim/Neovim syntax plugin (`editor/nvim/`) is out of date.** Asked by the project owner (Nov 15): it was last changed Aug 17, before the self-hosted compiler and most of the language's later changes. The lead updates it from GRAMMAR.md §2 (the lexical layer: keywords, contextual words, literals, escapes, comments, directives) and the resolver's prelude (`compiler/resolve/src/prelude.saw`).

What is wrong today, by reading the files against GRAMMAR.md:
- Keywords: `borrow`, `borrows`, `lend`, `unsafe` (a real keyword, highlighted as an error today) are missing as keywords; `defer`, `do`, `generic` are highlighted as errors though they are ordinary identifiers (the planned reservations are not enforced); `import`, `module`, `package`, `parent`, `sync`, `escaping`, `any` are highlighted everywhere though each is a contextual word, an identifier elsewhere.
- Contextual words with no highlighting: the effect slot (`consumes`, `sendable`, `constexpr`, `blocking`), `const` in a generic list, `private` before a field, `lends`, `copy` as a capture mode, `static_assert`.
- Literals: braces in a string are escaped `\{` and `\}` (the plugin highlights `{{` and `}}`); `\0` is an escape; a float has no exponent; `$0` closure parameters; the directives `#file`, `#line`, `#function`.
- Comments: `///` and `//!` doc comments are not told from plain ones.
- Names: `ImplicitCopy` (retired), `TaskHandle`, `Global`, `Resumable` listed; the prelude's `Thread`, `Byte`, `NoMove`, `ZeroInit`, `UnsafeSend`/`UnsafeSync`, `Serialize`/`Deserialize`/`Encoder`/`Decoder`, the range types, `AllocError`, `Some`/`Ok`/`Err` missing.
- README: install paths name an old checkout directory.

**Lead's note with p1 (Nov 15): the Vim/Neovim plugin follows GRAMMAR.md's lexical layer.** Written by the lead at the owner's request; three files under `editor/nvim/`, nothing else. The gate runs only `compiler/tests/run.py` on it (`./build.sh test --dry-run` on the diff: every other part skips).

**What changed** (`syntax/saw.vim`, against GRAMMAR.md section 2 and the resolver's prelude, `compiler/resolve/src/prelude.saw`):
- **Keywords:** the list of section 2.2 (`borrow`, `borrows`, `lend` and `unsafe` added; `unsafe` was highlighted as an error). `defer`, `do` and `generic` are no longer errors: the planned reservations are not enforced, so they are identifiers.
- **Contextual words, each only in its position:** `import`/`module`/`export` at the head of a line; `package`/`parent` inside `public(...)`; `private` before a field; `type` and `any` before a name; `const` after `<` or `,`; the effect slot after `)` (`consumes`, `constexpr`, `blocking`, `sync`, `sendable`, `escaping`, with `unsafe` among them), and `blocking func`; `lends` before a name; a capture list's `copy`. So `let type = 7` and `let defer = 1` are plain identifiers.
- **Literals:** `\{` and `\}` are the brace escapes (the plugin highlighted `{{` and `}}`), `\0` is an escape, a float has no exponent, `$0` closure parameters, the `#file`, `#line` and `#function` directives, numbers whose `_` separates two digits.
- **Comments:** `///` and `//!` lines are doc comments (`SpecialComment`); `////` and a trailing `///` are plain.
- **Names:** the prelude's types, traits and cases (`Thread`, `Byte`, `NoMove`, `ZeroInit`, `UnsafeSend`/`UnsafeSync`, `Serialize`/`Deserialize`/`Encoder`/`Decoder`, the six range types, `AllocError`, `Some`/`Ok`/`Err`); `ImplicitCopy`, `TaskHandle`, `Global` and `Resumable` removed. The builtins are `print`, `panic`, `assert`, `sleep`, `cancelled`, and `static_assert`/`sizeof`/`alignof` in their positions (the bare `spawn` and `yield_now` removed).

`ftplugin/saw.vim`: `comments` continues `///` and `//!` as themselves. `README.md`: what is highlighted, and install paths with no particular checkout's directory.

**Checked by the lead:** a sample file with every construct above, through headless Neovim 0.12 (a script that prints each word's syntax group) and plain Vim 9.1 (filetype, three groups, `comments`, no error messages); and `std/taskgroup.saw` (4,491 lines) highlighted in 0.19 seconds, its keyword words being only the real ones. Evidence: `.build/scratch/pvim/` in the lead's checkout (`sample.saw`, `groups.txt`, `vim_check.txt`, `groups_taskgroup.txt`).

**Not done:** an indent file (the plugin keeps `smartindent`), and a Tree-sitter grammar.
