The authorities:
- The spec's Argument Evaluation Order: arguments are evaluated left to right, and a by-value argument is moved "at evaluation time". So `move r` happens before `try fail()`, and the moved value is then a temporary of the statement.

**The mechanism:** two paths made an owning operand's temporary without registering it with any scope. One was the operand funnel's hand-off (`operand_at`, reached by every funnel entry: arguments, receivers, constructions, elements, conversions, and `let`/assignment/`return` values). The other was `snapshot`, reached by `call_arguments`, `builtin_operands_from`, `receiver_operand`, `element_operands` and `arithmetic`.

**The fix, one funnel in two halves:**
- `hold_operand` (entries `operand_at`, `snapshot`) registers the temporary with the innermost scope as a hand-off entry.
- `relinquish_operands` (entries `assign`, `emit_call`) takes it off its scope when the consuming instruction is emitted.
- `own_temp` adopts a held temporary, so a construct that keeps it drops it at the end of its statement.
- `finish_function` clears the scope stack before drop elaboration.
