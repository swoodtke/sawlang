**Found by:** u7skel and u7b (U7a, SL-505, merged as 7e04eaa0). **Area:** MIR's Force projection and the LLVM backend's panic path.

**The defect:** a failing `try!` prints `try! failed` with nothing after it. Stage 0 appends `: <error>` when the error is `Printable`, and the spec's panic text implies it. MIR's Force projection carries no rendering of the error, so the backend has nothing to print.

**Fix:** the Force projection on a `Result` with a `Printable` error carries the rendering call, and the backend formats it into the message through the same 508-byte cut. Pin a run-lane program with a `Printable` error, and one with a non-Printable error (bare text).

**Registered from SL-781 (agent p781's matrix):** sawc2's `try!` never renders the error (`panic … try! failed`, even for a plain error type). When this lands, `try!`'s render goes through `Printable.format` like every render site, and SL-781's `coro.format-suspends` covers it automatically (it refuses suspending `format` implementations, not positions). Pin: `try!` on an `Err` whose `format` suspends is refused once `try!` renders. (SL-729 looks like a duplicate of this.)
