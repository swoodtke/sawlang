**D1. `@section` and `@align` are refused, never silently dropped** (SL-606 bucket D, first, per the Air in m520: kernels rely on them). Finding: LLVM 3.

- **The bug:** `places.saw` writes every static as `internal global T V`, with no `section` or `align`. `bodies.saw` writes no `section` on a define, and nothing past the parser reads `@section`. A kernel or embedded image that relies on placement builds clean and is wrong. That breaks the doctrine "never hide errors".
- **Fix, interim:** refuse `@section` and `@align` on statics and functions as `backend.not-yet`, and list them in the backend README's refusal table. Emitting them is a follow-up unit: the LLVM `section "…"` and `align N` attributes, through the target where a fact is target-dependent.
- **Pins:** the review's `review_llvm_attrs` probe as a refusal fixture, for each attribute on a static and on a function; and a census that `std/` and `compiler/` use neither today. If they do, STOP and report.

**d1attr: done in worktree branch `d1attr`, commit 8231d69a6 (not submitted).**
- **Reproduced** on Stage 2 at 16e7f834a. The `@section` on the static and on the function, and the static's `@align(64)`, were all dropped. A sibling the issue didn't name is the same mechanism: a local's `@align(64)` came out as `alloca i64, align 8`.
- **Census:** no `@section` or `@align` in `std/`, `sawc/rt/` or any `compiler/` source sawc2 builds. They appear only in parse, grammar, resolve, typecheck, MIR and eval fixtures, and none of those reach the backend.
- **Fix:** `ll_refuse_placement` in `compiler/llvm/src/program.saw`. It visits every attribute node and refuses each `@section` and `@align` as `backend.not-yet` at the attribute, before emission. It sits in the backend, not typecheck, so the typecheck, MIR and eval goldens that use `@align` are untouched.
- **Pins:** the run-lane due programs `section_static`, `section_function`, `align_static` and `align_local`. The refusal row is in `compiler/tests/run/README.md`, since `compiler/llvm/` has no README.
- **Gate:** `run.py --only subset,programs` and `battery.sh citations`, both green.
