## Description

**A Mach-O link with line tables spends about 58 ms looking up its tools, outside `--time-stages`.** Found by SL-955 unit 4's census (`.build/scratch/p955f/census.md`).

**What happens:** each `-g` link on macOS runs three tool lookups through `/bin/sh`: `clang -print-prog-name=ld`, `ld -v` and `clang -print-prog-name=dsymutil`. Together they take about 58 ms, 18% of `hello` -O0's build. `--time-stages` shows none of it.

**Proposed fix:** `build.py` records the three in the runtime manifest beside the binary, as it already records `objcopy` (`choose_objcopy` / `target_record_lines`), and the driver reads them from there. `--time-stages` then times whatever lookup remains.

**Pins:** the record names the tools; a `-g` link runs no lookup (a logging wrapper); a record without them is a clear error, or a fallback that is timed.
