**A4. Driving a child frame, and the test intrinsics.**
- **Scope:**
  - Embedded child slots: parameter slots written at the call, the drive block, `Pending` returned as is, and `Ready` with the result moved out (§3.1).
  - `__saw_drive`, `__saw_drive_steps` and `__saw_suspend` as test lang intrinsics, with `steps` counting `Pending`s. Resolve stops refusing the three. A drive point is a root over a stack-allocated frame, and its rule depends on the reason a `Pending` reports (t8):
    - **on a ready yield,** it resumes at once;
    - **on any other reason** (timer, fd, channel, offload, or a group it runs inside), it hands the wait to the executor's sweep and idle wait. That shares the ambient scheduler, as Stage 0's drive points do: "one sweep … shared by every drive point" (`sawc/std/taskgroup.saw`).

    Until A7b provides that sweep, a non-ready `Pending` at a drive point panics, naming the reason, rather than spin. A busy re-resume would mask a lost wake.
  - `coro.drive-in-suspending` for a drive site in a body that suspends.
- **Depends on:** A3b.
- **Files:** `compiler/coro/src/lower.saw`, `compiler/llvm/src/calls.saw`; cross-track, `compiler/resolve/src/lookup.saw`.
- **Pins:**
  - `coro_nested_suspend_two_deep`, `coro_drive_void_body`, `expr_suspend_return`;
  - refusals `drive_site_in_suspending_body` and `drive_site_in_suspending_function`.

  The drive-point programs that sleep or run a group move to the unit that makes them pass: `taskgroup_suspending_parent_sleep` to A11; `taskgroup_nested_groups`, `taskgroup_in_suspending_fn`, `taskgroup_suspending_cancel` and `taskgroup_suspending_deinit_join` to A8a.
- **Done when:** the pins pass. Census: the intrinsic-driven programs whose parks are all ready yields or `__saw_suspend`s start passing. The census lane reports the exact count, rather than this plan guessing it.
- **Estimate:** 1.5.

**Plan:** SL:coroutines §4, unit A4 (approved by the user, Oct 12). The plan text is authoritative; this issue is its tracker entry. Dependencies are named by unit id; see the epic's child list for their issues.

**A4 stop (a4drive): the drivers need a front half the plan doesn't name.**

A4's files are `coro/src/lower.saw`, `llvm/src/calls.saw` and `resolve/src/lookup.saw`. But `__saw_drive(f(args))` and `__saw_drive_steps(f(args))` can't reach coro without changes in four other stages. Every A4 run pin calls `__saw_drive` (`coro_nested_suspend_two_deep`, `coro_drive_void_body`, `expr_suspend_return`), and so do both refusal pins. So no A4 pin can pass inside A4's files.

What is missing, stage by stage (checked at 9734b1183):
1. **Resolve.** `lookup.saw` can stop refusing the names, but nothing declares them. `prelude.saw` declares only `__saw_test_suspend` and `__saw_suspend` as builtins (`res_test_intrinsics`). The two drivers need declarations there, which A4 doesn't own.
2. **Typecheck.** `bodies.saw:builtin_call` refuses any other builtin as `slice.not-yet` ("outside the sync-only bootstrap slice"). The drivers need typing: the argument must be a direct call, `__saw_drive` gives the root's result type and `__saw_drive_steps` gives `Int`. The root call must also stay out of the enclosing body's effects. Stage 0 does this in `effects.py` (the drive-site map). Without it, the root `f()` counts as a suspending call of `main`, so `main` becomes framed, and `coro.drive-in-suspending` can't be told apart from a plain sync drive. The summaries are `summaries.saw`, which b1caps holds. The borrow check's `suspends_at` reads the MIR flag the same way.
3. **MIR.** A drive site isn't a call of its root. Like A7d's `MirCallKind.Spawn`, it needs its own call kind (target, the root's arguments evaluated at the site, the result as `dest`), plus its build/lower, dump and verifier rules (`mir/src/{model,lower,dump,verify}.saw`).
4. **Mono.** The worklist has to instantiate the driven root, as it does for `Spawn` (`worklist.saw` arm `case Spawn`). b1caps holds that file.

Only then do coro (the driver root over a stack frame, the `Pending`-reason rule, `coro.drive-in-suspending`) and the backend's driver loop (`calls.saw`) apply.

**What does fit A4's files, and what A7a needs:** parameter slots written at the call, and `Ready` moving the result out of the child's result slot. They extend A3b's drive with no front-half change. They can be pinned without the drivers, through a suspending `main` on A3b's entry executor (for example `let y = mid(3)` with `mid(b: Int) -> Int` yielding). `__saw_suspend`'s `park(ready)` lowering and `__saw_test_suspend` as an effect-only no-op also fit.

**Proposal (the lead decides):**
- A4 lands the parameter slots, the result move-out and the two `__saw_*suspend` lowerings, with run-lane pins through a suspending `main`.
- A new unit (front half plus mono, after b1caps frees `summaries.saw` and `worklist.saw`) takes the driver form, modelled on A7c/A7d's spawn form. A4's driver lowering, `coro.drive-in-suspending` and the five pins move to it.
