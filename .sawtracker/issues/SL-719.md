**SL-719.p1: summary for review.**

- **`std/numeric.saw`, Int half:** `abs`, `min`, `max`, `clamp`, `pow`, `is_even`, `is_odd` and `signum`, ported verbatim from Stage 0 with `///` docs, to the spec's text (LANGUAGE_SPEC "extension Int"). `abs(Int.min)`, a negative exponent and an overflowing `pow` panic. The Float half waits on SL-722.
- **`std/slab.saw`:** `SlabHead` (NoCopy, bare fields after the `redundant-visibility` lint), `slab_alloc` and `slab_dealloc`. A `static SlabHead` refuses in the backend (`backend.not-yet`, the non-integer static), so the pin uses a local head over a `GlobalAllocator` region.
- **One compiler line pair, a lead ruling:** `compiler/resolve/src/loader.saw` `load_new_std_modules()` gains `numeric` and `slab`. The new std's modules are a hardcoded list, and `import std.X` finds only a module on it. D12's `std_extends_builtin` (typecheck conform.saw) then makes `extension Int` visible with no import, as Stage 0's `(5).abs()` needs. No seed bump, and no golden or dump file changed.
- **Pins:** `int_methods`, `int_abs_min_panics`, `int_pow_negative_panics`, `int_pow_overflow_panics` and `slab_local` match Stage 0 at -O0 and -O2. The three panic pins carry `// differs:` because std raises the panic.
- **Corpusdiff (SL-666):** three rows move to pass (`int_methods`, `int_abs_min_panics`, `int_pow_negative_panics`). Three move to later refusals: `any_box_slab` (`Box<any Trait>.make`), `kernel_slab_idiom` (Box forwarding, SL-712) and `slab_basic` (the non-integer static). Nothing moves to `wrong`.
- **Gate (Sonnet, under the lock):** `run.py --only resolver,typechecker,mir,std,programs,coro` ok, `corpusdiff gate` ok (all 2661), citations green. `git merge-tree` shows it merging cleanly with SL-564's and SL-718's branches.
- **Noted, not changed here (spec semantics, filed separately):** `clamp` with `low > high` returns a value silently, and `pow` is linear in `exp`, so `1.pow(Int.max)` does not finish. Both follow the spec and Stage 0 as written.
