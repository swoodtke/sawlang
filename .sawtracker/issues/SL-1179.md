**A deadlock among pool frames that wait in place on different workers hangs unreported.**

**The probe:** SL-1161.p1 r4's `.build/scratch/p1161r4/probes/walk_active_t2.saw`. It hangs 3/3 both before SL-1161 and after it.

**The mechanism** (from the r4 agent): each frame is ACTIVE, waiting in place in a join on its own worker. Each worker's deadlock walk reads the other worker's active slot as running, so no walk ever concludes. K205 names this case as out of scope today: "a pool join made by a pool's task (the joining frame reads as running on its worker)".

**Why it matters:** never-hide-errors. A silent hang is the worst failure shape for a deadlock.

**Directions** (unruled): workers publish "waiting in place on word W" beside their active slot, so a walk can treat an active-but-waiting frame as blocked and follow the wait graph. Or a pool task's join parks rather than waiting in place, which is design 1161 §6's open alternative, now plausible since a worker runs its own groups between turns.

It follows SL-1161 (the pool's progress and walk changes land there first).
