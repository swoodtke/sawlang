## Description

Found by U4c's differential (SL-424.p3).
1. **Possible recognizer divergence.** The recognizer drops line breaks inside a generic list, and splits `>=`/`>>=` at a list close, only when every token of the list is in its `_GENERIC_INNER` set. GRAMMAR.md states no such limit, and the parser does not model it. So `let v: Vector<[Int; a == b]>= w` would split in the parser but not in the recognizer. No corpus case reaches it. Decide from GRAMMAR.md's text: remove the approximation from the recognizer, or state the limit in §13, and pin it.
2. **Corpus gaps.** The parse corpus has no case for these; add golden or negative cases through cases.py:
   - `&[T; N]` (a reference to an array, which U4c first parsed as a slice);
   - `x as &Int ?? z` and `x as (A) -> B ?? z` (`cast-target-question` beyond named targets);
   - `a<b> - 1` (named `generic-or-less`).
