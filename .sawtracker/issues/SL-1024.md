Found by the SL-1021 spec review (F8).

LANGUAGE_SPEC "Exclusivity, invalidation, and the fences" says a trait may require a `borrows` method, and "a call through a bound or an `any Trait` follows the requirement's declaration".

With a trait requirement `func slot(&var self, i: Int) borrows -> &var Int`:
- `poke<T: Keyed>(t: &var T) { t.slot(0) = 9 }` gives `mono.not-yet`: a borrow of an accessor whose body is not lowered;
- `poke2(t: &var any Keyed)` gives the same.
