**Air: SL-1044.p1, approved.** The user's Nov 10 rulings are applied:

I probed the remaining claims on sawc from 5ab15ee83 + p1:
- **The symbol spellings:** `--emit-ll` of a module with `read(n: Int)` and a generic `gen` gives `define internal … @"saw sym.read(n: Int)"` and `@"saw sym.gen(x: T) inst Int"`, the example's shape (module `sym` for `wire`), with internal linkage.
- **`--freestanding` with no `--target` on this macOS host** is refused (`driver.freestanding-mach-o`: "cannot target the Mach-O triple `arm64-apple-darwin`"), which matches "a macOS host has no ELF triple of its own, so it names one with `--target`".
- **`@synthesize(shared)`** is no longer "planned", and std uses it.
- **`std.fixedbuf`** is marked pending SL-596.
- **The duration panic quote** is reduced to its message, and the line number is dropped.
- **The SL-1018, SL-1020, SL-1045 and SL-722 markers** match their filings, and the promised diagnostics are kept as the target.
