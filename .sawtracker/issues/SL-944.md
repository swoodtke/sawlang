**`std.numeric`'s Float math calls libm, and nothing refuses it under `--freestanding`.** `abs`, `floor`, `ceil`, `round`, `sqrt`, `min` and `max` are prelude-visible methods declared `extern "C"` over `fabs`, `floor`, `ceil`, `round`, `sqrt`, `fmin` and `fmax`. A freestanding object that calls one references a libm name that no environment is told to supply. Found by SL-929's census (`.build/scratch/p929/census.md`, the `float_census_numeric.saw` probe): on both arches, at -O0 and -O1.

**USER RULING (Oct 30): std implements them in Saw.** The freestanding contract stays at the seams, the atomics and the soft-float routines, with no libm.
- The Saw bodies are exact: floor, ceil, round, abs, min and max by bit manipulation, and sqrt correctly rounded.
- Where the target has an FPU, the hosted build keeps the hardware instructions, through LLVM's intrinsics.
- Where it has none, a soft-float target references no libm name.

The brief owes a census of how std can choose between the two per target: a compiler builtin lowered per the target's features, or another route. It also owes the NaN, signed-zero and ties rules the spec states for each method, which the Saw bodies must meet bit for bit; `fmin`/`fmax` versus LLVM's `minnum` is the sharp edge there.
