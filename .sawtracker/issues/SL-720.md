**SL-720 batch 1 (aacaef7f0): lead review. One revision before submission.** Path's members and `Env.set_cwd` are right, and their pins match Stage 0. Three changes:

1. **`Env.cwd` misreports its error.** It calls bare `getcwd` in a doubling loop, and std sees no errno after a bare libc call, so every failure is retried. The revision is queued as an Opus unit (brief `p720rev`). SL-737's sweep covers `set_cwd`/`chdir` as process-global state.

Approved patch SL-720.p1 (revision 1)

**Air: SL-720.p1 r1 approved; arming it.** Path's members and the Env cwd pair are right, and the seam is correct in both runtime trees.

**Read:**
- `rt_fs_getcwd` returns `getcwd(NULL, 0)`'s buffer, or NULL with the positive tag through `status_out`, as `opendir` does. `Env.cwd` copies with `string_from_bytes`, and frees the buffer on the success path and on the UTF-8 panic path.
- `set_cwd` refuses a NUL byte before C, as SL-718's ops do, and maps the negated tag.
- The non-UTF-8 panic follows the file's existing convention (`get`, `argument`, `executable_path`).
- The ABI entry and the history row are in both ABI.md copies, plus `runtime_abi.py` and `runtimeabi.saw`.

**Probes:**
- `air-review/sl720/edges_new.saw`, with its Stage 0 twin `edges_s0.saw` (`ext`/`as_str`). Sixteen edge cases, printed identically by Stage 0 and the patch: `a.`, `.bashrc`, `a.tar.gz`, `a/b/`, `/`, `//`, `/x`, `x`, the empty path, `a/.b.c` and `..`, plus `join` with a trailing `/`, an absolute `other`, each side empty, and `a//`. So the port is faithful.
