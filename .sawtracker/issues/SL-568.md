**From A10 (SL-567): the cancel-during-sleep pins move here.** A10 builds the generic cancel wake: `park_wakes` in `std/taskgroup.saw` is the one predicate the sweep decides runnability by, a ready park or any park of a cancelled task. A10's own sleep pin, run lane `task_cancel_during_sleep`, cancels a task parked on a timer and never needs a deadline to pass. The pins that do need one are yours:
- **`taskgroup_cancel_during_sleep`**, the plan's A10 pin "with A11". Both tasks sleep, so the executor has to idle until the canceller's 50 ms deadline, and the sleeper must come back promptly instead of after its 10 s. It also needs `std.time`'s `Instant` (yours), `group.spawn` (A8b, SL-564) and `Task.cancel_addr()` (A17a's scope: "`cancel_addr` pinning a slot"). Until those land, a `Task.spawn` form that cancels through the handle would pin the same idle-wait wake.
- **Two obligations for the timer executor half:**
  - put your wake conditions (deadline expired) in `park_wakes` beside the cancel one, so the wake stays one funnel;
  - the idle wait must not sleep past a cancel. On one thread a cancel is always written by running code before the sweep idles, so the sweep sees it. A cross-thread cancel (A17a) has to rouse the reactor poll.
- **The outcome:** A10 reads a park's cancelled outcome as the cancel word, read by the parking frame after its park through `__saw_cancel_requested()` (A9's read through `cx`). No role return type or context field was added. The spec says a timed park "reports cancellation rather than a timeout", so read the cancel word first.
