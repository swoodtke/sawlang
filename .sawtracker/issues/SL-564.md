Stage 0 has the same class. Before A8b no safe code could reach it, since the background group never dies, so it is the SL-662 pattern again: A8b makes it reachable. The plan gives spawn *borrows* an extent the handle carries (A8c) but not the handle's own reference to its group. Options: A8b lands after a fix (A8c's extent machinery carrying a shared loan of the group looks like the natural home), or A8b lands with SL-683 open as a recorded hole.
