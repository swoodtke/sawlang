**A7d. The spawn site in MIR, and the must-consume obligation by provenance** (back track; added during implementation, SL-561 c2/c3).
- **Why it exists:** A7c's stop (SL-561 c2) found two gaps in the plan.
  - **No unit lowers a spawn site into MIR.** Drop elaboration and A7a's task roots both need one. Lowering the form as a builtin would evaluate `f(x)` synchronously first.
  - **The must-consume obligation can't be a role on `Task<T>`/`VoidTask`.** `group.spawn` mints the same type and its handle may be dropped (spec, "Two engines coexist" and "THE HANDLE IS MUST-CONSUME"; `conformance/K80_group_spawn_handle_may_be_dropped`; `compiler/tests/drops/README.md`). The obligation follows the FORM.
- **Scope:**
  - **A MIR spawn-site terminator:** the spawn form (`Task.spawn`, the singleton; the group form arrives with A8b), the target function and its argument operands (NOT a call of the target: the arguments are evaluated, the body isn't run), and the handle's destination. Plus its dump spelling and verifier rule, and its lowering from A7c's typecheck spawn role.
  - **coro refuses a spawn site** until A7a lowers it, with the rule naming A7a.
  - **The obligation:** a singleton-form spawn site MINTS an obligation on its destination. Drop elaboration follows it through moves and refuses `drop.must-consume` on any path where the handle reaches an implicit drop without being consumed by `join`, `detach` or `cancel`. Stage 0's `types.py:_mint_spawn_obligation` and its per-path check are the reference semantics; match them, and list any divergence. The group form mints nothing.
  - **One funnel** names where obligations are minted (obligation 1). Its entry points are the spawn-site lowering now, and the `Thread.spawn` form when A18 lands it.
- **Depends on:** A7c.
- **Files:** `compiler/mir/src/{model,lower,dump,verify}.saw`, `compiler/drops/src/elaborate.saw`, `compiler/coro/src/` (the refusal only), `compiler/tests/{mir,drops,coro}/`.
- **Pins:**
  - `refuse/drop.must-consume.saw` rewritten to `Task.spawn`, which it currently spells with `Thread.spawn`;
  - a handle consumed on one branch and dropped on the other (refused), and one moved to another local and then joined (accepted);
  - a MIR golden of the spawn site;
  - K80's negative pin when A8b adds the group form (A8b's).
- **Done when:** the pins pass, and `Task.spawn` reaches the coro refusal naming A7a.
- **Estimate:** 1.5.

**Plan:** SL:coroutines §4, amended during implementation by the lead (reversible; SL-561 c3). A7a now depends on A7d.
