**Found by:** the Air, reviewing SL-505.p1. Repro `f2_shleq.saw` is in chat file f22 (m454). **Area:** typecheck, compound assignment for shifts.

**The defect:** `u <<= k`, with `u, k: UInt8`, is refused: "expected `Int`, the type of the compound assignment's target". The target is `UInt8`. The spec exempts the shift count from the operand-type match and says "the compound forms `<<=` and `>>=` follow". Stage 0 accepts it.

**Fix:** a compound shift checks its count as the binary shift does. Sweep `<<=` and `>>=` across the integer widths, with the count at a width different from the target's.
