## Description

**Unit 6 of SL-661: delete `sawc/`.**

**Delete:**
- `sawc/` and its std (`sawc/std/`, `sawc/builtin.saw`);
- `test_runner.py`;
- the Stage-0-only tools and lanes unit 5 retired;
- the `make` targets and `bin/` shims that run it.

**The consumer sweep (obligation 2)** covers every tracked file naming `sawc/`, `sawc.py`, `test_runner` or `.venv/bin/python sawc`, including Makefile, build.sh, tools/, compiler/, devtools/, designs/ (pointers only, history stays), the skills and the docs. Each reference is removed, repointed to sawc2, or recorded as a v0 build recipe.

**Freeze, with a recipe:**
- **Blade (`blade/`), `libs/` and the freestanding suite (`tests/freestanding/`)** stay in Stage 0's language until they're ported. Each gets a short README note: frozen until its port to sawc2; to build it, use the v0 compiler through the pinned toolchain. Give the command; unit 7 names the variable. Nothing in sawlang gates them.
- **`examples/`:** the corpus the full suite ran. Either it is frozen the same way, or its programs move into the corpusdiff corpus. Say which.

**The resolver** (`tools/toolchain.py`) recognizes a sawlang checkout by `sawc/sawc.py`. It must recognize one by sawc2 instead, keeping its public names (`SAWLANG_ROOT`, `SAWC`, `sawc` on `PATH`, `sawlang.pin`) and their meaning. Its tests and the `toolchain` lane follow.

**Docs:** CLAUDE.md, README.md, TESTING.md, the saw-lang skill and `compiler/README.md` describe a single compiler. LANGUAGE_SPEC.md's references to Stage 0's behaviour are checked: the spec describes the language, not an implementation.

**Depends on:** SL-539 (the tag exists), and units 2–5 landed.

**Done when:** `git ls-files sawc` is empty, the per-patch gate is green on the deleting patch, and a consumer with `SAWLANG_ROOT` at a v0.13.0 checkout still builds.
