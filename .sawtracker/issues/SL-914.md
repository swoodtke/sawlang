`UnsafeMemory` is missing from sawc's std (`std/` has no definition), but the language spec and the freestanding toolkit name it, and v0.18.0's std had it. Found by SL-865's step-1 plan (p865, `.build/scratch/p865/plan.md`).

**Who it blocks:** 9 coroutine-corpus rows, 11 sawos files, and the freestanding suite's `struct_layout` case. That puts it on the sawos restart's path (SL-865 U9 and the sawos batch update).

**Related:** SL-379 (needs a ruling) asks whether `UnsafeMemory.read()` should follow the payload's copy tier or restrict views to trivial `T`. The port should take that ruling rather than copy v0.18.0's unretained bitwise alias, so SL-379 is decided first or at the port's dispatch.

**Scope:** port the type into the new std with the spec's surface (unsafe-typed, per design 130/136), plus its pins. Size and consumers to be measured at dispatch; SL-913's sweep counts the sawos sites.
