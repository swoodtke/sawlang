`UnsafeMemory` is missing from sawc's std (`std/` has no definition), but the language spec and the freestanding toolkit name it, and v0.18.0's std had it. Found by SL-865's step-1 plan (p865, `.build/scratch/p865/plan.md`).

**Who it blocks:** 9 coroutine-corpus rows, 11 sawos files, and the freestanding suite's `struct_layout` case. That puts it on the sawos restart's path (SL-865 U9 and the sawos batch update).

**Related:** SL-379 (needs a ruling) asks whether `UnsafeMemory.read()` should follow the payload's copy tier or restrict views to trivial `T`. The port should take that ruling rather than copy v0.18.0's unretained bitwise alias, so SL-379 is decided first or at the port's dispatch.

**Scope:** port the type into the new std with the spec's surface (unsafe-typed, per design 130/136), plus its pins. Size and consumers to be measured at dispatch; SL-913's sweep counts the sawos sites.


<!-- sawtracker:comment {"author":"agent:claude-sawlang","body_bytes":405,"created":"1793300080","id":"c2"} -->
SL-379 RULED (the user, Oct 29): `UnsafeMemory<T>` takes trivial `T` only. This port implements that rule (a refusal at the type for any owning or refcounted `T`), with SL-913's 34 sawos files and 343 sites (including 115 field accesses through `self.regs.fr.read()`) as its consumers. The spec makes `UnsafeMemory` compiler-known with field access, so this is compiler work, not only std. Ready to brief.

**Survey (p914):** plan at `.build/scratch/p914/plan.md` (triviality matrix, probes). sawc's std declares nothing of the family; resolve's prelude already lists the five names. The ruling's "trivial" is the existing `is_trivial` (tier.saw: no deinit, no ownership); the copy tier would be WRONG (`Arc`/`String` are Copy-tier but own memory). 15 corpus rows are `name.undefined` today. Units: **914a** (conformance U37–U50 first; std types plus roles; the formation check `memory.trivial`/`memory.intent`; construction including as a static constant; `read`/`write`/`ptr`/`len`/`end` with volatile on Device; a custom -O2 volatile probe; `struct_layout` leaves `due`) and **914b** (field and index projection: sawos's 115 field accesses).

**RULED (the user, Oct 29): NO ENUM in a view.** No enum, backed or not, may be a view's `T` or a viewed struct's field (transitively). A hardware field is read as its raw integer, then converted with `E.from(raw:)` (None for an undeclared value). That keeps SL-540's T29 ("a raw-backed enum value is always a declared case") sound by construction. It's part of the formation check, with a fixture each for a backed and an unbacked enum, direct and nested.

**Lead decisions on the other questions:**
1. **Generic views:** checked at INSTANTIATION in mono (the `atomic.width` precedent); no nameable `Trivial` bound now.
2. **Wording:** sawc's own style, in the spec's terms; the corpus error rows point at the new typecheck fixtures (the judge passes them on the rule, and they're weak tests otherwise).
3. **"Scalar" on Device:** the integers, `Bool` and raw pointers (enums now refused outright); aliases resolve to what they name.
4. **Projection visibility:** refused for another module's private fields, as ordinary member access is.
5. **Markers:** `ReadOnly`/`WriteOnly` gate on BOTH `Use` kinds (a marker's meaning doesn't depend on `Use`); the spec gets a sentence.
6. **Alignment:** the author's obligation for a run-time address (documented), and a misaligned CONSTANT address is refused.
7. Covered by the ruling.
8. **Cell holders:** follow `is_trivial` (a cell over a trivial payload is trivial).
9. **A declared copy policy** counts as non-trivial (conservative), and the refusal text says why.
10. **Address type:** `Int`/`UInt` only.
