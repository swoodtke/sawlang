**Port gmgate to sawc2: run the ownership and concurrency oracles, built by sawc2, under Guard Malloc.** The user ruled "port it" (Oct 15, on SL-666 c3). It must land before SL-667 deletes `sawc/`.

**Why:** `tools/gmgate.py` (design 159 unit 4, design 192 unit 4) is the only lane that turns a missing retain or a use-after-free into a crash. Under Guard Malloc (`/usr/lib/libgmalloc.dylib`), every allocation gets its own page and is unmapped on free, so a surplus release faults at the instruction instead of reading plausible freed memory and exiting 0. It drives Stage 0 today, so it never sees sawc2's output, and sawc2's drops, retains and coroutine frames are exactly the surface it exists for.

**The change:**
- The two lanes keep their meaning: `ownership` (copies, retains, drops, refcounts, containers; 10 runs) and `concurrency` (values in heap-resident coroutine frames or crossing tasks: frame handoff, captures, group teardown, channel send; 5 runs).
- Each program is built by sawc2 (`compiler/tests/run/`'s build path, or `sawc2 build --std-root std`) and run under `DYLD_INSERT_LIBRARIES=/usr/lib/libgmalloc.dylib`. The verdict is the lane's today: any fault, signal or wrong output fails.
- **The program list:** map each current program to its sawc2-buildable twin (`tests/corpus/`, `compiler/tests/run/`). A program sawc2 can't build yet stays listed with the reason and the unit that unblocks it, never silently dropped. The list's coverage of the two lanes' categories is stated in the tool's docstring.
- It joins the battery as the `gmgate` stage, replacing the Stage 0 version, and stays out of the per-patch gate (it's slow).
- It runs through the shared worker budget (`tools/gate_jobs.py`, the 75% core cap).

**Done when:** both lanes pass on sawc2 builds; an injected surplus release (a deliberately dropped retain in a scratch copy of one program's build, or a probe that releases twice) faults under the lane and passes natively, proving the lane sees what it exists to see; the Stage 0 driver is gone from the battery.

**After SL-666 lands** (it rewrites `tools/battery.sh`).

SL-697.p1 submitted: gmgate runs on sawc2 (the user's "port it", SL-666).

- **Build path:** every program builds through `oracle.sawc2_build` (shared with the run lane, the census and corpusdiff), with its own `COMPILE-FLAGS`, and runs under `libgmalloc`, spread through `gate_jobs` under the 75% cap. Corpus programs are judged by their own directives, and run-lane programs by Stage 0's recorded run. Guard Malloc's banner and its per-run `debug.toggle_address_reuse` sysctl line are filtered from stderr.
- **The lists, by category:** twins (the corpus copies of the Stage 0 lane's programs), stand-ins (programs sawc2 builds today that police the same shape, labelled as such), and blocked twins, each with its first refusal's rule and the unit that clears it. Blocked twins are built on every run, and the lane fails when one starts building or changes refusal, so the lists can't silently go stale. `--list` checks them without building, including the docstring's coverage statement.
- **Coverage today:** ownership runs a program in 5 of 6 categories (Data copy-on-write waits on SL-596), and concurrency in 6 of 6. Only 2 of 26 and 4 of 25 of the Stage 0 twins build, so 45 are blocked: Arc (SL-701) 17, `group.spawn` (SL-564) 13, Data 5, and the rest one or two each. The previously unfiled gaps are now SL-710 (collection literals), SL-713 (`Printable` on `Box<any Error>`), SL-712 (forwarding through Box/Arc) and SL-714 (`len` of a fixed array).
- **The proof (done-when):** `tools/gmgate_surplus_release.saw`, a refcount whose copy skips its retain. Natively it prints 7 and exits 0, 5 of 5 times. Under Guard Malloc it takes SIGSEGV, 5 of 5 times. With the retain restored it passes. It runs as a self-test before the lanes count, and the lane fails as "blind" if it stops faulting.
- **Battery:** the `gmgate` stage runs the sawc2 tool; the Stage 0 driver is gone; it stays out of the per-patch gate. CLAUDE.md's battery paragraph is updated (lead edit).
- **Gate in the worktree:** the gmgate pin, `battery.sh gmgate citations childgroups`, and `corpus.py` on the new `.saw`. All green. Both lanes pass: 31 programs, about 1 s with sawc2 built.

**Landing interaction:** when SL-701 (Arc) or SL-564 (`group.spawn`) lands, its blocked twins start building and gmgate fails until the lists move them. gmgate is a battery stage, not in the per-patch gate, so this blocks no merge. The follow-up moves the twins after each landing.
