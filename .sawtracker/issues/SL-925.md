Raised by the Air during SL-754's review and never filed; surfaced again by p921 (SL-654).

**A `@synthesize` conformance doesn't check that every field meets the derived trait.** `tests/corpus/equatable_field_not_equatable_error.saw`: `struct Inner { name: String }` declares no Equatable; `@synthesize extension Outer: Equatable {}` over an `inner: Inner` field typechecks. The failure surfaces only in mono, as `mono.not-yet` ("a call of `Equatable.equals…` on …, which no conformance of it meets"). The spec (design 32) says deriving requires every field to conform, and that it fails naming the offending field (the program's EXPECT: "does not conform to `Equatable`").

**Obligation 4 sweep:** every `@synthesize`-derivable trait (Equatable, Comparable, Hashable, Copy, ExplicitCopy, Printable if derivable, `shared` twins) over a field (and an enum payload) that lacks it, plus a generic field whose argument lacks it. One check at the derivation, naming the field.

**Why it matters for SL-654:** this is the last route to mono's "a call … which no conformance of it meets" message, which SL-654 wants to reword as an internal INVARIANT once nothing user-reachable gets there. Do that reword here, after this check lands.

**1.
