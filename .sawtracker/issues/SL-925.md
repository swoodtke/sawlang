Raised by the Air during SL-754's review and never filed; surfaced again by p921 (SL-654).

**A `@synthesize` conformance doesn't check that every field meets the derived trait.** `tests/corpus/equatable_field_not_equatable_error.saw`: `struct Inner { name: String }` declares no Equatable; `@synthesize extension Outer: Equatable {}` over an `inner: Inner` field typechecks. The failure surfaces only in mono, as `mono.not-yet` ("a call of `Equatable.equals…` on …, which no conformance of it meets"). The spec (design 32) says deriving requires every field to conform, and that it fails naming the offending field (the program's EXPECT: "does not conform to `Equatable`").

**Obligation 4 sweep:** every `@synthesize`-derivable trait (Equatable, Comparable, Hashable, Copy, ExplicitCopy, Printable if derivable, `shared` twins) over a field (and an enum payload) that lacks it, plus a generic field whose argument lacks it. One check at the derivation, naming the field.

**Why it matters for SL-654:** this is the last route to mono's "a call … which no conformance of it meets" message, which SL-654 wants to reword as an internal INVARIANT once nothing user-reachable gets there. Do that reword here, after this check lands.

**Status (lead, Nov 12): the user-visible half is already met; what is left is small and rides behind SL-1018.**

- `tests/corpus/equatable_field_not_equatable_error.saw` is `pass` in `corpus_diff.tsv`: mono refuses with `synthesize.unmet-member`, naming the field, its type and the trait.
- That the refusal comes at the comparison and not at the declaration is the spec's rule, not a gap: Equality says "a genuinely non-Equatable member remains a clean error at the comparison site", and typecheck's derivation funnel says the same of Equatable, Comparable and Hashable (`checks.saw`, `check_derivations`). So `coro --check` staying silent on an uncompared type is by design.
- The copy policies are the exception, and SL-1018 moves them to the derivation funnel (its implementation detail of today).

**What remains here, after SL-1018 lands:**
1. A sweep (obligation 4) proving nothing user-reachable still gets to mono's "a call … which no conformance of it meets" `mono.not-yet`: Equatable, Comparable and Hashable, each over a non-conforming struct field, an enum payload, a generic field whose argument lacks it, and a tuple or optional member; each must read `synthesize.unmet-member`.
2. Then SL-654's reword of that `mono.not-yet` message as an INVARIANT.

Kept in the queue directly behind SL-1018, as one small unit.
