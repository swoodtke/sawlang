## Description

**Port `std.cbor` to the new std: `CborEncoder: Encoder` and `CborDecoder: Decoder`.** Stage 0's `sawc/std/cbor.saw` is 1,113 lines. The new std has the wire contract (`std/CBOR.md`) and the vectors (`tests/cbor_vectors/accept`, `reject`), but no module. Part of SL-596.

**Depends on SL-833** (the serde traits `Encoder`/`Decoder`/`Serialize`/`Deserialize`, and `EncodeError`/`DecodeError`). Land it after.

**Order (user, Oct 30):** cbor before Blade (SL-936). Blade's `lock.saw` CBOR half and `tests/lock_cbor.saw` need it, and that test becomes one of this port's pins.

**Shape:** a straight port to the current language and std, with the vectors as the oracle: every accept vector round-trips byte-exact, and every reject vector is refused with its stated fault. Expected tier: Sonnet, under a no-guessing rule (an undetermined behaviour is flagged OPEN, never invented).

**Corpus rows it should move:** the `cbor169_*` programs' twins in `tests/corpus/`, `d249_both_std_encoders_bare_is_a_tie_error` and `d249_std_json_and_cbor_both_name_encode` (the last two named on SL-833).
