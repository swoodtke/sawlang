**The executor half (`std/taskgroup.saw`):** `__saw_exec_offload_pipe(job)` is `__saw_rt_offload_pipe_fd(job)`, and `__saw_exec_offload_done(job)` is `__saw_rt_offload_done(job) != 0`. Their externs sit beside them, clear of T3a's reactor declarations.

**R6:** the take runs inside the task's own resume, and `complete` (the one place a frame is freed) runs only after `Ready`, so after the take. A group's teardown drives each task to completion and never cancels and frees. The take closes the job's pipe, which withdraws it from kqueue or epoll. This reasoning is single-threaded; A17a re-checks it under the pool.

**Host-name `connect` (`std/net.saw`):** a `blocking` `__saw_rt_resolve_ipv4` call (offloaded by A14a's lowering), with Stage 0's `resolve_first`/`resolve_error` ported.
