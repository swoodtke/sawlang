## Description

**The committed std suspension table is behind Stage 0's own std, and the `std_suspension` step doesn't notice** (found by p667b, SL-667 P2, Oct 24).

Re-recording `std_suspension.txt` and `stdsuspension.saw` from v0.18.0 (and from the in-tree `sawc/`, byte-identical) adds 3 rows the committed table lacks: `std.taskgroup.__bt_write`, `__saw_exec_create_reactor` and `__saw_exec_poll_counted`. So the table was already stale against Stage 0's own std, and the step passes anyway: it checks something narrower than "the table equals a fresh record".

**Scope:**
- find what `std_suspension`'s step actually checks, and why a 3-row drift passes;
- decide what the table is for once Stage 0 is gone (SL-667): if it's a Stage-0-derived oracle, re-record it once from v0.18.0 and freeze it with the other snapshots; if sawc is its source of truth, derive it from sawc;
- make the step fail on drift either way (a check that would pass vacuously is the risk SL-667's plan §4 guards against).
