**Choose `sawc build`'s codegen-unit and LTO defaults from measurement** (the owner, Nov 26: "let's definitely do the codegen-unit and LTO defaults").

**Today:**
- `--codegen-units=1` is the default: the whole program is one LLVM module, compiled by one clang process.
- A split build (`--codegen-units=module`, one unit per Saw module, compiled in parallel) links with ThinLTO unless it is `-O0`.
- Only a split build uses the per-unit object cache.
- `SAWC_CODEGEN_UNITS` and `SAWC_LTO` override the defaults (`driver_codegen_choice`).

**The lead's measurement** (Nov 26, the sawc driver itself, `-O2`, `--no-object-cache --no-front-cache`, Apple clang, the Studio):

| | Wall | clang | link |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--codegen-units=1` | 58.7 s | 52.3 s (one 101 MB module) | 0.08 s |
| `--codegen-units=module` | 24.5 s | 4.8 s (parallel) | 12.5 s (ThinLTO, presumably) |

Parsing the text IR is about 2 s of the `-O2` compile (`llvm-as` on Homebrew LLVM), so in-process LLVM is not the lever; the codegen split is. (The pinned, fetched LLVM toolchain is deferred by the owner.)

**What the defaults are chosen from (a measurement sweep first):**
- **Build time:** cold (no caches) and warm after a one-line edit in one module (object cache on), per mode: units 1; units module with ThinLTO; units module with no LTO; units module with full LTO. At `-O0`, `-O2` and `-Os`, on the sawc driver, Blade, a mid-size program and a small one.
- **The code produced:** runtime of the output (sawc compiling a fixed workload, Blade's tests), binary size, and stack frame sizes where they matter (freestanding).
- **Why the split link takes 12.5 s:** `-flto-jobs`, the linker, the ThinLTO cache.
- **A consumer survey for the flip (obligation 2):** who relies on today's single-module default:
  - build.py's stage builds and the fixpoint (stage 2 against 3);
  - the run lane;
  - the freestanding suite and `-c` objects (a split `-c` needs `llvm-objcopy`);
  - dsymutil and DWARF;
  - `--emit-ll`;
  - determinism;
  - Blade, sawos's image builds, and the Linux lane.

**Then:** the lead proposes the defaults (they may differ by optimization level, for example split with ThinLTO for `-O1` and above, and split without LTO at `-O0`), recorded here, and a unit flips them with the consumers updated.
