Found by p540's SL-540 sweep (`.build/scratch/p540/plan.md` §1).

**Two TOTAL casts on non-scalar types are refused by the backend** under the same `backend.not-yet: this conversion is not in the backend slice yet` message as SL-540, although neither is a partial conversion:
- an `as` through a distinct alias over `String` (alias ↔ underlying);
- a same-type `as Arc<Res>` (an identity cast), which is conformance row V65's program.

The front end accepts both. Each is representationally free (an alias is its underlying at runtime, and an identity cast is a no-op), so the backend's `convert_into` total branch should pass a non-scalar operand through when the cast is an identity at the representation, with a pin each, and V65's ledger row updated.
