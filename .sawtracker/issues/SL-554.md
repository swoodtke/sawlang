- **Scope:**
  - For each framed item, the first-cut storage lifetime of each local: maybe-initialised (`MirInit`) through its scope-end drop. That is conservative, and B1 refines it.

- **Done when:** the layout and resident sets are stable in goldens, and the backend still refuses (A3b lowers).

**A3a stopped before implementation: the projection kind cannot land inside the unit's file list.**

The plan gives A3a "a new MIR projection, the frame slot `(*frame).[off: T]`", touching `compiler/mir/src/{model,dump,verify}.saw` "for the projection kind only", and the brief says not to touch `compiler/llvm`. Those two cannot both hold:

- `compiler/llvm/src/places.saw`, `LlEmitter.place_addr`, holds an exhaustive `match step.kind` over `MirProjKind` (arms `Field`, `Tuple`, `Deref`, `Index`, `Payload`, `Force`, no `case _`).
- A new `MirProjKind` case makes that match non-exhaustive, and Stage 0 refuses it while building Stage 1. Probe (`.build/scratch/probe_exh.saw` in my worktree, a three-case enum matched on two): `error: match is not exhaustive, missing variants: C`.
- It is the only exhaustive match over `MirProjKind` in `compiler/`. The other consumers (borrowck, drops, mir's `initialisation`, `build`, `lower`) test kinds with `==`, so they compile, and they never meet the new kind since nothing in A3a creates one.

Two ways to resolve it, both reversible. The choice is the lead's:

1. **A3a's files gain `compiler/llvm/src/places.saw`, one arm.** `case FrameSlot -> { refuse_here(...); return addr }`, naming SL-555. It is unreachable in A3a: nothing creates a frame slot yet, and `coro.not-yet` still refuses every framed item before the backend. A3b, which already lists `places.saw`, replaces the arm with the real lowering (one `getelementptr` off the frame pointer). This crosses into SL-592's directory. I have not checked whether SL-592 edits `places.saw`.
2. **The projection kind moves to A3b,** which already owns `places.saw` and is the first unit to create the projection. A3a keeps the resident sets, the first-cut storage lifetimes, `assign(values, interference) -> offsets` with the no-sharing policy, and `sawc2 coro --layouts`. `--layouts` prints each resident value's offset, size and alignment, and A3b's lowering is what spells it as `(*frame).[off: T]`.

Nothing in A3a's other scope depends on the choice. `MirInit` runs on mono's concrete bodies through `init_analysis`. I'm holding the rest of the unit until you rule, as the brief says.

**Review note (lead): SL-554.p1, unit A3a.**

**What it builds:**
- **Lifetimes and the resident set:** for each framed item, a first-cut lifetime per local, from where it may be initialised (`MirInit`) to its scope-end drop, and the resident set, the locals whose lifetime contains a suspension point.
  - **Suspension points:** each definitely-suspending call (after its arguments are taken, before its result is written) and each yield-capable op-budget point.
  - **Lifetime rule:** a definitely-moved local is dead until it is reassigned. A local that is only ever copied lives to the end; B1 refines that.
- **The layout entry,** `coro_assign(values, interference) -> offsets`, here with the no-sharing policy. Interference is computed and passed in, so B2 replaces only the policy body.
- **Frame order:** the state word (`Int`), `_0`, every parameter, then resident locals in local order. The frame has no drop glue (§3.1). Child-frame slots are A4's.
- **The MIR projection `FrameSlot`,** spelled `(*_K).[OFFSET: T]`. Its verifier rule: a frame slot is the second step of its place, directly after the dereference of a local, at an offset of at least 0.
- **`sawc2 coro --layouts [--target]`:** the record format is in the coro README.
- **`compiler/llvm/src/places.saw`:** exactly ONE arm, `case FrameSlot`, which refuses and names SL-555 (the lead's decision, c3). It can't be reached: `coro.not-yet` still refuses every framed item before the backend.

**Pins:**
- **p01** is `coro/golden/yield_loop.saw`, with `.coro` and `.layouts`. A `blocking` extern stands in for `yield_now`, and the header gives the swap. `tally`'s frame is 64 bytes: the state word, the result, `rounds`, `label`, `total`, `i`, the `usleep` result and `step`. The seven moved temporaries stay on the stack.
- Every coro golden now has a `.layouts` file beside its `.coro`. A1's three `.coro` files are byte-identical.
- The agreement pass runs `--layouts` over all 75 mono and run-lane programs.
- The unit program `mir/tests/verify_frame_slots.saw`, over `tests/mir/inject/frame_slots.saw`: the dump spells `(*_1).[8: Int]` and the verifier admits it; a slot directly on `_1`, and one at a negative offset, are both reported.

**Files beyond the list:**
- `coro/src/{program,api,suspends}.saw`: the frame tables, the options, a `cyclic` flag (no frames are computed when a cycle is refused) and a public `coro_source_label`;
- `driver/src/main.saw`: the `coro --layouts` and `--target` options;
- the lane, README and `run.py`'s whitespace list.

**Lead's checks:**

- Read `yield_loop.layouts` against the program: the resident set is as the header claims, and `main` has no child slot, correctly, since that's A4's.
- Confirmed the `places.saw` arm is the single ruled one.
