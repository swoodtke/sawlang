## Unit A: GRAMMAR.md, the recognizer and the corpus

**W1: a `while` head unwraps an optional place, and a `guard` takes no borrow binding** (SL:borrowing §2.4).
1. **§9:** a borrow binding stands in a `borrow` block, an `if`, `else if` or `while` head, a `for` head, and a variant's payload pattern. A `guard`, the top level of a `case` pattern and a tuple pattern take none.
2. **The `while` production** gains the unwrap head: `"while" borrow-unwrap NEWLINE* block`, or whatever shape fits `syntax.borrow.unwrap`'s node. Give the alternative its own `@name`.
3. **`syntax.rule.borrow-form`:** "at an `if` or `while` head, an optional-place unwrap".
4. **A named refusal for a borrow binding at a `guard` head.** Add a removed production with its §10 row, spec and hint. The hint names `if borrow let e = … { … } else { … }`.
5. **§12:** the `cond` cells follow for the `while` and boolean `guard` hosts. The parenthesized block stays a condition in both.
6. **The recognizer:** SL-426's `if-head` filter, which makes a binding at an `if` head the unwrap, extends to the `while` head. Add the `guard` refusal.
7. **The corpus:**
   - the bare-block negative and the unwrap golden at a `while` head;
   - the `guard` refusal negative;
   - the parenthesized forms as goldens at both hosts.

**W2: one-element tuple patterns and trailing commas.**
1. **§8 `tuple-pattern`:** `()`, `(p,)`, and two or more elements with an optional trailing comma, `(a, b)` and `(a, b,)`.
2. **A bare `(p)` in a pattern is refused.** Add a removed production with its §10 row, and the hint "write `(p,)`".
3. **`variant-pattern`'s payload list** takes an optional trailing comma: `Some(x,)` and `A(x, y,)`.
4. **§16:** a row for sawc, which reads `(p)` as a one-tuple and refuses `(p,)`, `(a, b,)` and `Some(x,)`. Measure each of those before writing it.
5. **The corpus.** U4e's pins flip:
   - the golden `one-element-is-a-tuple`;
   - the two `one-element-trailing-comma` negatives;
   - the Air's refused `case (a, b,)` and `case Some(x,)`.

   Record them with `cases.py --write`. Add goldens for `(p,)`, `(a, b,)` and `Some(x,)`, and a negative for `(p)` at each pattern position: `case`, `let`, `if let`, `guard let`, `while let` and nested.

## Unit B: the parser

1. **One comma-list funnel.** Today each comma list in `compiler/parse/src/parser.saw` has its own loop: call arguments, tuple expressions, tuple types, tuple patterns, variant payloads, generic parameters and generic arguments, and U4f's lists. Replace them with one function that takes:
   - the element parser;
   - the closing token;
   - a trailing-comma policy: *allowed*; *refused*, naming its rule and hint (generic lists, per GRAMMAR); or *required for one element*.

   It returns the element count and whether a trailing comma was seen. Line breaks inside the brackets follow §2.4 in one place.

   - Prove the lane with an injection.

Re the brief: Unit A implements both rulings as recorded. Four constraints for Unit B's funnel, where the lists it absorbs differ in ways a refactor could erase quietly:

1. **The closer is a predicate and an action, not a token.**
   - **Generic lists** close through `closes_list()` and `close_list()`. They split `>>`, `>=` and `>>=` in place, and a `>>=` closes only when two lists are open.
   - **Closure parameters** end at `in`, not at a bracket. Line breaks before the `in` belong to the head (§7.5).
2. **Line breaks differ by list.**
   - Inside `( )` and `[ ]`, §2.4 makes them insignificant.
   - Map and set lists sit in braces, where §2.4 doesn't apply. Their productions spell `NEWLINE*` around entries, including before a set's `,`.
   - A map entry's `:` must stay on its key's line, which is the refusal c53's matrix pins.
3. **The generic-args refusal must keep U4c's speculation behaviour.** Inside a speculation, a trailing comma is noted, and refused only once the list is kept. If the funnel refuses inside the speculation instead, the list fails and is re-read as comparisons. Then `f<Int,>(1)` gets a different refusal from today's `refused-arg-comma`. That case, and the kept, split lists, should be rows in the position matrix.
4. **The lane needs an exemption list for commas that separate things other than list elements.** A `match` arm's `,` separator (arm-sep) consumes `Comma` in a loop that is not a bracket list. Give each exemption a reason, as the speculation ledger does, so a new loop outside the funnel still fails. And prove the lane with an injection that is not exempt.
