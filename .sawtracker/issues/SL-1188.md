**Release v0.24.4.** The project owner ruled on Nov 25, in the lead's session, on sawos's m1641: tag now with what is merged, and tag v0.24.5 once SL-1150 and SL-1161 land.

sawos is pinned at v0.24.3 and moves to this tag to start:
- SO-158, distinct index types in the kernel (SL-1012);
- the yield seam, with sosrt defining `__saw_rt_thread_yield` (SL-1159).

**The patch:** `.sawtracker/version` becomes `v0.24.4`, and `driver_version()` in `compiler/driver/src/main.saw` becomes `"0.24.4"`; run.py's `version` step holds the two equal. The server creates the annotated tag at the patch's merge commit. Anything merged before it is in the tag.

**What v0.24.4 holds beyond v0.24.3:**
- **SL-1012, type aliases are distinct types.**
  - `type UserId = Int` is its own type. A value of the representation needs `UserId(x)`, and an alias converts down to its representation implicitly.
  - An alias's siblings (aliases on a diverging chain) don't mix: `OrderId(user as Int)`.
  - An alias of an enum, `Optional` or `Result` is refused.
  - An alias takes no extension.
  - Literals adopt an alias at a `static` or an annotated `let`.
  - Joins merge to the chain's type.
  - Mono keys by representation.
- **SL-1161 p1, the join pair.**
  - `Task.join` inside a task parks, and its `blocking` twin sweeps: a `main` or an `@export` that joins needs `blocking`.
  - Task and `TaskGroup` handles are thread-bound.
  - A pool pins a frame only while it parks holding one.
  - `FrameDescriptor` gains a trailing `thread_bound` word (runtime/ABI.md).
  - Lock-blocked deadlocks are a stated limit.
- **SL-1156 U1-U8, lending a static from a `borrows(ROOT)` function** (a function with no receiver):
  - the declaration and naming rules, the lend and the window charging the root;
  - the call summary refusing a conflicting window opened through a callee;
  - the `Sync` check on a shared root, and shared windows across threads;
  - the `deinit` a declared root `unsafe static var` may hold.

  U7c (made root-free at creation) is in revision and lands later.
- **SL-1159:** `SpinLock` spins, then yields through a weak `__saw_rt_thread_yield` seam (`@weak`, with std's default for freestanding).
- **SL-1170, external C:** objects, archives, libraries, library paths and frameworks reach sawc's link (`--link-*` flags), and Blade's `[native]` compiles and caches a package's C sources, including into an image.
- **SL-1182 unit 1:** `libs/lz4`, vendored lz4 behind a safe Saw API.
- **SL-1166:** a task's frame has a compile-time maximum per target profile (`--max-task-frame=SIZE`, and a Blade key).
- **SL-1163 and SL-1116:** a type is at most 2^31-1 bytes on every target, and layout arithmetic is checked (no panic, no wrong size at riscv32).
- **SL-1174:** a `///` above a private field keeps it private.
- **Soundness and crash fixes:**
  - SL-677 (`try` over a binding judged by the payload's copy tier);
  - SL-935 (64-bit hashes on 32-bit targets use both words);
  - SL-1112 (the executor's internal entries are not importable);
  - SL-1155, SL-1171, SL-1173 (MIR invariants);
  - SL-1175 (an `extern "C"` is checked against an `@export` of the same symbol);
  - SL-1176 (a seam export's width class);
  - SL-1167 (the hosted runtime honours alignment above 16);
  - SL-1158 (esp-clang's frame pressure).
- **Tools:**
  - SL-1092 (`libs/toml` reads all of TOML 1.0);
  - SL-1122 (Blade manifests);
  - SL-1154 (Blade's `--no-hidden-alloc` opt-in);
  - SL-1183 (`blade test` shows compile errors);
  - SL-1164 (stable Homebrew tool paths);
  - SL-1177, SL-1178 (the gate's complexity row and lock).

**For a project moving its pin from v0.24.3 (sawos):**
- **Aliases:** an alias needs an explicit construction from its representation (`Handle(x)`), and an alias of an enum is refused.
- **Joins:** a joining `@export kmain` takes `blocking`.
- **The runtime:** the descriptor's `thread_bound` word; a runtime may define `__saw_rt_thread_yield` strongly (it returns at once in interrupt context).
- **Sizes:** a type over 2^31-1 bytes is refused.
- One rebuild of every package (a new `--build-id`).

**Known, not in v0.24.4:**
- SL-1150 (`Task.spawn` freestanding) and SL-1161.p2 (the teardown park): these two make v0.24.5;
- SL-1156 U7c;
- SL-1186, SL-1187.

**Landing:**
1. `git rev-parse v0.24.4^{commit}`.
2. Check that the merges above are ancestors.
3. Post the version and sha to `@claude-sawos`.
