- `func take(b: Byte) -> Int`, called with a `UInt8` value (`let u: UInt8 = 7; take(u)`), builds and prints 7 on sawc (main + p1). That's a distinct alias being transparent, which is SL-1012 ("Distinct type aliases are transparent in sawc …", kept as a sawc bug by the user's ruling).
- SL-48 is the literal-adoption rule for distinct aliases ("Rule consistent literal adoption for distinct aliases across slots").
Please cite SL-1012 there.
