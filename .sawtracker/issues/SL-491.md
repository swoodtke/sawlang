**Found by:** batch B2 (tcb2), on SL-486. **Area:** typecheck, reference sigils on a subscript.

**The defect:** `&v[0]` and `&var v[0]` are still accepted. SL:borrowing §9 retires them in favour of `borrow v[0]` and `borrow var v[0]`, and the spec after SL-482 says the same (`&v[i]` becomes `borrow v[i]`). B2 exempts them from the D29 getitem rule so as not to refuse something that is still accepted. The two changes should come together:
- refuse the retired spelling, with a fix-it to the `borrow` form;
- then drop the exemption.

**Scope:**
- every argument and binding position where a sigiled subscript is accepted today, including method-call receivers and chained subscripts;
- std, `compiler/` and the tests corpus all need a census of uses first. They may still use the retired spelling, because Stage 0 compiles them.

Coordinate with SL:hazards, because Stage 0 may still require the sigil form somewhere.
