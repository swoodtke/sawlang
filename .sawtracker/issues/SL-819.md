## Description

**Move `String.to_float` and `StringBuilder.append(value: Float)` into their receivers' modules (SL-722 U5, second patch, after the seed bump).** Per the lead's ruling (SL-722 c50), the public members live in `std/string.saw` and `std/stringbuilder.saw`, as thin calls into `std.float`'s `public(package)` `float_parse`/`float_append`, so that they work without `import std.float`, as `String.to_int` does. Those two modules import `std.float`, and every compiler stage reaches them.

The pinned seed's `load_new_std_modules` has no `float` line, so Stage 1 then fails (`import.unknown-module std.float`; p722u5, Oct 22). That is the two-patch rule (compiler/README.md "The seed", SL-662).

**Sequence:**
1. **U5's first patch (SL-722):** the `float` loader line, `std/float.saw`, numeric's Float half, the Format cell, and the std profile's `fixed-array`. Until this issue lands, `to_float` and `append(value: Float)` stay `std.float` extensions, visible with `import std.float`.
2. **ONE seed bump, in SL-815,** to a main commit holding both SL-788's patch and U5's first patch.
3. **This issue:** move the two members, using p722u5's worktree work (the members, the one-way imports, the free-function helpers). `string_to_float` and `float_text_format` then pass without an import; record the baseline.
