LANGUAGE_SPEC "The Copy Trait Family": a place read whose element type mentions a type parameter "is legal exactly when every parameter the element type mentions carries a `Copy`-family bound, and the refusal is reported in the generic body rather than at one caller's instantiation."

In sawc, `func rd1<K>(v: &Vector<K>) -> Int { let s = v[0]; 1 }` passes `coro --check`. Instantiating it with a NoCopy `R` reports copy.requirement at the CALL: "`rd1` requires `K` to be Copy, as its body copies one…".

The probes are `/Users/shawn/Projects/sawlang/.build/scratch/p1000f/F6_generic_read_site.saw` and `/Users/shawn/Projects/sawlang/.build/scratch/p1000f/a_generic_read.saw`.

**Expected:** a refusal in the body at the read, naming the missing bound, whether or not anything instantiates it.

**Ruled by the user (Nov 9):** sawc's behaviour is adopted: the requirement is inferred from the body, and a breaking instantiation is refused at its call, naming the body site. Rewrite the spec sentence ("…the refusal is reported in the generic body rather than at one caller's instantiation") and drop its pending note.
