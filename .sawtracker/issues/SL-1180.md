**`Child.wait` sleeps the calling thread, so a task calling it stalls its executor and the frames pinned to its worker.**

**The mechanism:** `Child.wait` → `process_reaped` loops on `__saw_rt_sleep_ns`, which sleeps the thread, and neither function is `blocking`. Two effects follow:
- **On a pool:** the worker runs nothing until the child exits, including the frames SL-1161 pins to it. The reviewer's `cmdwait_b.saw` has a 10 ms nap ending more than 500 ms late in 20/20 runs on SL-1161 r3/r4, against 0/10 on v0.24.3.
- **On a cooperative executor:** a task's `Child.wait` blocks the executor's thread. That predates SL-1161.

**The direction** (unruled, since it is an API change): in a task, a child's exit is waited for by parking, on the reactor (a pidfd or kqueue `EVFILT_PROC`) or on a timer, through a suspending twin, the way `Task.join` became a pair. SL-1161 states the limit in K227 meanwhile.
