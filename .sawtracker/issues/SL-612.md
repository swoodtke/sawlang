**A6. A NoCopy value is never duplicated by a repeat literal, `??` or a cell constructor** (SL-606, bucket A; the Air moved it out of C in m520). Findings: Typecheck-body 2 and 3.

- **The bugs:**
  - `repeat_expr` only `consume`s its value, so `[make(); 3]` with a NoCopy `Handle`, and `[Vector<Int>(); 2]`, are accepted and duplicate the element bitwise.
  - `coalesce_expr` and `cell_construct` call `consume(x)` before `through_refs(x)`, so `o ?? Handle(…)` with `o: &Handle?` and `UnsafeMutableInterior(r)` over `r: &Handle` copy a NoCopy referent silently.
  - All three pass `drops --check`, so they are double drops.
- **Fix:**
  - the repeat literal requires a freely copyable element (spec, Composite Types);
  - the other two sites read through the reference before the copy check, so the check sees `Handle`;
  - add all three to `read_value`'s ENTRY POINTS.

  The full copy-tier funnel (bucket C) comes later. Keep this to the three sites.
- **Pins:** the review's `review_tcbody_repeat`, `_coalesce_ref` and `_cell_ref` probes, each refused, plus controls with a Copy element and a Copy referent, which are accepted.
- **Done when:** all three are refused and the controls are unchanged.
