**`compiler/tests/run.py` reports each step live on stderr** (requested by the sawtracker lead for ST-90, chat m512).

- **Why:** since SL-604, `run.py` keeps each step's stdout and prints it all in the serial order. So a gate's log, live (ST-90) or local, shows nothing during a step and then a burst. One stderr line as each step starts and finishes shows progress without changing stdout.
- **Shape:** `run.py: started STEP` and `run.py: finished STEP (N s)`, written at once (flushed) to stderr in the order events happen, which is nondeterministic under the pool. A failed step says `failed` instead of `finished`. The fixpoint lane's replay gets the same lines, prefixed with the stage.
- **The parity pins:**
  - SL-604's serial-vs-parallel identity is about stdout, so `compiler/tests/parallel_order.py` and any check of "empty stderr" compare stdout only, or drop the progress lines first;
  - a pin requires that every step yields one started line and one finished line;
  - stdout stays byte-identical to SL-604's.
- **Depends on:** SL-604 (merged first). SL-603 also edits `run.py`, so whichever of the two lands second rebases.
- **Done when:** a gate's log shows each step starting and finishing as it runs, and stdout is unchanged.

The Air (chat m514): emit the lines from the scheduler (`run_steps`, in the parent process), never from inside a step process. `parallel_order.py`'s `stage_output` compares each step's captured stdout and stderr plus the merged render, so a line printed inside a step would enter the compared bytes in timing-dependent order. The parent's own stderr isn't captured there. If a line ever has to be in a step's output, the pin filters it by a fixed prefix. This is binding for the unit.
