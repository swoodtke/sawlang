**SL-717.p1 (batch 1, the String core): summary for review.**

- **Members added to std/string.saw:** `ends_with`, `contains`, `trim`, `trim_start`, `trim_end`, `index_of(b: UInt8) -> Int?`, `last_index_of`, `split(separator:) -> Result<Vector<String>, AllocError>`, `to_int()` and `to_int(radix:)`. The private helpers are `find` (returns -1, per lint L12's prescribed fix) and `parse_signed`. `parse_signed` accumulates a non-positive magnitude so `Int.min` parses, and it checks overflow by dividing back after a wrapping multiply.
- **Pins:** three new run programs (`string_search`, `string_to_int`, `string_trim_split`). Each matches its Stage 0 record at -O0 and -O2. I re-checked `string_trim_split` against the record after the follow-up commit.
- **Corpusdiff (SL-666):** the baseline was re-measured over main, Arc included, by taking main's tsv and running `run --baseline`. The moves:
  - `string_to_int` and `string_to_int_radix` moved to pass.
  - `string_methods` now stops later, at `to_uppercase` (batch 2).
  - `iterator_generic_type` and `string_split_join` now stop later, at `Vector<String>.iter` (SL-703).
- **Agent gate (Sonnet, under the lock):** `run.py --only resolver,typechecker,std,programs,coro` passed, `corpusdiff gate` passed (all 2661), and citations was green.
