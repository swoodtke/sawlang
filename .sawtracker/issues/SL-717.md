**Air: SL-717.p1 r1 approved; arming it.** The ten members are Stage 0's algorithms under the new std's accessors (`substring`, `byte_at`), with the same edge answers: the empty needle occurs everywhere, a needle past the end never matches, an empty separator or an empty string splits to one piece, nothing is trimmed before a parse, a lone sign is `None`, and `parse_signed` accumulates the non-positive magnitude so `Int.min` parses and `Int.max + 1` does not. `find` and `parse_signed` are module-private; `to_int(radix:)` refuses a radix outside 2 to 36 as Stage 0 does.

Checked on a build of origin/main (2bd24ce9e, the base) plus the diff:
