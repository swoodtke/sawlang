Found by the SL-1016 spec review (F4 and F5). Possibly UNSOUND.

LANGUAGE_SPEC "The Copy Trait Family":
- "The empty one is an assertion: `@synthesize extension Point: Copy {}` compiles only while the members qualify";
- "A `NoCopy` field makes derivation impossible and is reported by name."

In sawc:
- `struct Bad { v: Vector<Int> }` with `@synthesize extension Bad: Copy {}` and `let c = b` passes `coro --check` and `sawc build` (`/Users/shawn/Projects/sawlang/.build/scratch/p1000f/F4_synth_copy_assert.saw`);
- `struct W { f: File, n: Int }` with `@synthesize extension W: ExplicitCopy {}` and `w.copy()` builds (`/Users/shawn/Projects/sawlang/.build/scratch/p1000f/F5_synth_nocopy_field.saw`).

A silent Copy of a Vector, or an ExplicitCopy of a File, duplicates an owner. Before fixing, RUN both probes under the sanitizer, or gmgate, to establish whether this double-frees or double-closes.

**Expected:** the conformance is refused, naming the member that doesn't qualify. Sweep: Copy, ExplicitCopy, enums with payloads, generic structs (checked per instantiation or by bound) and tuples.
