## Description

Split out of SL-118 by the Sep 25 tracker cleanup (SL:tracker-cleanup). The runtime is Saw code that carries over to the new compiler (SL:architecture §3.11).

Design 130's follow-up: decompose the oversized functions the unsafe migration marked wholly unsafe, so the policy "an unsafe function is short enough to review as a unit" actually holds:
- `__saw_exec_worker` (about 150 lines);
- the `rt/host_*/reactor.saw` bodies;
- `rt/common/os_ops.saw` (15 of the runtime's 47 marks on its own).

Shape: extract the raw-pointer bookkeeping into small `unsafe` helpers and leave the surrounding loop safe. This is judgment-heavy refactoring of the executor's hot paths, which is why design 130 kept it separate from its mechanical migration.

Under the freeze this is not scheduled unless it blocks the new compiler.
