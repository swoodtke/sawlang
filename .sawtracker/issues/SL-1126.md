## Description

**Two rule questions about optional chains, for the project owner. Found by the reviewer while probing SL-1119.p1 (Nov 14); neither is a defect of that patch, and each is the same on main apart from what the patch newly lets build.** The reviewer's probes are `x19a_move_chain_timing.saw` and the generic one in `.build/scratch/air-review/sl1119_p1/agent/probes/` on its machine (ask in chat).

**Q1. `(move o)?.label` does not consume `o` at the statement.** The chain reads in place, and `o` is dropped at the end of its scope, not where it was moved. Options: (a) a `move` at a chain's head consumes the Optional at that statement (the chain's value is read out of a temporary, which is then dropped), which is what `move` means everywhere else; (b) today's behaviour is the rule, and the spec says that a `move` at a chain's head is a no-op for timing. **Lead recommends (a).**

**Q2. A generic body moves a `NoCopy` final field out of a chain, and the same chain written without the generic is refused.** `func first<T>(...) -> T? { cell(...)?.value }` at a `NoCopy` `T` builds (since SL-1119; before it, it stopped at SL-1119's internal error) and moves the value out of the temporary, with every deinit running once. `make(true)?.t` with a `NoCopy` field `t` is refused by `transfer.implicit-copy`, the rule that a chain's final field must be copyable (design 111). Options: (a) the generic body is refused too, at its declaration, for want of a `Copy` bound on `T` (a place read in a generic body needs a `Copy` bound: the design-141 rule); (b) moving the final field out of a chain whose head is an owned temporary is allowed for every type, generic or not, since nothing else names the temporary, and the copyable-final-field rule narrows to a chain whose head is a place. **Lead recommends (b)**: it is what the lowering already does soundly, and it removes a refusal with no safety reason behind it; but it changes a design-111 sentence, so it is the owner's.

Until answered, nothing changes: the generic spelling builds and is sound by the reviewer's deinit counts, and the plain spelling is refused.

**What the sweep found.**
- **The lost `move` is wider than a chain's head, and it is also SL-1125.** `(move x)` as the base of any data-field read (`.f`, `!.f`, `?.f`, `.0`, `[i].f` on a fixed array) is read in place: no move is recorded, `x` stays usable (`let n = (move o)?.n; let m = o?.n` builds), and `x` is dropped at the end of its scope, not the statement. **Lead's note with p2 (Nov 15): unit U2. A chain over an owned temporary moves its final field out (Q2), and `(move x)?.finish()` consumes at its statement; with it the issue closes.** One Sonnet unit from the detail's U2 plus the cell U1 left. Evidence: `.build/scratch/p1126u2/` in the lead's checkout. The lead read the baseline's diff and the amended program, ran `front_parts.py --check` (ok), and checked the patch applies to main.

**The two changes.**
- **Typecheck** (`optional_chain`, one branch): a non-silently-copied final field is refused only when the chain's top is a PLACE (`self.expr_place[top]`); over a temporary it is consumed, as `!.` and `.` already were. `refuse_chain_field`'s text is unchanged; the funnel's docstring says the rule is the place head's.
- **Lowering** (`move_val`): a `move` whose use is `Move` but that a `?.` hop reads the payload of (`reads_payload`) also goes into a statement temporary, through `moved_to_temporary`. Its typecheck dump confirmed U1's guess: the use was `Move` with a `payload` adjust, so U1's branch never saw it.

**The matrix** (448 cells, `-O0` and `-O2`; `chain_compare.md`):
- **Every one of the 252 temporary-head cells builds and drops each value the expected number of times;** 108 flipped from refused to right (every `ExplicitCopy`, `NoCopy` and `Box` final field over a call, `(call)`, `(move h)`, a second hop, a method's result in the middle, a value `if`, `try!`, a `??` result).
- **Every one of the 196 place-head cells is unchanged, message for message.**
- A temporary whose own type writes a `deinit`: `?.t` is now the existing `slice.not-yet`, as `!.t` and `.t` are; never an internal error. Generic bodies unchanged.
- **`(move x)?.finish()`**: at a `let`, a `var` and a by-value parameter, both levels, the value is dropped at the statement and a later use is `move.use-after` (12 cells; all internal errors before).

**Pins.** Conformance **O06** and **O19** rewritten to the narrowed rule: `O06_chain_final_field_over_a_temporary_moves_out.saw` (11 refusals before) and `O19_chain_final_field_over_a_place_is_refused.saw` (the place rule, unchanged). V175's programs gain the three `?.finish()` forms and its row no longer names them as an internal error. A run probe, `optional_chain_final_field_temporary.saw` (`-O0`, `-O2`, `leaks: none`); a MIR refusal fixture for the `deinit`-writing temporary. `transfer.implicit-copy.chain-field-temporary.saw` is deleted (its program now builds; O06 holds it). **`tests/corpus/errors/optional_chain_nocopy_field.saw`** (its head was a call, so it would now build) is kept an error program by moving its head to a local, the rule that stays, with directives that hold for sawc's text; its baseline row moves from its frozen oracle to "judged by its own directives".

**The spec** (optional chaining): "A final FIELD projection of a chain over a place (a local, a parameter, a field, an element) copies its value, so the field must be copyable ... Over an owned temporary (a call's result, a `move`d binding) the final field is moved out of it, whatever its type, and the rest of the temporary is dropped at the end of the statement, as `!.` and `.` do." The saw-lang skill says the same.

**Gate:** run.py `seed,units,golden,subset,fixture_whitespace,std,typechecker,mir,borrowck,drops,mono,coro,programs,comments,docs`; `citations` and `gmgate`; `corpusdiff.py run --baseline` (its diff: O06, O19 and the `errors/` program's row only); after a rebase, a rebuild and `typechecker,mir,drops,programs`; after the `errors/` program's edit, the baseline and `citations` again. No line saying failed but the corpus programs' own expected text. Lint ok.

**Not in this patch:** moving a field out of a temporary whose own type writes a `deinit` (`slice.not-yet` everywhere: the spec says nothing of it; the MIR lane records the question open).
