**Lead's note with p1 (Nov 24).** Evidence is in `.build/scratch/p1177/`: `bisect.tsv`, the per-stage raw counts, `siblings.txt`, and the one-row probe `measure_params.py`.

**The bisect** covered 8 builds, each commit's own Stage 2, median of 3 runs at N=1600:

| commit | patch | median r |
|---|---|---|
| a117fd2c3 | SL-983.p1 | 2.050 |
| f50a76173 | SL-1156.p4 | 2.052 |
| 9c1dcbbaf | SL-1156.p5 | 2.163 |

It is one step, about +0.11, at SL-1156.p5, the static-root summaries.

**The mechanism** (verified):
- `sample` at N=51200 puts 622 of about 700 samples in `TcProgram.eval_function_arguments`.
- That function calls `takes_function_argument` for each written argument, and for a named call that function scans all `c.bind_count` bindings. So a call of N arguments costs N².
- Typecheck's instructions rose 34M at N=1600 and 132M at N=3200: four times the cost for twice the size.

**The fix:** one pass (`function_argument_table`) builds the per-position answer. Its behaviour is identical:
- the first binding of a position decides;
- a position out of range is false;
- a value call keeps its own path;
- every other role is false.

**Siblings:**
- `calls`, `ctor` and `match` measured 2.030, 2.030 and 2.051 on the culprit, so they are not affected; ctor calls and value calls don't scan the bindings.
- With the fix, every row is under its ceiling: params 2.051, yields 2.082, match_mixed 2.057, and the rest at or below 2.05.
- `mir --check`'s similar extra is UNVERIFIED as the same cost: it runs typecheck first. The lane's total for `params` is back at 2.049.
