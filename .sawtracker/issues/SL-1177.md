**The complexity lane's `params` row sits at its failure line on main, so any SL gate can fail on it whatever the patch changes.**

**The evidence:**
- **Main** (74676bf21), measured by the lead with `compiler/tests/complexity/complexity_lane.py`: `params` at N=1600 has a median r of 2.154, from 2.141, 2.178 and 2.154. Its ceiling is 2.07 and its slack 0.10, so it fails above 2.17.
- **Server gate:** SL-1156.p7's gate failed only on this step, with r = 2.17. That patch changes no compiler or std source.
- **The reviewer** (chat m1464) saw 2.17 to 2.19 on SL-1161.p1's tree and on a control tree of main plus SL-1170.p1.
- **The measure** is instructions retired, so load doesn't explain it.
- **The other seven rows** sit within 0.05 of their ceilings.

**The shape** (`shapes.py`) is one `func g(p0: Int, … p{N-1}: Int)` and one call `g(s.v.len(), …)` with N arguments, each a place read through the same root `s`. Its ceiling was recorded at 2.07 (SL-983).

**Mechanism: unknown, and presumed a class until a sweep says otherwise.** A per-argument check that compares against every earlier argument, or every loan on the root, would grow like this. Candidates by date include:
- SL-1156's root summaries (U7a) and window checks;
- SL-1175's and SL-1176's export and extern signature agreement;
- SL-1159's `@weak`;
- SL-1012's alias work.

UNVERIFIED: no commit has been measured.

**Plan:**
1. Bisect the `params` row over main's landings since its ceiling was written. Use the row alone at N=1600, three runs per commit, with each commit's own Stage 2.
2. Name the commit and the stage: `--time-stages`, or per-stage instruction counts at 2, N and 2N.
3. Make the stage near-linear again. If the growth is intrinsic to a ruled feature, re-record the ceiling with `--raise params=…` and write the reason in the patch.

Retesting a patch meanwhile passes or fails on noise.
