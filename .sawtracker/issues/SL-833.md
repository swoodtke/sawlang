**User ruling (Oct 30): the module is `std.serialize`, not `std.serde`.**

`Serialize`, `Deserialize`, `Encoder`, `Decoder`, `EncodeError`/`DecodeError` and their fault enums live in `std.serialize`. It is the format-neutral layer `std.json` and `std.cbor` (SL-938) both implement. "serde" was an abbreviation the no-abbreviations rule argues against, and it didn't explain itself to readers.

The port renames every mention of `std.serde`:
- `std/json.saw`'s doc comments
- LANGUAGE_SPEC.md's Serialization section
- the saw-lang skill
- `std/CBOR.md`/`std/JSON.md`
- any corpus program or resolver reservation that names it

The p833 survey lists the exact sites. Still open in this issue: whether the prelude exposes all four traits or only `Serialize`/`Deserialize`.
