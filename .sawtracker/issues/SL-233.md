## Description

Follow-up from the compile-performance work on SL-230; filed so it is not
lost. Backlog, no implementation order assigned.

## The shape

`Namespace` answers "find this name in me or in anything I import,
transitively" three times, once per table:

* `_lookup_struct_deep` (sawc/namespace.py)
* `_lookup_type_alias_deep`
* `_lookup_enum_deep`

They are the same walk over the same `imported_search_namespaces()` iteration,
differing only in which per-namespace lookup they call
(`lookup_struct` / `lookup_type_alias` / `lookup_enum`). The triplication
predates SL-230; that patch added a `_seen` dedup set and had to add the same
five lines to each of the three.

Brief obligation 1 is the reason to write this down: a rule quantifying over
"every position where X appears" is either a funnel with named entry points or
a matrix its tests cover row by row. Three hand-copied walks are neither, and
the failure mode is exactly what SL-230 ran into — a correctness-relevant
change (cycle termination, dedup) has to be applied three times and can be
applied twice.

## Why it was NOT folded in SL-230

Measurement, not preference. This is the hottest iteration in the type
checker: on a six-module app `imported_search_namespaces` yields ~40 M times
and `_lookup_type_alias_deep` was entered ~20 M times before the dedup. A
shared `_lookup_deep(table_lookup, name, _seen)` helper adds a Python call
frame per visit on that path, and SL-230 was a performance patch — folding a
structure change with an unmeasured cost into it would have made its A/B
numbers mean something else. It is a separate judgement.

## What a fix would have to establish

1. One walk, with a docstring naming its three entry points.
2. An A/B showing the funnel does not give back a meaningful part of SL-230's
   measured -5.7% on a module-heavy program (the sawtracker server is the
   workload that exercises it; a shallow program such as `devtools/irdet` shows
   no difference either way and cannot police this).
3. `irdet --all`, since this is an iteration order.

Bound-method dispatch or passing the table dict rather than a closure may make
the frame cost negligible; that is the thing to measure, not assume.

Related: SL-230 (the perf work that added the dedup), SL-232 (the separate,
much larger back-end finding).
