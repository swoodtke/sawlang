**SL-565.p1 submitted (A8c, one commit; agent p565, rebased by the lead onto main with SL-571 and SL-569).**

**Conformance first:** K14–K20, K135 and R25 already named their covering tests. K14/15/16/17/20 gain the capture-spelling tests in both INDEX.md copies, and CONFORMANCE.md's R24/R25 move from `slice.not-yet` to U6d2.

**The funnel:** `spawn_loans` in `compiler/borrowck/src/loans.saw` makes a new `Spawn` loan kind. Entry points: `argument` (the `ref` loan a root operand carries) and `capture` (a closure operand's capture loans).
- The loan is made at the spawn and charges the borrowed place's root.
- It is released after a `join` of a binding carrying the handle, or after the group's drop, path by path (`step_forward`).
- Typecheck's `slice.not-yet` is removed.

**Refusals (Stage 0's wording, existing rule IDs):**
- access inside the extent: `loan.read-while-exclusive` / `write-while-shared` / `exclusive-twice`;
- `loan.move-while-borrowed`;
- declared after the group: `loan.drop-while-borrowed`;
- loop body: `loan.exclusive-twice`.

**Reversible decisions (the lead accepts both):**
- **Stricter than Stage 0:** a spawn borrow still held at a `return` into a caller's `&TaskGroup` is refused as `loan.handle-escape`. Stage 0 compiles it, and it is a use-after-free once the function returns. Recorded as `refuses stricter` in `loan_differential.tsv`, with a Stage 0 record.
- **More permissive than Stage 0:** a chained `group.spawn(f(&var n)).join()` releases the borrow. Stage 0 recognises a join only on a named binding.
