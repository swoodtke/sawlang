**Refusals in the corpus, per SL-710's re-measured baseline:**
- `mono.not-yet` "the builtin `swap` is not in the backend slice yet": `array_swap_builtin`, `array_swap_const_oob_error`, `array_swap_dynamic_oob_panic`. `a.swap(i, j)` on `[T; N]` (spec, design 72: bounds-checked, receiver a `var`).
- `mono.not-yet` "the builtin `copy` is not in the backend slice yet": `array_explicit_copy_dotcopy`, `conformance/V44_bounded_wrapper_copy_runs`, `optional_explicit_copy_deep`. A spelled `.copy()` on a wrapper with no declared hook (a fixed array, an Optional, a tuple), which copies each part by its own `copy()` in order.
- `backend.not-yet` "the operator `==` on `equatable_array_field.Row`": `comparable_bits` and `equal_at` in `compiler/llvm/src/ops.saw` have no `TargetShape.Array` case, so a struct with a `[T; N]` member (spec: element-wise conjunction) is refused.

**Mechanism:** the builtins no declaration writes are admitted one by one in `mono_builtin_supported` (`compiler/mono/src/worklist.saw`) and lowered one by one in `LlEmitter.builtin` (`compiler/llvm/src/calls.saw`); `swap` and `copy` are the two left. `copy` on a wrapper is a call of the wrapper's copy glue (`build_copy_glue`, which covers arrays since SL-710, Optionals and tuples), its result the call's destination; `swap` is two bounds-checked element addresses (`check_index` in `compiler/llvm/src/places.saw`) and an exchange of their bits. The `==` part is one more shape in the comparison walk, each element compared by its own rule in index order.

**Pins:** the seven corpus programs to `pass` (or to a later refusal), run programs matching Stage 0 at -O0 and -O2, and the corpusdiff baseline.

**SL-734.p1: the builtin `swap` on `[T; N]` and the builtin `.copy()` on a wrapper. Summary for review.** (Its third part, `==` on a value holding an array, landed with SL-759.)
- **Admission:** `mono_builtin_supported` (`mono/src/worklist.saw`, entry point `resolve_call`) admits `swap` and `copy`. `need_builtin_copy` asks for the copy glue of the receiver's referent unless the copy is plain bits; it is added to `glue_of`'s entry points.
- **Lowering:** `LlEmitter.builtin` (`llvm/src/calls.saw`, entry point `call_term`).
  - `array_swap` checks `i` then `j` through `check_index`, then exchanges the two elements' bits through a temporary slot (no copy, no drop).
  - `builtin_copy` calls the copy glue into the call's destination, or copies bits.
  - `places.saw` makes `check_index` public and factors out `element_addr`, which indexing and `swap` share; indexing's emitted instructions are unchanged.
- **Matrix, matching Stage 0 at -O0 and -O2:** `swap` on `[Int; 4]`, `[String; 3]`, NoCopy elements, an array field through `&var self`, `i == j`, and a dynamic index out of range (exit -6; with both indices bad, `i` is reported); `.copy()` on arrays, Optionals, `None`, nested tuples and wrapper mixes. `leaks --atExit` finds 0 leaks.
- **Divergence, accepted:** `[R?; 2].copy()` and `[(R, Int); 2].copy()` run per design 139, where Stage 0 refuses them as not Copy. The probe `wrapper_copy_array_of_wrappers` states its own output.
- **Pins:** `array_swap`, `array_swap_out_of_range`, `wrapper_copy`, with darwin AND aarch64 records (the run lane is ok on both).
- **Corpus:** `array_swap_builtin`, `array_swap_dynamic_oob_panic`, `array_explicit_copy_dotcopy`, `conformance/V44_bounded_wrapper_copy_runs`, `optional_explicit_copy_deep` and `df151i_tuple_copy` → pass. `array_swap_const_oob_error` → **`wrong / accepts a refusal test`**, accepted by the lead: before this it was refused only because `swap` didn't lower, and the compile-time constant index check is SL-730's (noted there; precedent SL-711/SL-727).

Approved patch SL-734.p1 (revision 1)

**Air: SL-734.p1 r1 approved; arming it.** `swap` checks `i` then `j` through the shared `check_index` and exchanges the two elements' bits through a temporary, so there's no copy and no drop. `.copy()` calls the referent's copy glue, or copies bits for a plain-bits type. Indexing and `swap` share `element_addr`.

**Owning-type probe** (`air-review/sl734/owning.saw`; on e4bf209a0 = main + r1, both stages rebuilt): byte-identical to Stage 0 at -O0 and -O2.
- `swap` on `[String; 3]`, including `swap(1, 1)`;
- a nested `[[String; 2]; 2]`, swapping rows then inside a row;
- `[(Int, String); 2]`;
- `[String?; 2]` holding `None`;
- an array field through a `&var self` method;
- `.copy()` of `[String; 2]` then writing the copy (the original keeps `keep`);
- `.copy()` of a `(String, Int)?`.

Under Guard Malloc (`libgmalloc`) it exits 0 at both levels with identical output, and `leaks --atExit` finds 0 leaks at both.

**The accepted divergence is the spec's:** "an `Optional<T>`, a tuple, a fixed array … each take the strongest tier among the values they hold", and `.copy()` exists when the payload's tier provides one. So `[R?; 2].copy()` over an ExplicitCopy `R` is right, and Stage 0's refusal is Stage 0's gap. `array_swap_const_oob_error` → `wrong` is recorded and owned by SL-730 (c-note there), as the SL-711/SL-727 precedent allows.
