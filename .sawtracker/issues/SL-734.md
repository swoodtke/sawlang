**The backend still refuses the builtin `swap` and `copy` methods and `==` on a value holding a fixed array.** Siblings SL-710's sweep reached but did not take (SL-710 took the literals, fixed-array indexing with its bounds check, the builtin `len`, and a fixed array's drop and copy glue).

**Refusals in the corpus, per SL-710's re-measured baseline:**
- `mono.not-yet` "the builtin `swap` is not in the backend slice yet": `array_swap_builtin`, `array_swap_const_oob_error`, `array_swap_dynamic_oob_panic`. `a.swap(i, j)` on `[T; N]` (spec, design 72: bounds-checked, receiver a `var`).
- `mono.not-yet` "the builtin `copy` is not in the backend slice yet": `array_explicit_copy_dotcopy`, `conformance/V44_bounded_wrapper_copy_runs`, `optional_explicit_copy_deep`. A spelled `.copy()` on a wrapper with no declared hook (a fixed array, an Optional, a tuple), which copies each part by its own `copy()` in order.
- `backend.not-yet` "the operator `==` on `equatable_array_field.Row`": `comparable_bits` and `equal_at` in `compiler/llvm/src/ops.saw` have no `TargetShape.Array` case, so a struct with a `[T; N]` member (spec: element-wise conjunction) is refused.

**Mechanism:** the builtins no declaration writes are admitted one by one in `mono_builtin_supported` (`compiler/mono/src/worklist.saw`) and lowered one by one in `LlEmitter.builtin` (`compiler/llvm/src/calls.saw`); `swap` and `copy` are the two left. `copy` on a wrapper is a call of the wrapper's copy glue (`build_copy_glue`, which covers arrays since SL-710, Optionals and tuples), its result the call's destination; `swap` is two bounds-checked element addresses (`check_index` in `compiler/llvm/src/places.saw`) and an exchange of their bits. The `==` part is one more shape in the comparison walk, each element compared by its own rule in index order.

**Pins:** the seven corpus programs to `pass` (or to a later refusal), run programs matching Stage 0 at -O0 and -O2, and the corpusdiff baseline.
