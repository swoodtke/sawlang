**Found by:** u7skel and u7b (U7a, SL-505, merged as 7e04eaa0). **Area:** `compiler/mono/src/dump.saw`, and the summaries mono reads.

**The defect:** the mono dump prints the generic signature's conditional effect (`suspends when T...`) on calls already instantiated at a concrete type, where the condition is decided. It's harmless today, but coroutine lowering will read the effect per item, so the item must carry the resolved effect (suspends or not), not the conditional one.

**Fix:** mono substitutes the effect's condition at instantiation and the dump prints the decided effect. Pin it with a mono golden over a generic called at a suspending and a non-suspending type.
