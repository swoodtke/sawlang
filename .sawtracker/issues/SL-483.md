## Description

**sawc2's typecheck still accepts writes to heap storage reached from a `&self` receiver: design 200's carve-out, which SL:borrowing §9 retires.** SL-105 closed on Sep 25 as superseded by the §9 ruling (SL:tracker-cleanup t2, carried in SL-393), and SL-479.p1 specs the refusal ("`&self` is read-only all the way down"). Found by the Air (chat, re m410).

**Today:** M32 and M41 are pinned as accepted by `compiler/tests/typecheck/golden/mutability.saw`. `self.rows[0][0] += 100` and `self.rows[0].push(9)` in a `&self` body both typecheck.

**Owed:**
1. **Refuse a write, in a `&self` body, to any storage reached from `self`** through owned storage: a field's heap buffer, a `Vector` element, an exclusive accessor called on a field. Name the mechanism in `compiler/typecheck/src/mutability.saw` and route every write position through it (obligation 1). The position matrix:
   - assignment and compound assignment;
   - an `&var` argument;
   - a `&var self` method call;
   - an exclusive accessor;
   - a place write through a chain.
2. **Exception (D28):** a write through a raw pointer's pointee stays legal.
3. **Flip M28, M32 and M41 to the refusal,** annotated "language changed".
   - Any tests/corpus program that Stage 0 accepts and this now refuses goes in the differential, classed with a reason.
4. **Report every `&self` writer this catches in std (`--std-root std`) or `compiler/`.** Those are std changes, so stop and report them; don't fix them.

Typecheck. It's scheduled first in batch B2, after B1 lands (B1 owns `mutability.saw` now).

**SL-487** (f429f9ed). An assignment's use lands on the `BorrowPlace` node, and the writability funnel's place filter never admitted that node. So every statement-form write went unchecked:
- `borrow var` through a `-> &T` lend;
- any write through a bare `borrow`;
- `borrow var` on a `let` root or on a `&T` parameter.

The funnel now judges a written `BorrowPlace` by its inner place, and a write whose chain ends in a bare `borrow` is refused with a `borrow var` hint. Fixtures: `mutability.immutable.read-only-lend-statement*`, `.shared-borrow*` and `.borrow-statement*`. The differential `lend_accessor_local.saw` becomes `accepts owned`, because typecheck now refuses its write first.

**SL-483** (c650354b). Design 200's heap carve-out is removed: `TcCrossing.Heap`, `lends_indirectly` and its dead helpers.
- **Kept (D28):** writes to a raw pointer's pointee.
- **Newly accepted:** the walk stops at a place lent by a `&self` accessor, whose receiver is only read (§3). D28 needs this to work through an accessor.
- **Fixtures:** one per matrix position, the `-heap*` family plus `shared-capture-heap`, `shared-accessor-heap` and `exclusive-only-heap`.
- **Rows:** M28, M32, M41 and M33's heap form are flipped and annotated "language changed".

**SL-486** (1f3d44ad), D29 plus the member hops. `check_plain_subscript_reads` holds every plain subscript read to the getitem it is (`transfer.implicit-copy`, with a `borrow` fix-it). The exception is a projection that ends where a place is lent: a `borrow` form, `lend`, a `for` head, or a sigiled `&`.

- An element type naming a type parameter gets D27's inferred requirement.
- Fixed arrays and raw pointers are not getitems.
- Fixtures: `transfer.implicit-copy.getitem-*` (interpolation, message, format, assert, panic, comparison, field, method, chain, explicit, subscript, scrutinee) and `copy.requirement.getitem-hop`.

**SL-484** (1bc328b2). `refuse_sigil_mismatch` runs at every argument position, in both directions. `&var x` for a `&T` parameter gets a fix-it to `&x`. `&x` or `&var x` for a by-value parameter gets "drop the sigil", `.copy()` or `move`.
