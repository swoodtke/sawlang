**Found by:** u5b3 (SL-456 U5b3). **Area:** `compiler/tools/std_cone.py`, the cone walk.

**The defect:** the cone does not list conformance methods reached through a refining trait. `SystemError.format`, `SystemErrorKind.describe`, `Utf8Error.format` and the existing `AllocError.format` are all reached, through `Error` refining `Printable`, but none appear in the recorded cone. `std_cone_review.md` covers them by hand for now.

**Why it matters:** the cone is what the hazard review and the leak-tolerance condition are checked against, so a declaration missing from it escapes that review silently.

**Fix:** when a conformance to a refining trait is reached, the walk includes the conformance methods of every trait it refines. Sweep the other ways a method is reached without a written call: default bodies, operator sugar (`==` through `equals`), `Printable` through interpolation and `{}` format arguments, and `deinit` and `copy` hooks. Re-record both cone files, and put the before and after in the patch.

Deferred until SL-505 merges, because it rewrites cone files SL-505 also edits.
