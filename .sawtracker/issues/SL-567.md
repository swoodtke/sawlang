SL-567.p1 (A10) submitted. The change is in std only, with no compiler file.

- **The wake:** `park_wakes(cell)` in `std/taskgroup.saw` is the one test the sweep uses to decide what can run: a ready park, or any park of a cancelled task, whatever its reason. Before this, a cancelled task parked on a timer made the executor panic ("parked on a timer, which this executor does not wait on yet"). The timer, fd and channel executors still to come inherit the wake without a cancel path of their own.
- **One cancel write:** `request_cancel(cell)` serves `Task.cancel`, `VoidTask.cancel` and the background group's close. It writes only the cancel word, never the park word.
- **The outcome:** a park returns nothing. Its cancelled outcome is the cancel word, which the frame reads after the park through `__saw_cancel_requested()` (A9's read through `cx`). No context field was added.
- **R3, a cancel arriving between record and arm:** `park_wakes` reads the cancel word after the resume that parked has returned `Pending`, so the record and the arm both precede the read. A cancel arriving before, between or after them is seen at the next decision, and since a cancel never writes the park word, a later record can't overwrite it. Under `threads: N` the word must become atomic and a cross-thread cancel must rouse the idle wait (A17a).

**Pins:** five run programs, each matching Stage 0's records on macOS and Linux: `task_cancel_check`, `task_stale_handle_cancel`, `task_cancel_embedded`, `task_cancel_parked_ready`, and `task_cancel_during_sleep`, which panicked before this patch. They use `Task.spawn` because `group.spawn` waits on A8b.

**Moved pins**, recorded on their units: SL-568 (A11) `taskgroup_cancel_during_sleep`; SL-569 (A12a) a pipe-park cancel that withdraws its registration, plus the `net_*_cancel` programs; SL-571 (A13a) `taskgroup_cancel_receive` and `channel_recv_cancel`.

**Golden churn:** 11 coro records. With item numbers masked, the only new lines are the two new std items (`park_wakes`, `request_cancel`), both `(frame no)`. No `INVARIANT`.

Gate in the worktree, on current main: `run.py --only std,std_suspension,mono,coro,programs` ok, and `battery.sh citations` green.
