**Air: SL-887.p1 approved.** Reviewed r1 on 46e7a30d4.

**Determinism, the property that would regress quietly:**
- `sawc build --emit-ll` of one program from two absolute paths (`…/sl887p/a/dbg.saw` and `…/sl887p/deep/x/y/dbg.saw`) gives byte-identical IR (25,205 lines).
- No `/Users/` appears in the IR, the binary or the dSYM.
- Files are recorded relative: `dbg.saw` and `std/panic.saw`, under directory `.`.
- Two modules whose files share a name keep distinct `DIFile`s: `a/util.saw` and `b/util.saw` beside `main.saw`.

The lead's fixpoint result (Stage 2 = Stage 3, a path-free seed) covers the compiler side.

**Correctness of the DWARF** (`dbg.saw`: a panic two frames deep inside a coroutine):
- `xcrun dwarfdump --verify` on the dSYM: "No errors" at -O0 and -O2.
- `atos` on the address of `saw resume dbg.inner(n: Int)`, the coroutine's resume function, gives `dbg.saw:5`, its source function's line. The panic prints `panic at dbg.saw:8`.

**Cost.** `-O2` binaries grow about 2-3.5% with the default `-g`: 116,872 → 121,000 bytes for `dbg.saw` and 96,776 → 98,888 for a run probe, since the DWARF lives in the dSYM. Build time doesn't change measurably at this size. `-g0` emits no dSYM. The `sawc build` usage lists `[-g | -g0]`; the README/CLAUDE.md usage blocks are the lead's noted follow-up.

**Gates:**
- `run.py --only census,fixture_whitespace,parallel_order,parser,programs,resolver,seed,std,subset,units,golden,coro,sanitize` passes; `seed` because `seed.py` and `build.py` change.
- `battery.sh citations docverify` is green.
- `corpus.py` on the 13 `.saw` files accepts all 13.
