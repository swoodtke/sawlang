**From A10 (SL-567): the cancel-during-a-channel-wait pins move here.** SL:coroutines R3 asks for cancel during a channel wait. A10 builds the generic cancel wake: `park_wakes` in `std/taskgroup.saw` is the one predicate the sweep decides runnability by, a ready park or any park of a cancelled task, so a task parked on a readiness word (park word < -1) is resumed once it is cancelled. The pins need `std/channel.saw`, which is yours:
- **`taskgroup_cancel_receive`** and **`channel_recv_cancel`**: a consumer parked in `receive` on an empty channel is cancelled. Its `receive` answers `Err(Cancelled)` and dequeues nothing. Per the spec, `receive` checks the cancel word at its loop top, ahead of the queue read, through `cancelled()`/`__saw_cancel_requested()` (A9's read through `cx`). Both use `group.spawn` (A8b, SL-564), and a `Task.spawn` form pins the same thing until A8b lands.
- If the channel's own wake needs a predicate (the readiness word set), put it in `park_wakes` beside the cancel one, so the wake stays one funnel. Also withdraw a waiter's registration before its cell is freed (SL-556 c5).

**SL-571.p1 submitted (A13a; agent p571 then p571b, one commit).**

**Surface:**
- `std/channel.saw` (new module `std.channel`) holds `Channel<T: Send>`, a `Copy` handle with an atomic refcount and the runtime's one-word lock. It provides `init`, `send`, `close`, `try_receive` and `receive`, plus `ChannelError` (`Closed`, `Cancelled`, `Alloc(e:)`, `Printable` + `Error`), with `///` docs throughout.
- Left out: the blocking `recv` (the thread engine, out of scope) and Stage 0's transform-only helpers.
- Divergences from Stage 0: the runtime lock in place of a pthread mutex and condition variable, and an out-of-memory panic that reports the node's or block's own `sizeof`.

**Readiness:**
- `publish_ready` is the one writer of the invariant (`ready != 0` exactly when `count > 0` or `closed`). Its entry points are `close`, `enqueue` and `take`.
- `receive` checks `cancelled()` at the top of its loop, before reading the queue.

**Executor (`std/taskgroup.saw`):**
- `park_wakes` wakes a channel park once its readiness word is nonzero. Its entry points are `TaskGroup.runnable`, `should_stop` and `idle`.
- `waits_on` accepts a channel park.
- `idle` panics with an interim message naming A13b when only unwoken channel parks are left (the lead's ruling under the deferral rule; K67 waits on SL-572).
- A channel park registers nothing, so there is nothing to withdraw.

**Pins:**
- Five run programs matching Stage 0 (darwin + aarch64): `channel_recv_nested`, `channel_receive_binding_and_try_heads` (K47), `channel_receive_woken_by_send` (K40, replacing K63, which goes to A16b), `taskgroup_cancel_receive`, and `channel_recv_cancel` (a receive parked, then cancelled).
- The std-lane pair `channel_queue`: close-then-receive, `try_receive` on an empty and a full channel, and FIFO order with two producers.
- 19 equivalence rows.

**Corpus:** exactly the 50 `no module std.channel` rows move.
- 26 pass, and 2 refusal tests pass.
- The rest are recorded against their owners, each registered on the owner's acceptance checklist:
  - 14 at `threads:`: SL-578;

  - `coro_channel_receive_moves_element`: SL-764;
  - K67 `wrong`: SL-572.

**Other files:** the `sawc2.cone` growth (`ChannelError` and `AllocError.format`; the lead allowed it), gmgate's two blocked entries that now pass (61 programs, 0 failing), and the dropped `timer_park` channel row.
