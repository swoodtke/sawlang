## Description

SL-406.p4 adds three `defect` rows to GRAMMAR.md §16 for lexical diagnostics. The self-hosted lexer (`compiler/lex`) should follow §2.7 here; the frozen `sawc/lexer.py` and parser keep the recorded disagreement.

1. **`syntax.lex.unterminated-string`:** both lexers report it at the end of input. §2.7 wants the opening quote.
2. **`syntax.lex.unclosed-bracket`:** the frozen parser anchors `(` and `[` at their opener, but reports an unclosed `{` at the next function's `func`. §2.7 says an unclosed bracket is reported at its opener.
3. **`syntax.lex.float-point`:** the frozen parser refuses `.5` as "Unexpected token: DOT", with no hint.

**Fix:**
- compiler/lex reports each at the position and with the hint §2.7 gives.
- Update the golden token fixtures.
- Remove the three §16 rows once sawc2 follows §2.7.
- The parse corpus's cases for these drop their position today (SL-406.p4 revision). Restore it once the lexer is right, if the contract wants it.
