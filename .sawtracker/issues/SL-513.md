**Found by:** the Air, reviewing SL-505.p1 (U7a). Repros `f2_min.saw`, `d8_matchtime.saw` and `e9_matchconsume.saw` are in chat file f22 (m454). **Area:** MIR's match lowering, and a spec gap. **Needs a user ruling.**

**The divergence:**
- In a consumed `match`, `case Full(_) -> print("arm")` prints `arm` and then `drop 3` in sawc2, but `drop 3` and then `arm` in Stage 0.
- sawc2 also disagrees with itself: an arm that binds part of the payload drops its `_` parts at arm entry.
- On an owned `Noisy?`, sawc2 drops at the arm's end and Stage 0 at the scope's end. There, sawc2's behaviour reads like the spec's "match consumes an owned enum".

**The gap:** the spec doesn't say when a discarded (`_`) payload, or an unbound part of one, is dropped. The candidates are arm entry, arm end and scope end. A ruling is needed before MIR picks one; then the spec states it and both match paths follow it.
