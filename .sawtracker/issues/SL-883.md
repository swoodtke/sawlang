## Description

**An optional-chain member read (`a?.b`, `syntax.expr.optional-member`) is `slice.not-yet` in sawc's typecheck** (found by the lead's triage of the coroutine corpus, Oct 25).

It's one of the largest single gaps among the corpus's refusals: five programs are refused with `` `syntax.expr.optional-member` is not in the bootstrap slice yet ``:

- `coro_try_landing_within_guards.saw`
- `expr_suspend_optchain_hop.saw`
- `expr_suspend_optchain_multihop.saw`
- `import150_std_qualified_positions.saw`
- `optional_chain_suspend_method.saw`

The `?.` ASSIGNMENT is built (SL-670), but the read isn't.

**The feature:** design 111's full optional chaining. `a?.b?.c()` is arbitrary in length, including call heads and method hops. One short-circuit skips the rest of the postfix chain, its arguments included. Flattening never makes `U??`. The final field must be copyable. A suspending hop works (design 120). The spec and the saw-lang skill are the oracle.

**Obligation 1:** the chain's lowering is one funnel, with its entry points named. Cover these positions: a field hop, a method hop, a call head, a subscript hop, a suspending hop, a chain in an argument, in a condition, and in `??`, and a chain whose base is a place versus a temporary.

**Lead decisions on its 10 questions** (spec "Optionals" and GRAMMAR §13 are the oracle):
1. **`o?.xs[0]` / `o?.pair.0`:** GRAMMAR's `syntax.rule.optional-chain-run` closes the run at a subscript or tuple index, so it is `(o?.xs)[0]`, a subscript on an optional. Refuse it cleanly, with the text naming `if let` (or `o?.xs.get(0)` where that exists). No grammar change in this unit.
2. **A conditional-lend head with a method hop:** the short-circuit wins. The spec says ALL later evaluation is skipped, so no window opens on the absent path.
3. **Temporaries past the first `?`:** the spec's Temporaries rule (end of the full expression) applies, and a skipped hop makes no temporaries. If the two corpus programs' expected drop order disagrees with that, STOP and report; don't add a chain scope. One spec sentence states it.
4. **The final-field refusal:** reuse `transfer.implicit-copy`, with the spec's out ("end the chain in a method instead"). It must cover a temporary base (`mk()?.f`) too.
5. **Reads past a conditional lend:** no "bind the lend first" fence. A read writes nothing.
6. **A chain ending in a `borrows` accessor:** a PRECISE `slice.not-yet` naming that shape; filed as a follow-up, not built here.
7. **A discarded `Result?` from a chain:** refused as a discarded Result (never hide errors; design 151's rule extends through the chain).
8. **The assignment shares the hop funnel:** yes (obligation 1). The four typecheck goldens' `?.` lines may move; `optional_assigns.mir` must not.
9. **A consuming method hop:** follows `p!.m()`'s copy policy, except it never moves out of the place. Where `p!` would demand `move`, the chain is refused (the spec: chaining does not consume), with the text naming `if let` or `take()`.
10. **Scope:** all 20 rows.
