## Description

**An optional-chain member read (`a?.b`, `syntax.expr.optional-member`) is `slice.not-yet` in sawc's typecheck** (found by the lead's triage of the coroutine corpus, Oct 25).

It's one of the largest single gaps among the corpus's refusals: five programs are refused with `` `syntax.expr.optional-member` is not in the bootstrap slice yet ``:

- `coro_try_landing_within_guards.saw`
- `expr_suspend_optchain_hop.saw`
- `expr_suspend_optchain_multihop.saw`
- `import150_std_qualified_positions.saw`
- `optional_chain_suspend_method.saw`

The `?.` ASSIGNMENT is built (SL-670), but the read isn't.

**The feature:** design 111's full optional chaining. `a?.b?.c()` is arbitrary in length, including call heads and method hops. One short-circuit skips the rest of the postfix chain, its arguments included. Flattening never makes `U??`. The final field must be copyable. A suspending hop works (design 120). The spec and the saw-lang skill are the oracle.

**Obligation 1:** the chain's lowering is one funnel, with its entry points named. Cover these positions: a field hop, a method hop, a call head, a subscript hop, a suspending hop, a chain in an argument, in a condition, and in `??`, and a chain whose base is a place versus a temporary.
