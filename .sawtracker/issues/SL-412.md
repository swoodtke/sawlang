## Description

The reference recognizer does not model GRAMMAR.md §11's nesting-depth limit. It accepts 257 and 300 levels of parentheses, postfix hops and unary operators, each in under half a second.

**What that leaves uncovered:**
- design 259 R4's limit+1 refusal cannot be written as a negative case;
- SL-400 c6 Q21's call nesting has no case;
- only the accepting side of N1 (256 nested parentheses, and N1's other shapes) is pinned.

**Fix:**
- The recognizer counts nesting depth as §11 defines it and refuses at limit+1, naming the §11 rule.
- Add the limit+1 negatives for each shape §11 counts (parentheses, brackets, braces, closures, `if`s, hops, unary operators, calls), and Q21's call nesting.
- Require the refusing side in `parsecoverage`.

Comments on the brief before the agent gets far. Four points.

1. **Derive the recognizer's charges from §11's table, through extract.py (the one model).** Add a check that every §11 row has a charge in the recognizer, and that nothing charges which §11 doesn't list. Otherwise the two drift, as `syntax.decl.payload` did: sawc2 charged it one unit before §11 had the row. This is obligation 1's funnel-or-matrix, applied to the recognizer.
2. **Give every §11 row its limit+1 negative, not only the brief's eight shapes.** The table also counts types (ref, slice, array, tuple, paren, func), patterns, generic lists, const expressions, payloads, match/while/for/guard bodies and test groups. `compiler/tools/depth_funnel.py` already builds 256-accepted / 257th-refused texts for sawc2's cells. Building the recognizer's negatives from those same builders keeps the two lanes on the same texts.
3. **Three of §11's charges aren't plain nesting, and each needs its own pin:**
   - **Postfix chains.** A chain holds every hop's charge until it ends, so a flat chain of 257 hops is refused at its 257th hop (§11's second paragraph). A per-node depth count misses this.
   - **Interpolation.** A segment continues the string's depth (§2.5). The recognizer parses each segment on its own, so the enclosing depth has to be passed into that parse.
   - **Generic lists (D5).** A speculated generic list that the limit cuts short is refused as depth-limit, even where the comparison reading would win. The recognizer weighs every reading, so it has to apply the limit to the speculated list itself, not only to the tree the rules keep. It must also keep U4d's pre-scan: no speculation when no closing `>` comes before the enclosing bracket closes. So `f(x0 < x1, …, x256 < x257)` stays accepted, and it's worth a pin on each side.
4. **The refusal names the opener that would take the 257th level.** That is where sawc2 reports it. For `cases.py` the name is decided by the construct's own tokens, so it should settle whatever follows. Worth confirming on the hop-chain and generic-list shapes, whose "opener" is a `.` or a `<`.

SL-412.p1 is up: the recognizer models GRAMMAR.md §11's depth limit (brief, with c1). It has 2 commits and touches 13 files (+5823/−31). About 5,200 of those lines are case files and recorded expectations; the code is about 500 lines in `recognize.py` and 130 in the tests.

**Method** (`recognize.py`). The limit is checked after §13's rules choose the tree, on that tree, so it never chooses between readings.
- **Charges come from §11's table through `extract.py`,** as c1 point 1 asks.
  - `DEPTH_CHARGES` has one entry per §11 row.
  - "Held until" sets the release: the three chain phrases hold until the innermost chain ends.
  - `depth_charge_problems` checks both directions: a row with no charge, a charge with no row, an unknown alternative or release, and a chained link that isn't inside a chain. Five injections prove it.
- **Counting:** each charge is a span from its opener to its release, and a difference array gives each token's depth. The refusal is at the first opener past 256, named `syntax.rule.depth-limit`.
- **c1 point 3's three special charges each have pins:**
  - **postfix chains:** hops hold to the chain's end, so a flat chain of 257 hops is refused at hop 257;
  - **interpolation:** each segment starts at its string's depth, mapped back to file positions;
  - **D5:** for each `<` the tree reads as a comparison where U4d's pre-scan finds a closer, the list the parser would speculate is counted too. The pre-scan is built from the same `depth_terms` as sawc2's.
    - A list that parses is counted by its own tree.
    - A failed one is counted by a dynamic program over the Earley items on its prefix: the deepest reading, an upper bound.
    - The flat 257 pairs are accepted, and the balanced 257 lists refused.
    - Two new pins show a cut-short list refused even where the comparison would fit.
    - Switching off the speculation or the pre-scan flips these pins.

**Cases**
- **`negative/depth.saw`, 69 limit+1 cases:**
  - all 35 of `depth_funnel.py`'s refused cells, with the exact texts (c1 point 2);
  - 29 shapes the funnel lacks: tuple, repeat, Q21 nested calls, the call, subscript, tuple-index, optional-member and force chains, `not`, `~`, deref, ref, `&var`, move through `*` with hops, borrow places, payload, reference, array, one-element tuple and parenthesised types, generic arguments in a default, constant negation and groups, a segment, and the two D5 lists;
  - 5 where the named row itself takes level 257.
- **`golden/depth.saw`, 7 accepted pins:** the pre-scan, a 300-arm if-chain, a failed speculation giving its level back, Q21 at 256, and D5/segment at 256.
- **`parsecoverage`** requires the named depth-limit refusal, and a new "depth-row" item. Every §11 row needs a limit+1 case refused at its opener: 48 of 48.
- **`test_parse.check_depth_cells`** requires every short funnel cell to have a case with the same text at the same position (37 cells).
- **The `SL-400-c6-Q21` waiver is gone.** The new fixture `nesting_past_the_depth_limit.saw` is recorded as `c-conflict`, since the frozen parser has no limit.

**Parser against recognizer:** no disagreements. Every funnel cell and every extra shape is refused as depth-limit at the same L:C by both, checked directly against sawc2.
- **One grammar gap closed:** §11 didn't say where an inline module, a test group or a variant pattern takes its level. sawc2 takes it at the bracket, and one clarifying sentence in §11 now says so.

**grammarcorpus time:** 70 s before and 72 s after, warm.

**Follow-up (small):** `depth_funnel.py`, outside this unit's files, lacks the 29 extra shapes. Copying them from `negative/depth.saw` would put the parser lane on the same texts.
