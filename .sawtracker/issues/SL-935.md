Found by SL-865 U6c (p865c), the first riscv32 execution of mono's hash glue. On riscv32, an `Int64`/`UInt64` hash takes only its LOW 32 bits (the glue does `from(truncating:)` to the word), so every key sharing a low word collides: `value`, `value + 2^32` and `0x5678` all hash to `691574859` on riscv32, but to three different digests on arm64. Probe: `.build/scratch/p865/u6c/hash_probe.saw`.

The hash/== contract still holds, but this contradicts the prelude's stated property that a digest doesn't depend on the platform word, and it degrades a `Map<Int64, _>` or `Set<Int64>` on a 32-bit target.

The fix: mono's hash glue feeds a wider-than-word integer to the hasher as both halves (or as 8 bytes), identically on every target, so the digest matches across platforms. Pin: the probe's three values give the same three digests on riscv32 and arm64 (the freestanding suite's `wide_hash_format` case can assert them).
