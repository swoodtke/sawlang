**Freestanding builtins in Saw: a freestanding environment supplies only the `__saw_rt_*` seams.** USER RULING (Nov 2): option 2. std implements, in Saw, every non-seam routine sawc's freestanding output can reference, and sawc compiles them into the object for the target that needs them. Queued before the sawos re-sweep, after SL-940.

**Today** the environment supplies everything the unlinked object leaves undefined (LANGUAGE_SPEC.md, Profiles). The suite does it with `tests/freestanding/hal/support.c` and a vendored compiler-rt subset (SL-929). sawos does it by hand in `../sawos/rt/common_c/support.c` (`memset`, `memcpy`, `__udivdi3`, the four `__atomic_*_4`), with no soft-float routines and no `__divdi3`/`__moddi3`.

**The set** (the SL-944 survey, SL-929's census, the Air's SL-947 census):
- `memcpy`, `memset`, `memmove`: always.
- The 19 soft-float routines: on a target with no FPU (aarch64 at the freestanding default, riscv32 without F/D).
- The 5 word atomics (`__atomic_load_4` … `__atomic_compare_exchange_4`): riscv32 without `+a`.
- The 64-bit integer helpers on riscv32: `__divdi3 __moddi3 __udivdi3 __umoddi3` at every level, `__ashldi3 __ashrdi3 __lshrdi3` at `-Oz` (SL-947, a unit of this).
- (libm goes away with SL-944.)

**What the brief owes (a survey first):**
- How sawc gets the routines into the object only where they're needed: compiled from a std module per target, weak or not, `--gc-sections`-friendly, and what happens when the environment defines one too (sawos's support.c, a kernel's own `memcpy`).
- The self-call hazard: LLVM turns a copy loop into a `memcpy` call, so a Saw `memcpy` (and anything it calls) must not lower to a call to itself. The spelling (a no-builtin attribute on those functions, or a lowering rule) and its pin.
- The atomics without `+a`: a single-hart environment can use a plain load/store; a multi-hart one needs the environment's lock. Which are std's, and which stay a seam?
- The soft-float routines must be bit-exact with compiler-rt, including rounding, NaN results and the comparison return values; compiler-rt's own test vectors as the oracle. The aarch64 soft-float ABI (`-mabi=aapcs-soft`: `double` in general registers) for routines sawc defines.
- The contract rewrite: the spec's Profiles section lists only the seams as the environment's; the runner's `HAL_LIBCALLS`, `SOFT_FLOAT_ROUTINES` and the vendored compiler-rt go, and the symbol check refuses any non-seam undefined name.
- A consumer sweep (obligation 2): the suite's HAL, sawos's support.c and its arm64 HAL's soft-float debt.
