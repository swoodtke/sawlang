**A1. One capture lowering, and one "is it a reference" predicate** (SL-606, bucket A). Findings: Borrowck 1 (a use-after-free from safe code), Typecheck-body 1 and 1b, and M2.

- **Mechanism:**
  - MIR lowers an explicit `[&var v]`/`[&v]` capture of a REFERENCE PARAMETER as a reference to the reference. Borrowck's overlap rule then reads it as naming the reference, not its referent, so no loan check sees it. `self` and an implicit capture are lowered as reborrows and are checked.
  - Separately, typecheck asks "is this a reference?" with `kind == Ref` alone at a dozen sites (`add_capture`, `referent()`, `closure_result_value`, `open_return`, `ref_expr`'s reborrow, `adjust.saw`'s `convert`, `infer.saw`), so a `&[T]` slice is never a borrow.
  - `add_capture` also tests the `[move]`/`[copy]` branches before the frame-borrow test.
- **Fix:**
  - one capture lowering: an explicit capture of a reference is a reborrow of its referent, the way `self` is;
  - one predicate covering Ref and Slice, used at every site;
  - a capture word never skips the frame-borrow or move-from-borrow rules.
- **Pins first** (obligation 3): conformance rows for an explicit capture of a reference parameter under a window, a slice capture in an escaping closure, and `[move r]`/`[copy r]` of a reference. Use the review's probes (`review_bc_capture_*`, `review_tcsig_slice*`, `review_tcbody_capture_*`) as fixtures, each refused.
- **Sweep:** list every `kind == Ref`/`kind_of(t) == Ref` site in `compiler/typecheck` and `compiler/borrowck`, and route each through the predicate, or record why a slice can't reach it.
- **Done when:** every probe is refused with the right rule id, the matching local-root and implicit-capture controls are unchanged, and borrowck agrees with Stage 0 wherever Stage 0 can parse the form.

**Submitted as SL-607.p1** (a1cap, commit 65312296a, rebased). All four findings reproduced on main, and each is fixed by mechanism. Gate: `battery.sh compiler citations` green in 281s.
- **The use-after-free:** an explicit `[&r]`/`[&var r]` capture of a reference, slices included, lowers as a reborrow of its referent, as `self` is, so the loan checks see it. The probes are refused with `loan.window-root`, `loan.exclusive-twice` or `loan.closure-carrier`.
- **`[move r]`/`[copy r]`:** a reference is a frame borrow under every capture word. `[move x]` shares `move_from_borrow_reason` with `move x`.
- **One predicate,** `is_reference` (Ref or Slice), in `typeops.saw`, with its entry points named. The sweep's matrix lists the sites left on Ref alone, each with its reason.
- **Pins:** conformance rows M45, R45–R47, X46–X48 and V124; 8 borrowck and 9 typecheck refusal fixtures; goldens `borrowck/golden/slices.saw` and `mir/golden/reborrow_captures.saw`; and the run program `run/capture_reference.saw`, checked against Stage 0.

**Reversible decisions, for the Air's review:**
1. A slice is forwarded only with its sigil: bare `total(s)` is refused, and `&s`/`&var s` reborrow. The bare form was a copy with no loan, so `two(s, s)` aliased two exclusive references. This is a behaviour change, and the gate's corpus and std are clean under it.
2. `[&v]` of a `&var` reference narrows it to shared, as `[&self]` does.
3. `[copy r]` copies the reference, as Stage 0 does.
4. No slice twin in `convert`'s Shared step.

**Follow-ups, by the lead:**
- Stage 0's acceptance of an escaping `[copy r]`, whose environment points at the dead frame, goes in SL:hazards.
- A spec sentence on capturing reference parameters, and on a slice needing its sigil to forward, goes in SL-606's docs bucket.
