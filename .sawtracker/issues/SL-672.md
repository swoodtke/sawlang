## Description

**Port `Mutex`, `Once` and `SpinLock` to the new std (`std/`), each declaring `ZeroInit`.**

SL-660 added `ZeroInit` so a type whose fields are private can still be zero-initialized in a bare static, the freestanding idiom (`static LOCK: SpinLock<Counters>`, `static LIMITS: Once<Limits>`, `static REGISTRY: Mutex<Int>`). None of the three is in sawc2's `std/`. SL-596 c1 keeps them out "until they're scheduled", so SL-660 pinned its rule with test-local types. Under Stage 0's std root, sawc2 refuses the corpus's lock and once statics (`static.zero-hidden`, SL:hazards L30). Twelve `tests/corpus/` programs wait on this port:
- `spinlock_basic`, `spinlock_contention_mt`, `spinlock_no_hidden_alloc`, `errors/spinlock_needs_atomics`, `errors/spinlock_suspending_body`
- `mutex_static_inline`, `mutex_static_contention_mt`
- `once_static_set_get`, `once_cross_thread_publish`, `once_double_set_panic`, `once_get_before_set_panic`

**Owed:**
- the three types in `std/`, with their spec sections' API (`SpinLock`'s `sync`-enforced body, `Mutex`'s lock blocks, `Once`'s set/get) and an `extension T: ZeroInit {}` each, conditional on the payload where the spec's tier 1 says so;
- std-lane pins, and the twelve corpus programs moving in corpusdiff's baseline;
- a check against `Atomic` and the freestanding build, as the spec's tier 1 lists them.

**Depends on:** SL-660. **Relates to:** SL-596 (the std port epic) and the coroutine plan's channel and mutex units, whose cooperative halves may share code.
