**Bump the seed to 7b1054ce9** (main after SL-933 and SL-942), so the tree's std cone can import modules the old pin (9c520edd3, Oct 23) doesn't know.

SL-833's third patch needs this: std.json importing std.serialize (compiler/README.md, "The seed": the two-patch rule). SL-924 waits on it too.

**The patch:**
- `compiler/seed/manifest.tsv` and `compiler/seed/pin`: written by `seed.py write` for arm64-apple-darwin, and by `tools/linux.py seed` for aarch64- and x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu, all at 7b1054ce9. 46 files, uploaded to the blob host.
- `build.SEED_RUNTIME_MANIFEST` becomes `sawc-runtime.txt`: the new pin's driver reads its runtime manifest under that name (the sawc2 → sawc rename), and `seed.py check` refused the bump until it changed, as that constant's comment says.

- `build.seed_key` covers `SEED_RUNTIME_MANIFEST`: a `seed.py check` run before the constant changed had cached the new seed under the old name with the same key, and the next build couldn't find its manifest.

**Validated:** `seed.py check` passes (path-free, reproduced, and the runtime manifest name), and `build.py` builds Stage 1 and Stage 2 from the new seed.
