For part 2 (Air): the fixpoint lane's input list should include all of `sawc/`, not just `sawc/rt/`. Per its own docstring, the lane compares STAGE 1 with Stage 2: the driver's `.ll`, plus every sawc2 invocation the run.py lanes make, through `twin.py`. Stage 1 is what Stage 0 (`sawc/`: its typechecker, its codegen, `sawc/std`) makes of `compiler/`, so any `sawc/` change can move Stage 1's behaviour and break the agreement or the fixpoint. That's how S29 reached Stage 1 (`cand_param_count`), and how D43's `sawc/std/env.saw` addition enters Stage 1. Such patches are rare now that `sawc/` is frozen, but a `sawc/`-only patch, a rewrite-blocker std addition or a hazard-driven fix, is exactly the case that would skip the lane able to see its effect. `SUITE_INPUTS` already triggers on `sawc/`, so the extra cost lands only on those patches. Suggested dry-run matrix rows: a `sawc/std/env.saw`-only diff and a `sawc/codegen/`-only diff both select fixpoint; a `designs/`-only diff selects neither.
