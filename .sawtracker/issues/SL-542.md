**Found Oct 12 (SL-508.p1's blank "Tests failed"; sawtracker's ST-81).** `./build.sh test`, the per-patch gate, waits on the machine-wide suite lock (`/private/tmp/claude-<uid>/saw-suite-lock`) silently. SL-508.p1's server run spent 4h11m in that wait behind a sawlang agent's long local gate, printed nothing, and was then killed by a production restart, so its record shows an empty output. sawtracker can't see our lock (the sawtracker lead, m474), so the gate itself must say what it is doing.

**Wanted (in `build.sh` and `tools/patch_gate.py`):**
- when the lock is held at entry, print one line saying so: who holds it if knowable (the holder's pid or command, written into the lock directory), and since when;
- print a heartbeat line every N minutes while waiting, and the total wait once it is acquired;
- the gate summary records the lock-wait time separately from the test time.

Pins: a scripted holder plus a gate run, asserting the lines appear. A holder record means the suite-lock convention in CLAUDE.md gains "write a holder file into the lock dir"; update CLAUDE.md's suite-lock paragraph to match.
