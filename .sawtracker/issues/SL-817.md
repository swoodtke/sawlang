## Description

**Port Float's methods and its text conversions to the new std** (design 253: shortest round-trip formatting and correctly-rounded parsing). The new std has no Float surface. SL-813 (the `std.json` port) needs it for `JsonValue.Float`, in both directions. Part of SL-596's float port.

**What is refused today (corpus_diff.tsv):**
- `float_methods` is refused with `member.unknown` (`Float` has no `abs`);
- `float_text_format` is refused with `call.no-match` (no `StringBuilder.append` for a `Float`);
- `float_text_parse_vectors` and `float_text_shortest_vectors` are refused with `type.try`. Their file reading still names `IoError`, so they need migrating, as the SL-768 rows were.

**The reference** is Stage 0's `sawc/std/float.saw`, the design-253 implementation, ported to the new std's idiom; it is not copied:
- `Printable.format(into:)`, not `to_string`;
- `Result<_, AllocError>` for allocation;
- the subset checker's rules.

**The oracles:**
- `tests/float_vectors/shortest.tsv` (2897 rows) and `parse.tsv`, both generated from CPython and checked by the `floatvectors` lane. Byte equality, never a tolerance.
- The four corpus rows' own directives.

**The surface:**
- the methods `float_methods` uses;
- `to_bits`/`from(bits:)`, `is_nan`/`is_finite`/`is_infinite`;
- `Float: Printable` (shortest round-trip);
- `StringBuilder.append(value: Float)`;
- `String.to_float() -> Float?`;
- whatever the four rows need.
