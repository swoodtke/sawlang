## Description

**esp-clang dies compiling sawc's output for a windowed-Xtensa freestanding package: "Cannot scavenge register without an emergency spill slot".** Reported by the sawos lead (chat m1272, Nov 17), building SO-137 on the ESP32-S3: esp-clang 20.1.1 (`esp-20.1.1_20250829`) crashes on sawc's IR for a generic function `refused<V>` instantiated at `UInt8`, in a freestanding `+windowed` package (sawos `tests/uart-text-client`, SO-137.p1 revision 1). Splitting it into two non-generic functions avoids it. Source requested from sawos (to attach here).

**Leads to check (UNVERIFIED):** LLVM's register scavenger needs an emergency spill slot when a frame's offsets exceed what an instruction's immediate reaches; on windowed Xtensa sawc keeps no frame pointer (SL-1133), and a frame over 32,760 bytes already behaves differently (SL-1146). So: the function's frame size, whether sawc's IR or attributes for windowed Xtensa leave the backend no room to reserve the slot (`frame-pointer`, stack realignment, a large alloca), and whether esp-clang's windowed backend reserves one at all (an upstream bug to report, with a minimal `.ll`). Steps: reproduce with `sawc --emit-ll` + `--target xtensa-esp-unknown-elf --target-features +windowed` and Espressif's clang; reduce to a minimal `.ll`; decide sawc-side (what in our IR provokes it, and a fix) or esp-clang-side (report upstream; a sawc workaround only by the doctrine's rules); a freestanding suite case for the windowed row holding the fix.

Related: SL-1133, SL-1146, SO-137.
