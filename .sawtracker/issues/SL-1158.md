## Description

**esp-clang dies compiling sawc's output for a windowed-Xtensa freestanding package: "Cannot scavenge register without an emergency spill slot".** Reported by the sawos lead (chat m1272, Nov 17), building SO-137 on the ESP32-S3: esp-clang 20.1.1 (`esp-20.1.1_20250829`) crashes on sawc's IR for a generic function `refused<V>` instantiated at `UInt8`, in a freestanding `+windowed` package (sawos `tests/uart-text-client`, SO-137.p1 revision 1). Splitting it into two non-generic functions avoids it. Source requested from sawos (to attach here).

**Leads to check (UNVERIFIED):** LLVM's register scavenger needs an emergency spill slot when a frame's offsets exceed what an instruction's immediate reaches; on windowed Xtensa sawc keeps no frame pointer (SL-1133), and a frame over 32,760 bytes already behaves differently (SL-1146). So: the function's frame size, whether sawc's IR or attributes for windowed Xtensa leave the backend no room to reserve the slot (`frame-pointer`, stack realignment, a large alloca), and whether esp-clang's windowed backend reserves one at all (an upstream bug to report, with a minimal `.ll`). Steps: reproduce with `sawc --emit-ll` + `--target xtensa-esp-unknown-elf --target-features +windowed` and Espressif's clang; reduce to a minimal `.ll`; decide sawc-side (what in our IR provokes it, and a fix) or esp-clang-side (report upstream; a sawc workaround only by the doctrine's rules); a freestanding suite case for the windowed row holding the fix.

Related: SL-1133, SL-1146, SO-137.

**The sweep (Nov 18; evidence `.build/scratch/s1158/` in the lead's checkout; every tool run one at a time, peak 161 MB):**
- **The build is call0, not windowed.** sawc's `target_effective_features` puts `-windowed,-fp,-loop,-mac16,-bool` first for xtensa, and the package asks for call0. With only `-windowed` changed to `+windowed`, the module compiles at every level. As emitted, it crashes at `-O1`, `-O2`, `-Os` and `-Oz`, and compiles at `-O0`.
- **An esp-clang 20.1.1 call0 backend defect.** A 31-line plain `.ll` with no sawc names (`minimal.ll`) dies in Prologue/Epilogue Insertion with "Cannot scavenge register without an emergency spill slot".
  - The window, measured: a call0 frame of about 1056 to 2048 bytes crashes when every allocatable register is live at a store to a slot past `s32i`'s 1020-byte reach.
  - Below 1024, the store reaches. At 2048 and above, the backend reserves its emergency slot. In between it does neither. Why 2048 is UNVERIFIED (the backend source was not read).
- **sawc supplies the pressure.**
  - A by-address aggregate parameter is emitted as a bare `ptr` with no `align`, and its prelude `llvm.memcpy` has none either. call0 has no unaligned load, so every read of it is expanded into byte loads with shifts and ors, and every register is live at once.
  - Either change alone makes the whole 1.28 MB module compile at `-Oz`: `align 4` on those parameters, or on the memcpys.
  - The frame is large because sawc emits no `llvm.lifetime.*`: each `print` of a Printable has its own 512-byte StringBuilder buffer, and three never share space.
- **Why splitting `refused<V>` avoided it:** the split shrank each frame and lowered the pressure. Generic or not made no difference; the `UInt8` and `Void` instances have byte-identical bodies, and both crash.

**Lead's ruling (Nov 18):**
1. **Fix in sawc: a by-address aggregate parameter carries its type's alignment.**
   - Emit `align A` (the type's ABI alignment from the target layout) on the parameter, and give the prelude memcpy the same alignment on both sides.
   - This is correct information, not a workaround. Every caller passes the address of a slot, field or allocation of that type, which the layout aligns, so the attribute states what sawc already guarantees.
   - The implementer sweeps every position that hands such a pointer and proves it: a call's argument slot, an `@export`ed function called from C, a closure or coroutine frame field, a `borrows` lend, and a vtable or `any Trait` thunk. A position that cannot guarantee the alignment is a STOP.
   - Applies to every target.
   - Test: a freestanding suite case on the xtensa call0 row that crashed esp-clang before the fix, plus an IR golden of the attribute.
2. **Lifetime markers are filed separately.** Scoped slots marked with `llvm.lifetime.start`/`end` would let the 512-byte print buffers share stack space. That is a frame-size improvement with measured evidence (a 1696-byte frame from three buffers), and the S3's stack is small. Not this patch.
3. **The esp-clang defect is reported upstream with `minimal.ll`,** once the owner approves posting it to Espressif's tracker. Until it is fixed, a call0 function whose frame is in the window can still hit it from some other pressure source. The windowed ABI does not hit it.
4. **Retitled:** call0, not windowed.

**Lead's note with p1 (Nov 18), as ruled** (`briefs/q1158_sweep_ruling.md`, the sweep comment above; the owner ruled no upstream report). Evidence: `.build/scratch/p1158/` in the lead's checkout.
- **The change** (`compiler/llvm/src/bodies.saw`, `emit_function`'s by-address aggregate branch): the parameter is `ptr align A %pN`, where A is the type's layout alignment (`l.align`, never a constant). The entry memcpy is `ptr align <slot_align> %slot, ptr align A %pN`. The erased `&any Trait` fat reference is such an aggregate: `align 8` on the host, `align 4` on 32-bit targets. `ll_param_attributes`' doc states the rule.
- **The soundness sweep** (host `--emit-ll`, probes `sweep.saw` and `sweep2.saw`). Every position hands storage laid out for the type:
  - a local or temporary (an alloca at the layout's alignment; a constant operand is first written to such a slot);
  - a struct field (offsets rounded to the member's alignment, the base at the maximum);
  - a Vector element (copied to a slot first; the buffer comes from `alloc(n*size, alignof<T>)`);
  - an enum payload;
  - a closure's capture (copied out to a slot);
  - a coroutine frame field and a `Task.spawn` argument (`coro_align_up` per value, the frame allocated at its alignment);
  - an `any Trait` thunk (forwards the caller's slot), a generic instance, and a static.
  
  Positions that never reach the branch: `@export` (an aggregate by value is refused, `export.c-abi`), and a `&T`/`&var T`/`borrows` lend (a scalar `ptr`). The residual risk is a dereference by value through a misaligned user `UnsafePointer<T>`, which was already undefined behaviour (sawc's loads through it carry the natural alignment).
- **Tests:**
  - An IR golden `compiler/tests/run/param_align.defines` (run lane's `check_param_align`): `ptr align A` and the memcpy on arm64-apple-darwin and riscv32, for alignments 8, 2 and the word.
  - `hello.ll` and `noalias.defines` are re-pinned; only the parameter and memcpy attributes moved.
  - Position programs `param_align_positions` and `param_align_frame_positions`, run at `-O0` and `-O2`.
  - A freestanding case on the xtensa call0 row at `-Oz`, `param_alignment_frame.saw`: the `refused<V>` shape with three 512-byte print buffers, a frame of about 1.7 KB. It is red on the base compiler (esp-clang: "Cannot scavenge register without an emergency spill slot") and green on the change.
- **Gate:** run.py `seed,units,golden,subset,fixture_whitespace,std,comments,programs` green; `tools/battery.sh citations freestanding` green (210 passed); lint ok.
- **For the sawos side:** the hosted allocator relies on malloc's 16 bytes (the existing `runtime/ABI.md` contract). A freestanding runtime's allocator must honour `align` for the frame and Vector allocations this relies on.

Approved patch SL-1158.p1 (revision 1)

**Approved. The crash is fixed and pinned on the call0 row, each half of the fix is pinned by the golden, and every source of a by-address aggregate I probed meets the alignment the parameter now states** (f395).

- **The crash:** here, with Espressif's clang and QEMU, `param_alignment_frame` on the xtensa call0 row is green on the patch. With both halves dropped, which is the base compiler's IR, esp-clang dies with "Cannot scavenge register without an emergency spill slot".
- **Each half alone:** dropping only the parameter's `align` or only the memcpy source's passes the case, as the sweep found (either half suffices for the backend). Each one changes a line `param_align.defines` holds on both triples, so the golden pins it.
- **The claim holds where I looked:** in riscv32 IR, every static is emitted `align 8` and handed over as the global itself, a static array's element sits at a stride of 16, a static struct's field at offset 8, and an enum or optional payload goes through a slot. No type's alignment exceeds 8: every primitive's layout is at most 8 bytes, and `@align` applies to storage, never to a type. So `malloc`'s 16 and sosrt's granule cover every heap source. SL-1167 concerns a program's own `alloc(size, 64)`, not this attribute.
- **The re-pinned `hello.ll` and `noalias.defines`** move only by the parameter and memcpy alignment.
