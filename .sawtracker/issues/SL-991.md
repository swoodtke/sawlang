## Description

**Ruled (user, Nov 6): the seed is bumped for every non-trivial release, meaning any release that adds or changes a feature.**
- Such a release is followed by a seed bump pinned to the release's merge commit. A release of fixes alone may skip it.
- The bump is the patch right after the release merges, since a pin must name a commit main holds. The seed is then always a released compiler.
- The as-needed bump stays: whenever `compiler/` or `std/` needs something the seed doesn't compile.

**Why:** with as-needed bumps only, a landed feature waits, unusable by the compiler and std, until something forces a bump. Nov 6: SL-966 unit 2b, moving SL-971's compiler loops onto `append(other:)`, and `Vector.sort` delegating to `slice_sort` all waited on one.

**Change:** `compiler/README.md`'s "Bumping the seed" states the cadence.
