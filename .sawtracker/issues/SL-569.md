**From A10 (SL-567): the cancel-during-io pin moves here.** SL:coroutines R3 asks for "cancel during sleep, io and a channel wait". A10 builds the generic cancel wake: `park_wakes` in `std/taskgroup.saw` is the one predicate the sweep decides runnability by, a ready park or any park of a cancelled task. It reads the cancel word after the resume that parked has returned, so a cancel before the record, between the record and the arm, or after both is seen. The io half can't run before your executor half lands: the descriptor arm panics, naming you.
- **Owed by A12a:** a std-lane program in which a task parked on a pipe's read end is cancelled while nothing ever writes. It must resume promptly, `io_wait` must report the cancel (the cancel word read after the park, through `__saw_cancel_requested()`, ahead of any timeout), and the registration must be withdrawn on that exit (the Air's SL-556 c5 obligation). After it, a second task that parks on the same fd must not be woken by the stale token.
- **Owed by A12b (SL-570), named here so the hand-off is written down:** the corpus programs `net_io_cancel`, `net_cancel_parked_read`, `net_cancel_precise`, `net_cancel_unregisters_token` and `conformance/K93_timed_park_observes_cancellation`. Most of them also need `group.spawn` (A8b) and `cancel_addr()` (A17a).
- Put your wake conditions (the reactor latched the word, a bounded park's deadline expired) in `park_wakes`, beside the cancel one.
