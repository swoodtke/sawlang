**A design point to confirm while you're here.** `io_wait` reports cancelled first, through `__saw_cancel_requested()` after the park. A frame can't read its park word after it resumes, since the executor writes `PARK_UNSET` before every resume. So without a compiler change, the only std-side signal for "timed out" is the clock: a bounded park reports TimedOut when its deadline has passed at resume, else Ready. A readiness that lands in the same instant as the deadline therefore reports TimedOut, and the caller's retried operation still finds the data, as Stage 0's net layer retries the syscall before it honours the deadline. The latched word reads as `0`, which is `PARK_READY`, so `park_wakes`'s readiness arm is the existing ready test, plus a new arm for a bounded fd park whose deadline has passed.
