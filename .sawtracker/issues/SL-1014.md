Found by the SL-1011 spec review (F2).

For `p?.finish()` on a `consumes` method, the hint and LANGUAGE_SPEC "Optionals" both recommend `if let x = move p { x.finish() }`. That form is refused with consumes.move: "`finish` consumes its receiver, so the transfer is spelled at the call: write `(move x).finish(...)`".

So the hint's own suggestion fails. The spec text is being corrected to `(move x).finish()`. The diagnostic's hint should say the same.

The probes are `/Users/shawn/Projects/sawlang/.build/scratch/p1000e/F05_iflet_move_consumes.saw` and `/Users/shawn/Projects/sawlang/.build/scratch/p1000e/F03_chain_refusals.saw`.
