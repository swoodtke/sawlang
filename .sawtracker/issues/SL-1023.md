Found by the SL-1021 spec review (F5 and F7). LANGUAGE_SPEC "Subscripts: getitem, setitem and the place" says the position picks the role.

- **F5:** with a getitem `func []` and a setitem, and no place accessor, `print(o[1])` and `"{m[1, 0]}"` are refused with subscript.role ("used as a place accessor"), while `let x = o[1]` works. With the spec's Matrix family (a getitem and a `borrows` place declared together), `print(m[1, 0])` is still refused. The probes are `/Users/shawn/Projects/sawlang/.build/scratch/p1000g/U6_print_getitem.saw`, `U2_subscript_fam.saw` and `U4_subscript_get_place.saw`.
- **F7:** a plain `at(&self, i) -> Int` beside `at(&var self, i) borrows -> &var Int`: `b.at(1) = 5`, `b.at(0) += 1` and `borrow var e = b.at(0) { … }` give type.not-a-place. With both receivers `&var self` the pair works. The probes are `/Users/shawn/Projects/sawlang/.build/scratch/p1000g/Z2_plain_and_borrows.saw` and `Z2b_plain_and_borrows.saw`.

Rendering and comparison are value reads when a plain role exists. A pair that differs only in receiver is the spec's own shared and exclusive pattern.
