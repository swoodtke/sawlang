## Description

Found by the SL-345 implementer (Sep 24); present before SL-345. In a driven body, a closure that captures self (spelled [&self], or implicitly) and is called more than once panics at runtime on its second run. Mechanism: _materialize_receiver_capture (coro_transform.py) hands the receiver over as a one-shot move capture instead of a borrow. Sweep: [&self] vs implicit self; visitor closures (each/map) called per element; the &var self vs &self receiver; the generic-struct method family.
