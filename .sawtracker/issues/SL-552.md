**Review note (lead): SL-552.p1, unit A1.**

**The mechanism.** A new `compiler/coro` stage sits between mono and llvm (`compiler/llvm/src/api.saw` builds through `coro_lower_file`). A0's fixpoint MOVES there: `compiler/mono/src/suspends.saw` and `MonoProgram.suspending` are deleted, and `compiler/coro/src/suspends.saw` is the one suspension decision. Its entry points are `coro_solve_suspension`, `coro_call_is_source`, `coro_call_suspends` and `coro_item_suspends`.
- **The seeds** are all decided in `coro_call_is_source`: a flagged call whose target has no body (it keeps typecheck's answer, so the unanswerable case suspends), or whose target's summary is declared rather than body-derived. The second case is how A2's park intrinsics, which have placeholder bodies, become sources with no further edit (the Air's seam on SL-553 c6).
- **The fixpoint** is solved breadth first from the seeds over the reversed edges, so each item is marked once and its cause chain is a shortest one.
- **Cycles:** one `coro.suspending-cycle` refusal per strongly connected component of suspending items (iterative Tarjan). It names the shortest cycle through the component's first item and is anchored at that item's declaration. The wording is Stage 0's.
- **`coro.not-yet`** replaces A0's two `backend.not-yet` refusals in `bodies.saw`: one per item that needs a frame, at the call its suspension came through, with the cause chain. It is suppressed when a cycle is refused.
- **Invariant:** `coro_check_summaries` fails (`INVARIANT coro`) wherever typecheck's summary is unconditional and the decision disagrees at some instantiation, or a closure is decided suspending. The agent injected a fault (externs made non-sources): it reported 4 disagreements and correctly spared `run<Slow>`, whose summary is conditional.

**Pins:**
- the coro lane (`compiler/tests/coro/coro_lane.py`, in `run.py` and in the fixpoint lane's LANES): 3 goldens (`effects`, `chains`, `cycle_without_suspension`), 10 refusal fixtures and the agreement check over 75 programs;
- the 8 recursion shapes of the corpus, adapted to a `blocking` extern because their originals need A2, A4, A7c and A8b, plus a 9th, `.generic`, refused at `Slow` and not at `Fast`;
- the Air's negative pin, `run/cycle_without_suspension.saw`: a plain cycle and a generic cycle with flagged calls that reach no source at `Fast`. It builds, runs and matches Stage 0;
- A0's three run pins now expect the coro rules.

`effects.coro` prints `run … inst Slow (frame yes (via run -> Slow.step -> usleep))`, which closes SL-526's visible half.
