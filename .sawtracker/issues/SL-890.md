Found by SL-885's triage (p885). `net_precise_wakeup` and `net_precise_n_readers` counted spurious wakes through the private `net_buffer` layer. Rewritten to the public `TcpStream` API, they prove the outcome only (like `net_cancel_precise`), because std's `read` re-parks a spurious wake internally. Design 91's guarantee, that a read is woken only when its fd is ready, needs a direct pin: a runtime-level test, or a std test hook (`dump_tasks`-style counters) a test can read. Rule which, then pin it.
