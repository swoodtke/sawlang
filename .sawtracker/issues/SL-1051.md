The spec's "Prefix `*` — the pointer place, spelled" is marked **Status: implemented (design 219)**, and its example's last line is `let taken = move *p` ("the transfer, on an owning pointee"). sawc refuses that line with `slice.not-yet`: "`syntax.expr.move-deref.name` is not in the bootstrap slice yet" (`.build/scratch/p1000o/p10_prefix_frag.saw`, sawc at main). The other four forms build and run: the read `*p`, the store `*p = v`, `(*pt).x = 3`, `&var *p`, and `a * *p`.

**Expected:** `move *p` transfers an owning pointee, as design 219 says. Check its siblings in the same sweep: `move ptr[i]` (the pointer-place reads section, which builds per the review), `move (*pt).field`, and a `move *p` whose pointee is Copy.
