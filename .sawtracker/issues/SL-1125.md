## Description

**`(move p)!.label` is refused with an internal error.** Found by the reviewer while probing SL-1119.p1 (Nov 14; its probe `x19b_move_force.saw`, in `.build/scratch/air-review/sl1119_p1/agent/probes/` on the reviewer's machine: ask for it in chat). On main and on the patch alike.

- **What happens.** A forced unwrap of a moved Optional followed by a read of an owning field stops with `INVARIANT ... local _1 is dropped whole after a part of it moved out: a destructure does not dissolve it`.
- **Expected.** It builds: the Optional is consumed, the field moves out, the rest of the payload is dropped once.
- **The mechanism (UNVERIFIED, by its likeness to SL-1119).** SL-1119 was the `?.` form: a temporary taken apart by a field read was not dropped on the absent path. Here the local is moved, forced, and a part of it taken, and the whole is still dropped at its scope's end: the take does not forget the moved local as `take_from_temporary` forgets a temporary. Step 0 is the MIR of the smallest program, then the sweep SL-1119 used (the final read's type, the consumer, a local against a parameter against a field).
- **How badly.** An internal error on a legal program; `let q = move p` then `q!.label`, or `if let`, is the way round. Normal.

Related: SL-1119 (the `?.` form, fixed), SL-1088.
