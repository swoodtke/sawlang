Found by the Air (SL-898 c3; probes in its `.build/scratch/air-review/sl898/`). `git grep CLOEXEC -- runtime/ std/` is empty: `__saw_open_flags` returns bare flags, sockets and `accept` set none, and `proc_spawn_impl` creates the capture pipe with no `FD_CLOEXEC`, outside the env lock. The child between `fork` and `execvp` closes only its own pipe ends. Measured: a child holds the program's open file and listener; under 7 concurrent launchers every child holds 15-16 fds (other launches' pipe ends); 1-3 of 1,750 quick `output()` calls stall about 1 s, because a long-lived sibling child holds their pipe's write end. macOS has no `pipe2`/`accept4`/`SOCK_CLOEXEC`. **Folded into SL-898** (its B1 exec-errno pipe depends on it). The fix is the child-side close of every fd except stdio and the errno pipe, plus `O_CLOEXEC`/`SOCK_CLOEXEC`/`accept4`/`pipe2` where the platform has them. Close this issue when SL-898 lands.
