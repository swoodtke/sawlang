## Description

**Range subscripts that lend a view (SL:borrowing §6, Ruled) aren't built: `v[a..b]` on a `Vector` is `call.no-match`.** Found while scoping SL-971 unit 2.

**Probe** (`.build/scratch/p971/slice_probe.saw`): a `&[Int]` parameter and passing `&v` to it get through typecheck. `borrow let s = v[2..5] { … }` fails with `call.no-match`: "no candidate takes these arguments; the candidates are Vector.[](index: Int)".

**The ruled design (§6, with §12's block-scoped lends):**
- `&[T]` and `&var [T]` are a pointer and a length. They appear only as parameters and block-opening bindings, never in a field and never returned, except as a `borrows` accessor's lend (§7).
- A range subscript is a `borrows` call: `borrow let s = v[a..b] { … }` and `v[a..]` lend a view.
- A plain `vec[a..b]` on an ExplicitCopy parent (`Vector`) is refused, and `.copy()` on the view makes an owned copy. On a Copy-tier refcounted parent (`Data`, `String`), the copy is an O(1) window.
- Fixed arrays with a runtime range copy to a `Vector`.
- Whole containers coerce to `&[T]`.
- Strings are byte-indexed and panic inside a UTF-8 character.
- `s[i]` panics out of range and `s.get(i)` is optional.
- `&var [T]` allows element writes, `swap`, `sort` and `fill`, never `push`.
- An extern receives a pointer and a length.
- A slice held across a suspension is an ordinary borrow.

**Census first (obligation 4):** every §6 row on main, with compile-and-run evidence, for each parent kind (`Vector`, `[T; N]`, `Data`, `String`) and each operation:
- range forms `a..b`, `a..`, `..b` and `..`;
- shared and exclusive views;
- whole-container coercion;
- indexing and `get`;
- `&var [T]` writes, `swap`, `sort` and `fill`;
- a `push` through a view, which must be refused;
- a view across a suspension;
- an extern pointer-and-length;
- the refusals §6 names.

Then implement the missing rows through `borrows` accessors, following the place machinery (design 141/146), as one funnel per parent kind.

**Unblocks:** SL-971's range uses (the coroutine lifetimes copy and 4b's front-cache store) through `append(contentsOf: &[T])`.

**Scope widened (lead, Nov 6), found by SL-971 unit 2's step 1: slices aren't built in the backend at all, not only the range subscripts.** The front end accepts them: typecheck's `TcAdjustKind.Slice` coercion (`typecheck/src/adjust.saw:148`, `Vector` and arrays), and MIR's `MirRvalueKind.Slice`. The LLVM backend refuses every slice operation with `backend.not-yet` (`.build/scratch/p971/slice1.saw`, `slice2.saw`, and their logs):
- the `&v` to `&[T]` coercion, for both `Vector` and `[T; N]` (`llvm/src/ops.saw:68`);
- `len()` on a slice (`llvm/src/calls.saw:1569`), with `swap` refused the same way (:1592);
- indexing a slice, which also blocks taking an element's address (`llvm/src/places.saw:109`).

There is a typecheck gap too: §6 says `Data` coerces to `&[T]`, but `element_of_sequence` covers only arrays and `Vector`.

**Units:**
- **U1, slices in the backend:**
  - the pointer-and-length layout;
  - whole-container coercion from `Vector`, `[T; N]` and `Data` (`Data`'s typecheck half included);
  - `len()`, checked `s[i]` (which panics out of range), `get(i)`, and an element's address for an extern pointer-and-length;
  - through `&var [T]`: element writes, `swap`, `sort` and `fill`, with `push` refused;
  - a slice held across a suspension.

  Census first, the §6 rows that don't need a range subscript, each with compile-and-run evidence at -O0 and -O2, plus freestanding objects for riscv32 and aarch64.
- **U2, range-subscript views:** `v[a..b]`, `v[a..]`, `v[..b]` and `v[..]` lending `&[T]`/`&var [T]` through `borrows` accessors. The copy-policy rules (`vec[a..b]` refused for ExplicitCopy parents, O(1) windows for `Data` and `String`, a fixed array copying to a `Vector`). String byte-indexing with its UTF-8 panic. The refusals §6 names.

**Unblocks:** SL-971 unit 2 (`append(contentsOf: &[T])`) after U1, and its range uses after U2.

**SL-988.p1: unit 1, slices (`&[T]`, `&var [T]`) in the backend**

- **Census** (`.build/scratch/p988/census.md`, every §6 row needing no range subscript, at -O0 and -O2). On main:
  - **llvm** refused the coercion from `Vector` and `[T; N]`, `len`, `s[i]`, element writes, a generic `&[T]` parameter, a slice across a suspension, a slice into a closure, and a slice into a pool;
  - **typecheck** refused the `Data` coercion, plus `get`, `sort` and `fill`, which didn't exist;
  - **borrowck** refused `s.swap(0, s.len() - 1)`, a slice-only ordering bug.

  Every row that should work now prints its stated output. `push`, a write through `&[T]`, `&var [T]` beside another borrow, and a non-Sync element into a pool stay refused.
- **Layout:**
  - **MIR:** `slice(ref)` for an array, `slice(ref, parts)` for other containers, where `parts` comes from the container's view funnel. The reference stays an operand, so the slice keeps the container's loan.
  - **LLVM:** two words, the first element's address and the length, read and written only through `slice_start_length`/`store_slice`.
- **Per stage:**
  - **typecheck:** `Data` coerces to `&[UInt8]`. `get`, `sort` and `fill` are checked against their bounds and the receiver mode. The growth methods get a teaching refusal.
  - **MIR:** a slice method evaluates its arguments before reborrowing the slice, which fixes the swap row.
  - **llvm:** the coercion, reborrow, indexing with `slice: index out of range: 5 (len 3)`, `len` and `swap`.
- **std funnels:**
  - `Vector.view_parts`/`writable_view_parts` and `Data.view_parts`/`writable_view_parts`; Data's writable form separates shared bytes first;
  - `slice_get` (`T: Copy`), `slice_sort` (stable in-place, `T: Comparable`) and `slice_fill` (`T: ExplicitCopy`), with their ENTRY POINTS named.
- **Freestanding:** riscv32 and aarch64 objects at -O0 and -O2 need no new runtime symbol.
- **Pins:**
  - 11 `probes/slice_*.saw`, at -O0 and -O2, with `leaks: none` where they allocate. ASan is clean.
  - Refusal fixtures: 10 typecheck, 2 borrowck, 1 coro.
- **Ledger rows** (added by the lead):
  - T30: a slice index or `swap` out of range panics;
  - T31: `get` is optional;
  - X61: a `&var [T]` view holds its container exclusively.
- **Goldens moved:** `mir/golden/adjustments.mir` and `reborrow_captures.mir`, and `borrowck/golden/slices.borrowck`. Each changes for the view funnel and the argument-first reborrow, and is otherwise renumbered. `frontparts.saw` is regenerated for four new `TcKnown` fields.
- **Gates:**
  - `run.py --only seed,units,golden,subset,fixture_whitespace,std,std_suspension,typechecker,mir,borrowck,drops,mono,coro,front_parts,programs,sanitize`: ok;
  - corpusdiff: 2,676 hold;
  - docverify: ok.

**Follow-ups:**
- The slice funnels live at the end of `std/vector.saw`. A `std/slice.saw` module needs the loader's module list (SL-968).
- `Vector.sort` doesn't delegate to `slice_sort` until a seed bump, because the seed can't lower slices. So the sort algorithm exists twice for now.
