Found by SL-906's sweep (p906; probes `synth_writes_self.saw`, `synth_writes_self_uaf.saw` and `for_grow_alias__A` in its scratchpad). SL:borrowing §4: `@synthesize(shared)` derives the `(&self) borrows -> &T` twin by TYPECHECKING THE SAME BODY AGAIN under the shared signature, so a body that writes `self` is refused. sawc never does: it emits only the `receiver exclusive` instance and calls it from shared positions.
- An accessor whose prologue writes `self` is accepted; a read through a `&H` parameter mutates a `let h`.
- Two shared windows of an accessor whose prologue pushes onto `self.v` give a heap use-after-free on a `let` root.
- A synthesized `func cur(&var self) borrows -> CurX` with a `&var` field passes (the hand-written `&self` twin is refused `lends.origin`); under a `for` head it charges the root shared while `next` writes, and faults under ASan.

**The fix:** really derive and typecheck the shared twin (the §4 rule), and emit and call it from shared positions. std's `borrow var` arms in synthesized bodies (`map.saw` `find`/`[]`, `json.saw` `as_array`/`as_object`) must read as the lent type there (§2.7). **Obligation 4:** every `@synthesize(shared)` in std and the tests, plus probes of a prologue/epilogue write, a nested lend, and a borrowing-struct return. Conformance row first.
