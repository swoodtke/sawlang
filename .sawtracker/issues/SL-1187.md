A generic type's `deinit` that suspends through a bound `T` (its body calls a `T` method whose implementation suspends) crashes mono with an INVARIANT. It should be refused cleanly at the instance, the way a direct suspending `deinit` is.

**Repro:** `.build/scratch/p1156u7c/design_r2/probes/h01_generic_deinit_suspends.saw` (found by the SL-1156.p8 r2 design pass, Nov 25).

**Mechanism (UNVERIFIED):** the hook's effect is judged on the generic body alone. A suspension reached only through a bound is never judged at the instantiation. SL-1156.p8 r2 adds a per-instance mono judgment for roots (`judge_instance_roots`), and the effect side needs the same funnel for `deinit`, `copy()` and `format`.
