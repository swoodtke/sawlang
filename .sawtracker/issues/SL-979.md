## Description

**`match` lowering's decision tree grows steeply with mixed arms; a 350-arm `match` exhausts memory.** Found by the Air after SL-970 unit 3 (SL-970 comment, chat m667, repro f35 `m0089_250.saw`). Main's compiler grew past 20 GB on generated programs before it was stopped.

**Evidence** (unit 3's compiler, `mir --check`, the first K arms of one generated `match` over an `Int` plus its catch-all; arms mix literals, ranges, guarded wildcards like `case _ if x % 3 == 0`, and duplicate literals):

| arms | ranges | guarded | peak memory | `pick`'s MIR blocks |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 150 | 20 | 64 | 0.05 GiB | 25,063 |
| 200 | 25 | 90 | 0.25 GiB | 61,363 |
| 250 | 30 | 120 | 0.83 GiB | 246,368 |
| 300 | 37 | 155 | 2.07 GiB | — |
| 350 | 46 | 187 | over 4 GiB, killed | — |

At 250 arms the one function is 1.39 million lines of MIR. Typecheck peaks at 0.05 GiB throughout.

**Mechanism (the Air's reading, UNVERIFIED):** a row with no cell at the tested place goes down both edges of every test. That covers a guarded wildcard, since `_` simplifies away, and a range, which compares with no literal. A tree built from such rows copies each of them into every subtree of every earlier test, and each guard's false branch compiles the rows after it again. The tree's size then multiplies with the mix.

**Direction:** bound the code size of `match` lowering, keeping the arm semantics and diagnostics. Options:
- share identical subtrees: a DAG, memoised on (remaining rows, tested places);
- compile rows that take both edges by backtracking, falling through to the next row instead of duplicating it;
- a hybrid: a decision tree over literal columns and backtracking past guards and ranges.

Measure code size and compile time against arm count for each, and pin it with a complexity-lane shape (a mixed-arm `match`).

**Identity:** program behaviour must stay the same, but the MIR shape and IR will change. Golden MIR or IR dumps of `match` move once, reviewed.

**Unit A done; the lead picks option (c), the hybrid, plus continuing the guard's false edge in the loop.**

- **Mechanism (measured):** the blow-up needs a guarded arm whose test sends later rows down both edges.
  - A guarded range doubles the tree per arm: 26, 112, 450 … 114,698 blocks for 2 to 14 guarded ranges. A range's test is never decided (`tc_same_values` returns `Unknown`), and a guarded leaf compiles the rest of its rows on its false edge.
  - Guarded literals followed by ranges or guarded wildcards add a quadratic term (`split_literal_chain`'s `both` set).
  - Separately, `leaf` recursing once per guard is quadratic in memory: 860 MiB at 6,400 lit+guard arms.
- **Options at K=1,600, full mix with guarded ranges:**
  - tree: over 4 GiB at K=200;
  - (a) memo: 56,356 blocks, still superlinear, 15x the instructions;
  - (b) backtracking and (c) hybrid: 6,566 blocks each;
  - (b) or (c) plus the guard loop: lane r of 2.27, and 119 MiB at N=3,200.
- **Why (c):** on `Int` matches it equals (b), but it avoids backtracking's repeated tests on tuple and enum matches. All-literal matches and every std function are byte-identical. Diagnostics live in typecheck and can't move.
- **Behaviour:** 300 random mixed programs plus the 407 corpus programs holding a `match` give identical exit status, stderr and stdout.
- **Unit B:** implement (c) and the guard loop with no mode switches. Add complexity-lane shapes for a mixed-arm `match` with guarded ranges, plus a wildcard-heavy tuple `match`.
