Unit 6d of epic SL-398: **the borrow and exclusivity check** (SL:architecture §3.6). Package `compiler/borrowck/`, `sawborrowck`. It reads MIR, one function at a time, and accepts it or emits diagnostics. It needs no lifetimes and never looks outside the function: every loan starts and ends inside the function being checked, since references never escape and `lend`'s window is opened and closed by the caller.

**Two units, landing in order.** Each is briefed as a comment here when the one before it lands, and each is a patch on this issue.
1. **U6d1, the conformance matrix, then initialisation.**
   - **First commit (brief obligation 3):** the conformance matrix. Every row of `examples/conformance/INDEX.md`'s borrow-check sections (Mutability; References are parameters only; The Law of Exclusivity; Moves and use-after-move; Places, window discipline) maps to a sawc2 fixture or golden, or to a stated reason: owned by another stage (named), or outside the slice (`slice.not-yet`).
   - **Then the initialisation analysis:** a forward dataflow per place path (definitely initialised, maybe initialised, moved). That covers use after move, the refusal of a field move-out except `move self.<field>` in a `consumes` body (every path or none), and the facts §3.7's drop elaboration will reuse.
2. **U6d2, loans and conflicts:**
   - every `ref` and `window_open` is a loan (shared or exclusive), charging its root as declared (SL:borrowing §3);
   - authorisation: an access through the loan is its own use;
   - tracing through derefs and reborrows;
   - a loan carried by a borrowing struct (the Air's t21);
   - conservative overlap: distinct fields and distinct constant indices are disjoint, and dynamic indices overlap;
   - path sensitivity: an absent edge carries no loan;
   - suspension: a `borrows(sync)` loan must not span a suspension point (conditional ones included);
   - inside accessors (§2.7): the lent place's root, and a `borrows(sync)` loan live at `lend`;
   - statics;
   - diagnostics naming both sides.

**Acceptance, for the stage:**
- `compiler/`'s own source passes the borrow check whole;
- `std/` does too, under `--std-root std` (its accessors and windows are the real test);
- every conformance row in those five sections is accounted for;
- tests/corpus coverage is reported, plus a differential against Stage 0's verdicts on the corpus's refusal programs.

**Out of scope:** drop elaboration (§3.7, the next unit), mono, and codegen.

# U6d1: the conformance matrix, then the initialisation analysis (SL-460, the first of two borrow-check units)

- **Epic:** SL-398, unit 6d (SL-460). The issue has the split and the stage's acceptance.
- **Base:** main with the complete MIR stage (SL-452) and SL-459's MIR half (the double-drop and use-after-drop verifier).
- **Design:**
  - SL:architecture §3.6 (all of it, plus "Initialisation") and §3.7 (what drop elaboration will reuse);
  - the spec's moves, `consumes`, "moving a field out" and exclusivity sections;
  - SL:borrowing §2–§3 and §8a.
- **Package:** `compiler/borrowck/`, package `sawborrowck`. It depends on `sawmir`, is in the bootstrap subset, and has the checker and markers. Add `sawc2 borrowck (--dump | --check)`.

## Decisions (the lead)
1. **The conformance matrix comes FIRST, in its own commit** (brief obligation 3: a safety-surface brief writes its conformance rows first). `compiler/tests/borrowck/CONFORMANCE.md` lists every row of `examples/conformance/INDEX.md`'s five borrow-check sections:
   - Mutability (a non-`var` binding cannot be mutated);
   - References are parameters only;
   - The Law of Exclusivity;
   - Moves and use-after-move;
   - Places (`borrows`/`lend`), window discipline.

   **Per row,** with the row id:
   - (a) the sawc2 fixture or golden that covers it, under borrowck or under the earlier stage that already refuses it, named with its rule id;
   - (b) "U6d2", for the loan rows;
   - (c) "§3.7" for drop rows, or another named stage;
   - (d) `slice.not-yet` with the construct, if outside the bootstrap slice.

   No row is left unaccounted for. A lane check parses INDEX.md's five sections and fails on a row id missing from the matrix. This unit then fills the (a)s it owns.
2. **The initialisation analysis** is a forward dataflow per place path over the MIR's control-flow graph. Each place is **definitely initialised**, **maybe initialised** or **moved**.
   - **Use after move:** reading, borrowing, moving, or calling through a place that may have been moved, refused with both sites named (the move and the use).
   - **No partial moves:** moving out of a field or element is refused, except `move self.<field>` inside a `consumes` body, where each field leaves on every path or on none (the spec's "moving a field out"; SL-414's whole-receiver `move self`). MIR already refuses some of these shapes. Decide one owner per rule: the borrow check refuses, and an earlier refusal stays only if the spec's diagnostic needs it there. Record which.
   - **Re-initialisation:** assigning to a moved local makes it initialised again, and `x = v` through `&var` (whole-referent replacement, design 110) is a write, not a move.
   - **Path sensitivity:** joins are maybe-initialised; a conditional move on one arm makes the place maybe-moved after the join.
   - **The facts are exported for §3.7:** each `drop(p)` site is labelled definitely initialised, maybe initialised or definitely moved, in a side table drop elaboration will read. The dump shows them.
3. **The dump** (`sawc2 borrowck --dump`) shows each function's per-statement initialisation state for its owned places, compactly, and each `drop`'s label. It is specified in `compiler/tests/borrowck/README.md`.

## Tests
- Goldens per aspect: straight-line moves, branches and joins, loops (a move inside a loop is used again on the backedge), early exits, `consumes` field moves (all paths, none, and the refusal of some paths), re-initialisation, closures capturing by `move`, and the drop labels.
- A refusal fixture per rule, with a stable id; the lane fails any rule without one.
- **The verifier:**
  - every reachable statement has an initialisation state;
  - every `drop` has a label;
  - the labels agree with the MIR verifier's drop-if-initialised reading.
- **Acceptance:**
  - `compiler/` passes the initialisation check whole, with no refusal and no invariant;
  - `std/` passes under `--std-root std`;
  - the conformance matrix is complete;
  - corpus coverage is reported, plus a differential: for every tests/corpus program that expects a use-after-move or field-move error, sawc2 refuses it with the matching rule, or the matrix says why not.

## Rules
- **Where to work:** `compiler/borrowck/`, `compiler/tests/borrowck/`, the driver (the `borrowck` subcommand), `compiler/tools/build.py`, `compiler/tests/run.py` and the README lists.
- **Concurrency:** U5b2 runs concurrently in `std/`, `compiler/resolve/`, `compiler/typecheck/` and `compiler/tests/std/`. Don't touch those. `compiler/mir/` is free now, but change it only with approval in chat, in its own commit.
- **`sawc/` is frozen.**
- **Decisions:** a reversible reading goes in the report, and a change of meaning stops that item.
- **Gates:**
  - `run.py` per commit;
  - before reporting, `battery.sh compiler citations` under the lock;
  - `git diff --check`;
  - rebase if main moved.

## Report
- the commits and tip;
- the conformance matrix summary (counts per section: covered, U6d2, other stage, slice.not-yet);
- the initialisation lattice and the dump;
- the rule ids and fixtures;
- the drop labels for §3.7;
- acceptance, and the corpus differential;
- findings and readings;
- any rule slips;
- the diff size.
