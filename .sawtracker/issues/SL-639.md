**Typecheck walks over a type's parts are unmemoized, so a type that shares its parts costs 2^depth** (SL-606 bucket B, sibling of SL-627; found in SL-627's sweep).

- **Mechanism:** a walk that recurses into a type's arguments with no memo keyed by TypeId visits a shared part once per path to it, not once. Interned types are a DAG, so `type D0 = Int` with `type Dk = (D{k-1}, D{k-1})` has 2^k paths over k+1 types. SL-627 memoized the walks it holds (`unsafe_named`, `type_tier`, and the inline, bound, nesting and holds walks). Two remain, in files it does not hold:
  - `compiler/typecheck/src/refpositions.saw` `reference_in`: the reference-position funnel over a written type;
  - `compiler/typecheck/src/typeops.saw` `has_error`.
- **Repro:** `func wide(v: &D_N) {}` (the "plain" probe), and the same with `need<T: Equatable>(&v)` in the body. The generator is `.build/scratch/b1/gen_shared.py OUT N [plain]` in b1caps's worktree. `sawc2 typecheck --check --std-root std` on SL-627's tree:

  | N | plain | with the bound |
  |---|---|---|
  | 22 | 0.11 s | 0.37 s |
  | 24 | 0.38 s | 1.42 s |
  | 30 | does not finish | does not finish |

  Each two levels cost 4x. `sample` at N=29 and N=30 puts the time in `reference_in` (plain), and in `has_error` and `reference_in` (with the bound). Before SL-627, `unsafe_named` led the plain profile.
- **Fix:** memoize each by TypeId: program-wide where the answer depends on the type alone, as `TcProgram.walks` (walks.saw) does, or one memo per call where it depends on its position (`reference_in`'s allowance). Then census every remaining recursion over `types.arg` in compiler/ (typecheck, mir, mono, llvm, target) with this probe, a profile per stage, and take the next hotspot.
- **Pin:** the probe at N=40, with and without the bound, checks in well under a second, as a typecheck refusal fixture whose refusal follows the walks (`type.bound.shared-parts`).

**Air: a third exponential walk for this issue, from reviewing SL-627.p1:** `satisfies` under an open cycle (`compiler/typecheck/src/tier.saw` `satisfies_in`).

- **Mechanism:** SL-627 caches a pair's true answer only when no cycle it leaned on reaches below its frame. That is sound, but a pair that leans on an open cycle is reset to unknown and re-derived on every path to it. A DAG of shared parts under a cycle back to the root costs 2^depth.
- **Probe** (`.build/scratch/air-review/sl627/cyc/gen_pair.py OUT N`): `struct P<T> { a: T, b: T }` nested N deep in `struct A { x: P<…P<Vector<A>>…> }` (`extension A: NoCopy {}`), with `func need<T: Send>(v: &T) {}` called on `&A`. The written type is a chain, so this issue's `reference_in` and `has_error` stay linear and only `satisfies` is measured. Timed with SL-627's tree, `typecheck --check`:

| N | leaf `Vector<Int>` | leaf `Vector<A>` (cycle) |
|---|---|---|
| 16 | 0.01 s | 0.02 s |
| 20 | 0.01 s | 0.15 s |
| 24 | 0.01 s | 2.00 s |

  `sample` puts the time in `satisfies_in`, `nominal_satisfies` and `TcMemo.set`. Main before SL-627 times out (>60 s) already at N=16.
- **Fix sketch:** keep a provisional true reusable while the frame it leaned on is still open, with a per-push epoch, since frame numbers are reused after a pop. When the cycle's root finishes, promote those entries if it's true, and discard them if it's false.
- **Pin:** `gen_pair.py`'s cycle variant at N=40 checks in well under a second.
