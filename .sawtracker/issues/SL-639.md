**Typecheck walks over a type's parts are unmemoized, so a type that shares its parts costs 2^depth** (SL-606 bucket B, sibling of SL-627; found in SL-627's sweep).

- **Mechanism:** a walk that recurses into a type's arguments with no memo keyed by TypeId visits a shared part once per path to it, not once. Interned types are a DAG, so `type D0 = Int` with `type Dk = (D{k-1}, D{k-1})` has 2^k paths over k+1 types. SL-627 memoized the walks it holds (`unsafe_named`, `type_tier`, and the inline, bound, nesting and holds walks). Two remain, in files it does not hold:
  - `compiler/typecheck/src/refpositions.saw` `reference_in`: the reference-position funnel over a written type;
  - `compiler/typecheck/src/typeops.saw` `has_error`.
- **Repro:** `func wide(v: &D_N) {}` (the "plain" probe), and the same with `need<T: Equatable>(&v)` in the body. The generator is `.build/scratch/b1/gen_shared.py OUT N [plain]` in b1caps's worktree. `sawc2 typecheck --check --std-root std` on SL-627's tree:

  | N | plain | with the bound |
  |---|---|---|
  | 22 | 0.11 s | 0.37 s |
  | 24 | 0.38 s | 1.42 s |
  | 30 | does not finish | does not finish |

  Each two levels cost 4x. `sample` at N=29 and N=30 puts the time in `reference_in` (plain), and in `has_error` and `reference_in` (with the bound). Before SL-627, `unsafe_named` led the plain profile.
- **Fix:** memoize each by TypeId: program-wide where the answer depends on the type alone, as `TcProgram.walks` (walks.saw) does, or one memo per call where it depends on its position (`reference_in`'s allowance). Then census every remaining recursion over `types.arg` in compiler/ (typecheck, mir, mono, llvm, target) with this probe, a profile per stage, and take the next hotspot.
- **Pin:** the probe at N=40, with and without the bound, checks in well under a second, as a typecheck refusal fixture whose refusal follows the walks (`type.bound.shared-parts`).

**Air: a third exponential walk for this issue, from reviewing SL-627.p1:** `satisfies` under an open cycle (`compiler/typecheck/src/tier.saw` `satisfies_in`).

- **Mechanism:** SL-627 caches a pair's true answer only when no cycle it leaned on reaches below its frame. That is sound, but a pair that leans on an open cycle is reset to unknown and re-derived on every path to it. A DAG of shared parts under a cycle back to the root costs 2^depth.
- **Probe** (`.build/scratch/air-review/sl627/cyc/gen_pair.py OUT N`): `struct P<T> { a: T, b: T }` nested N deep in `struct A { x: P<…P<Vector<A>>…> }` (`extension A: NoCopy {}`), with `func need<T: Send>(v: &T) {}` called on `&A`. The written type is a chain, so this issue's `reference_in` and `has_error` stay linear and only `satisfies` is measured. Timed with SL-627's tree, `typecheck --check`:

| N | leaf `Vector<Int>` | leaf `Vector<A>` (cycle) |
|---|---|---|
| 16 | 0.01 s | 0.02 s |
| 20 | 0.01 s | 0.15 s |
| 24 | 0.01 s | 2.00 s |

  `sample` puts the time in `satisfies_in`, `nominal_satisfies` and `TcMemo.set`. Main before SL-627 times out (>60 s) already at N=16.
- **Fix sketch:** keep a provisional true reusable while the frame it leaned on is still open, with a per-push epoch, since frame numbers are reused after a pop. When the cycle's root finishes, promote those entries if it's true, and discard them if it's false.
- **Pin:** `gen_pair.py`'s cycle variant at N=40 checks in well under a second.

**SL-639.p1 (it carries SL-635 as a second commit).** Every finding reproduced on main. Each probe ran at depth 24–26 on the old binary (1.6–4.8 s) and at depth 40 on the patch (0.02 s).

- **`reference_in`:** program-wide memo per type. Only the outermost type depends on its position, and that is judged fresh at every position.
- **`has_error` and `mentions_param`:** they read a new `holds` bit set, which the interner records per type (error / parameter-or-`Self`; a projection inherits it through its base argument). The body verifier's private copy of `has_error` is gone.
- **`satisfies_in` under a cycle:** the provisional-true sketch, without an epoch. Every pair decided inside a frame settles when that frame closes: final, forgotten, or leaning on the lowest frame it reached. So a reused frame index never vouches for an older answer. The relink is quadratic in the worst case (pairs × depth).
- **`subst`:** an early return for a type holding no parameter or `Self` (the approved conform.saw edit, plus its import).
- **The sweep's five further walks:** `copies_silently` gets a per-type memo, since every tier is final before any body asks. `copy_needs`, `mentions_owner`, `mentions_unsolved`, `open_tier`, `mentions_unseen_param`, `borrowing_named`, `unify` and `peel` get per-call memos on branching types only. `unify` returns at once on identical parameter-free sides. The Air's SL-627 nit is taken: the memo hash masks keys to 32 bits before multiplying.
- **SL-635:** `comparable_bits` loses its depth cap. Its walk follows the paths `equal_at` emits a comparison along, over a finite type's inline members.

Approved patch SL-639.p1 (revision 2)

**Approved** (revision 2, the server's rebase onto SL-556's merge: its +/- lines and numstat are identical to revision 1, which I built and probed, applied with `--3way` to 4567c398d). The probes are in `.build/scratch/air-review/sl639/` on the Air.

**Every walk this issue names is now linear.** `typecheck --check`, 15 s limit, main against the patch:

| probe | N | main | patch |
|---|---|---|---|
| `func wide(v: &D_N) {}`, `Dk = (Dk-1, Dk-1)` (`reference_in`) | 24 / 40 | 0.35 s / TIMEOUT | 0.02 s / 0.02 s |
| the same plus `need<T: Equatable>(&v)` (`has_error`, `satisfies`) | 24 / 40 | 1.28 s / TIMEOUT | 0.01 s / 0.02 s |
| my c2 `gen_pair.py` cycle variant (`satisfies` under an open cycle) | 24 / 40 | 2.13 s / TIMEOUT | 0.01 s / 0.02 s |
| the no-cycle control | 40 | 0.02 s | 0.02 s |

The one probe that stays exponential is a *refused* shared type, `struct H { r: (D_N, &Int) }`: 1.1 s at N=24, and a timeout at N=40. That's SL-658, not a walk this patch owns. At N=16 the refusal line alone is 459 KB, because `type_text` spells out every alias.

**The provisional-true scheme is sound as written.** I read `satisfies_in`'s settle paths:
- **failure:** forget every pair decided since the frame's mark;
- **true with nothing leaned on below:** promote them;
- **true leaning below:** re-lean them on the lowest frame reached and keep them listed.

Every pair that leans on a frame was decided inside it, so it is settled when that frame closes, and a reused index can't vouch for it. In probes, sawc2 agrees with main on every query, in several orders:
- a cycle through `P<P<Vector<A>>>` whose root carries a raw pointer: `Vector<A>`, the pair type and `A` are each refused `type.bound`;
- a mutual cycle `A -> Vector<B> -> B -> Vector<A>` with the raw pointer only in `B`: `A`, `B`, a `C` holding an `A`, and `Vector<A>` are each refused;
- the same mutual cycle with nothing non-`Send` in it: all four accepted;
- a `Q` that holds by leaning on `A`'s open frame, after which `A` fails on its last member: `A` and `Q` are both refused.

**`reference_in`'s memo is position-independent.** `reference_part` caches a hit for a non-reference type only, and the part it stands in comes from that type's own structure. The one position-dependent case, a conditional lend's `Optional<&T>` at a `Lend` top, is intercepted before the memo, so neither order can leak an answer.

**SL-635:** `deep_equality.saw` prints the same 4 lines as Stage 0 at -O0 and -O2, and main refuses it with `backend.not-yet` on `L18`. Without the cap, `comparable_bits` is still an unmemoized walk over record parts. But it follows `equal_at`'s emission paths over inline parts, and a zero-sized type stops at the top, so its cost is bounded by the value's own size, not by a DAG of shared names.

**Gate checks:** `corpus.py` passes all 18 changed `.saw` files, and `astdiff` passes (its one parser rejection is a file Stage 0 can't parse).
