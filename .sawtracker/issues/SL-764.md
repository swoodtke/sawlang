1. Refuse a method call on a `Box<any T>` receiver with SL-712's no-forwarding refusal and its `borrow b.value().m()` fixit.

3. Rewrite the spec's existential example (and the other `b.m()` uses on erased boxes in the spec) to `borrow b.value().m()`.
4. Consumer sweep (obligation 2): the corpus programs, std and `compiler/` sites calling a method directly on a `Box<any T>`; rewrite each, and re-measure.
