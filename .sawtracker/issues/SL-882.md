## Description

**A `try!` panic says `try! failed` and drops the error it failed on** (found by the lead's triage of the coroutine corpus's wrong rows, Oct 25).

`tests/corpus/alloc_taskgroup_threads_forced_panics_named.saw` does `var pool = try! TaskGroup(threads: 2)` under a forced allocation failure, and states `// EXPECT-PANIC-CONTAINS: try! failed: allocation of`. sawc writes:

```
panic at alloc_taskgroup_threads_forced_panics_named.saw:25: try! failed
```

Stage 0 wrote `try! failed: allocation of 128 bytes (ali…`: the error rendered after the colon. Dropping it hides the error, against the never-hide-errors doctrine, and it's the one piece of the message a reader needs.

**Mechanism (UNVERIFIED):** the backend's `force_ok` (`compiler/llvm/src/render.saw`) renders the `Err` payload for `try!`. Either this path (an `init` returning `Result<Self, E>`, a fallible constructor) skips it, or it renders nothing for this `E` (`AllocError`).

**Obligation 4 sweep:** `try!` on a call, a method call, a fallible `init`, and an `Optional` (whose message has no payload). Cover `E` as a user enum, a struct, `SystemError`, `AllocError` and `Box<any Error>`, in sync and suspending bodies. The spec's wording of the `try!` message is the oracle; quote it.
