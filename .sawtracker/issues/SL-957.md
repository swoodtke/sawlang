Approved patch SL-957.p1 (revision 1)

**Air: SL-957.p1 approved.** Reviewed r1 on ccad40638.

**The mechanism is named and fixed at its cause, not deduplicated after the fact.** A window open's receiver reference (a `Ref` statement at the receiver's node) and the open's own root charge (`check_window_charge`, at the call's node) meet the same loans. Since `check_access` deduplicates once per node, the two nodes reported twice. `reported_access` gives the receiver reference the open's call node, so the existing dedupe reports the conflict once, where the window opens. The rules that read what a statement is keep the statement's own node, which `reported_access`'s docstring states. A second, *different* conflict at the same call is now reported after the first is fixed, rather than beside it; the program is still refused, which matches the existing first-conflict-per-node policy.

**Measured over the corpus, beyond the fixtures** (`.build/scratch/probe_dup_errors.py`). I compiled each of the 139 corpus programs whose recorded refusal is a borrowck-family rule (`loan.*`, `move.*`, `transfer.*`, `capture.*`, `borrowing.*`), with the base and then the patched compiler, and counted ERROR lines repeating the same rule and text on one source line:
- **base:** 11 programs, 23 extra lines: `loan.window-root` 9, `transfer.implicit-copy` 13, `borrowing.containment` 1;
- **patched:** 4 programs, 14 extra lines, with **`loan.window-root` 0**.

The remaining same-line pairs are distinct reports at different columns: V77's two arms of one `if` (34:23 and 34:34), and separate subexpressions in `borrowing_struct_value_positions.saw`. Counting with the column included gives **0** exact repeats both before and after, which tells you the old doubled report was the receiver/call column pair this patch merges, not an exact duplicate.

The lane's new `repeated_rules` check fails a refusal fixture whose rule appears on two ERROR lines. That pins the class at every borrowck fixture (172 checked); the four new receiver fixtures cover the other rules the mechanism reaches.

**Gates** (full logs, from `exit=` lines):
- `run.py --only` borrowck, drops, units, programs, subset, census, coro, sanitize, std, seed, golden and fixture_whitespace: `exit=0`;
- `battery.sh corpusdiff gmgate citations`: `exit=0`. Corpusdiff has all 2,675 holding (the moved columns change no recorded detail), and gmgate 101.

(SL-958's doubled `slice.not-yet`, from a pre-borrowck stage, is a separate mechanism and isn't touched here.)
