## Description

**Port the rest of `std.net` to the new std** (the owner A12b's deferred rows need; SL-570 c3/c4). A12b (SL-570) ported the minimal cooperative surface:
- `TcpStream.pair`, `read`, `write`;
- `TcpListener.listen`/`accept`, plus `local_port` and `connect` to an IPv4 literal;
- `IoError`/`IoErrorKind`.

**This issue owns the rest of Stage 0's `sawc/std/net.saw` public surface, per the spec's std.net section:**
- `Timed<T>` and the `timeout: Duration` twins of `accept`, `read`, `read_into` and `connect`;
- `read_into`;
- `set_no_delay` and `set_keepalive`;
- `IoError.from_errno`, if the spec keeps it public;
- host-name `connect` once A14b (SL-574) gives std the offload worker for the resolver. Until then, A12b's interim panic names A14b.

The raw layer stays private (the spec; SL-569 c4).

**From the Air's SL-570 review:** `IoErrorKind.of(tag:)` and `IoError.of(syscall:tag:)`/`of(syscall:kind:)` are `public` (as Stage 0's are), but they build values over the private seam's tag space. The spec's `IoError` surface is `kind()`, `code()` and the rendering, and SL-93 (DF-215e) raises the same concern about Stage 0's `from_errno`. If no module outside `std.net` builds an `IoError`, make them `public(package)` (with an `equivalence_exceptions.tsv` row). Decide this with this issue's port.
