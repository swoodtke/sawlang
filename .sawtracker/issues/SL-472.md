## The rulings (quoted; agents can't post kind=ruling)

## Decisions (the lead)
1. **SL-472, the funnel.** A by-value/move/copy capture is an immutable root in `place_verdict`, so every write shape is refused by the one funnel with `mutability.immutable` (or a capture-specific sub-id under it, your call): `x = v`, `x += v`, `x.f = v`, `v[i] = y`, a `&var self` call, `&var x`, `swap`/`take()`.
