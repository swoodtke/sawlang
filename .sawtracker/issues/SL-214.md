## Description

Unit E of SL-209 (ownership uniformity). Plan step 7. LAST in the sequence — makes completeness mechanically enforced.

## Make completeness mechanically testable
Add a VERIFIER before codegen that rejects missing, stale, or incompatible transfer decisions. Then test the interaction matrix across:
- Sources: owned parameters, locals, captures, fields, indexed places, and borrowed parameters.
- Copy tiers: every tier, including refcounted aggregates.
- Positions: bindings, arguments, explicit returns, tails, branches, wrappers, and loop results.
- Variants: generic/concrete and sync/suspending.

Test oracles:
- Compile-error assertions for illegal transfers.
- Destructor/refcount assertions for legal ones.
- Guard Malloc for lifetime failures.
- Tests that DELIBERATELY remove a decision and prove the verifier catches it.

## Completion criterion (for the whole SL-209 initiative)
A new expression form cannot silently bypass ownership checking: it MUST declare how it produces or forwards a value before codegen accepts it. The verifier is what turns that from a convention into an enforced invariant.
