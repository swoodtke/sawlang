User ruling (Oct 25): deleting an agent's VM clone is standard practice at landing, like removing its local worktree. Stale clones (`~/sawlang/<checkout>`, made by `tools/linux.py sync`) filled the `sawlang-linux` VM to 93% (14 dead clones, about 15 GB), skipping the Linux lane. Add a `tools/linux.py prune` that lists `~/sawlang/*` in the VM, compares them with `git worktree list` (by basename, the name `sync` gives a clone), and removes every clone with no live worktree, printing what it removed and the space freed; plus `--dry-run`. Cover it in `tools/test_linux.py` (a fake VM listing). Optionally, `sync` warns when the VM disk is above 85%.
