## Description

**Typecheck grants primitives builtin conformances the spec doesn't give, and mono refuses each one later, citing closed SL-715.** SL-715's last comment found the first two cells and named the fix (`primitive_grants` made per trait, as the spec lists them). The issue closed without that fix. SL-962's sweep (`.build/scratch/p962/sweep.md`, `matrix_after.txt`) found the rest still live on main.

**Cells typecheck accepts as a bound, which mono then refuses as `mono.not-yet`:**
- `Bool`: `compare` and `<` under `Comparable`. The spec's "Ordering" lists integers, `Float` and `String`.
- Raw pointers (`UnsafePointer`, `UnsafeMutablePointer`): `equals` and `==` under `Equatable`, and `hash` under `Hashable`. "Equality" lists integers, `Bool`, `Float` and `String`.
- `FuncPointer`: `equals`, `==`, `compare`, `<`, `hash`, `format`, `to_string` and `print("{}")`. `primitive_grants` treats it like a number, but the spec says only that it copies bitwise and is compared through `address()`.

**Mechanism:** `primitive_grants` (`compiler/typecheck/src/tier.saw`) is one blanket rule: every non-pointer primitive gets all five value traits, and the raw pointers get ExplicitCopy and Equatable, with Hashable through the trivial rule. The spec's lists are per trait.

**Fix:** make `primitive_grants` per trait, as the spec lists them, at every position a bound is checked: a call, a generic type argument, a bounded extension. The over-granted bounds become typecheck's bound error. mono's named refusals become unreachable, and their fixtures in `compiler/tests/mono/refuse/` (`bool-order`, `pointer-equality`, and the `std`/`derived`/`aggregate` cells citing SL-715) move to typecheck refusals.

**One spec question first (user):** "Hashing" says "primitives and `String`". Are raw pointers and `FuncPointer` primitives for hashing? The spec's other lists name raw pointers nowhere.

**Ruled (user, Nov 4): grant per the "just numbers" reading, and change the spec to match.**
- **`Bool` is `Comparable`, with `false < true`.**
  - The spec's Ordering list gains `Bool`.
  - The lowering compares unsigned (`icmp ult` on `i1`; a signed compare would make `true < false`), with a pin for it.
  - `@synthesize Comparable` on a type with a `Bool` field then derives.
- **Raw pointers (`UnsafePointer<T>`, `UnsafeMutablePointer<T>`) get `Equatable`, `Hashable`, `Comparable` and `Printable`, all by address**, on top of the `ExplicitCopy` they already have. `Hashable` requires `Equatable`, so the cell SL-715 called an over-grant becomes a spec addition.
- **`FuncPointer` gets the same four, by code address.** The spec states the rule: equality is identity of the code address. It already promises that pointers built from one named function hold one address, and sawc never asks the linker to fold identical functions. Printing shows the address in hex.

**So the fix:**
- the spec's Equality, Ordering, Hashing and Printing lists name these types;
- `primitive_grants` (tier.saw) grants per trait, as the spec lists them;
- mono lowers each cell instead of refusing it, on the address as an unsigned integer;
- the `compiler/tests/mono/refuse/` fixtures citing SL-715 become run-lane or corpus programs stating their output;
- every other refusal that cites SL-715 is checked, and either becomes unreachable or is re-pointed.

**SL-964.p1: primitive conformances per trait, as the spec lists them**

**What changes** (rulings of Nov 4):
- **`Bool`** is `Comparable` with `false < true`, compared unsigned.
- **Raw pointers** (`UnsafePointer`, `UnsafeConstPointer`) and **`FuncPointer`** are `Equatable`, `Hashable`, `Comparable` and `Printable` by address. Printing writes `0x` and lowercase hex, through one module-private `StringBuilder.append(address:)`.
- **`Void`'s spec-granted `Equatable` and `Hashable`** now lower. `equals` is `true`, `hash` writes nothing, and `==`/`!=` on a zero-sized operand are answered directly. Before, mono refused them, and with them `(Int, Void)`, `Void?` and `[Void; 2]`.
- **LANGUAGE_SPEC:** the Equality, Ordering, Hashing and Printable sections list the new types. Two pointers built from different functions never compare equal.

**Mechanism:**
- `primitive_grants` (tier.saw) is per trait instead of one blanket rule.
- `builtin_comparable` (bodies.saw) admits the direct operators.
- The member glue table (glue.saw) lowers every granted cell.
- `ops.saw` compares `FuncPointer` by address.

**Sweep** (`.build/scratch/p964/plan.md`, `sweep_*.txt`, `matrix_*.txt`): every type the grants and structural rules cover, against all ten value-trait routes through a bound and eleven directly. After the change, no cell that typecheck accepts is refused by mono. The one exception is `Float`'s generic `<=`/`>=`, which is SL-722 and unchanged.

**Pins** (run-lane probes, -O0 and -O2):
- `bool_comparable_order`: `true > false`, `false < true`, and a sort giving `false true true`.
- `pointer_value_traits`: offsets order, equal pointers hash equal, `Set` and `Map` keys work, and `0x1000 0x0 0xfff0` prints.
- `func_pointer_value_traits`: one function's pointers are equal and hash equal, two functions' are unequal, and `Map` keys work.
- `func_pointer_identical_bodies_unequal`: two functions with identical bodies, and two identical closures, compare unequal. Checked on macOS at every -O level, and in the Linux VM on aarch64 (whole, split, thin and full LTO, with lld 19) and x86_64.
- `void_equatable_hashable`.

**Refusal fixtures:**
- The five citing SL-715 are removed, and their programs are now stated probes.
- `generic-instances` and `task-root-instance` trigger on SL-722's generic `<=` at `Float`.
- `mono.not-yet.std` triggers on SL-966, the `T()` construction of an allocator with fields, still reported at the program's call with std's location in the message.
- A refusal through tuple or derived glue is now unreachable, and `compiler/tests/mono/README.md` states the invariant.

**Goldens:** `hello.ll` was re-recorded. All 76 changed lines are panic-site line numbers in `stringbuilder.saw`, shifted by the new routine.

Approved patch SL-964.p1 (revision 1)

**Air: SL-964.p1 approved.** It implements the Nov 4 ruling: `primitive_grants` grants per trait as the spec now lists them, the glue table lowers every granted cell, `ops.saw` compares `FuncPointer` by address, and `Void`'s `Equatable` and `Hashable` lower. The base is main's Stage 2 at 00ad69459, saved before applying.

**The unsigned rule, probed where a signed compare would show:**
- **Host, ASan, -O0 and -O2** (`order.saw`): `4096 as UnsafePointer<Int>` against `Int.min as UnsafePointer<Int>`, whose top bit is set and which prints `0x8000000000000000`.
  - It orders `low < high`, `high > low`, `!(high < low)`, directly and through `below<T: Comparable>`, and `compare` gives `Less`/`Greater`. A sort puts `low` first.
  - `Bool`: `false < true`, `!(true < false)`, `true > false`, through the bound too, `true.compare(&false)` gives `Greater`, and `[true, false, true]` sorts to `false true true`.
  - A `@synthesize`d `Comparable` struct with a leading `Bool` field orders `on: false` below `on: true`.

  Every line is identical at both speeds. The base refuses the program, since a raw pointer wasn't `Printable` before.
- **riscv32 freestanding IR** (`order_fs.saw`, exports through `UInt8`, -O0 and -O2), where the 32-bit threshold `0x8000_0000` is where a signed compare would break:
  - the direct pointer `<` is `icmp ult ptr`;
  - `member glue Comparable.compare() on UnsafePointer<Int>` is `icmp ult ptr` plus `icmp eq ptr`;
  - the `Bool` direct `<` and its compare glue are `icmp ult i8` plus `icmp eq i8`.

  None of the probe's functions or their glue contain a signed ordering compare.

**Pins and the rest:**
- **The five new pins** (`bool_comparable_order`, `func_pointer_identical_bodies_unequal`, `func_pointer_value_traits`, `pointer_value_traits`, `void_equatable_hashable`) match their stated output at -O0 and -O2 (`.build/scratch/probe_stated.py`), with `ll omits` held.
- **Call-signature census** (`.build/scratch/probe_call_signatures.py`): all 2,675 corpus programs plus the new pins, SL-961/962's pins and `order.saw` give **1,591 compiled, 0 mismatches**.
- **No remaining citation of SL-715** anywhere in `compiler/`, `std/`, `tests/` or the spec.
- **`hello.ll`'s move is line numbers only:** `stringbuilder.saw`'s new `append(address:)` shifts its panic sites (75 to 81, 113 to 119, and so on).
- **Gates:**
  - `run.py --only mono,programs,std,subset,typechecker,coro,golden,units,census,parallel_order`: `exit=0`, with the run lane at 520 stated programs, the five new ones included;
