## Description

**The run probe `executor_records_reused` passes or fails by timing: on aarch64 Linux at `-O2` it prints "1 executor records" in about one run of eight, where it states 2.** Found by the lead's whole Linux lane on main after SL-1113 (Nov 14, main dea960bdc): the lane's ONLY failure on aarch64 (x86_64 was green), `.build/scratch/p1113f/linux_lane_after.log`:

`run compiler/tests/run/probes/executor_records_reused.saw -O2: exit 0 (expected 0); output: line 1: printed '4000 tasks, 1 executor records', expected '4000 tasks, 2 executor records'`

Measured in the VM with the binary built from main: `-O0` 30 of 30 print 2; `-O2` 26 print 2 and 4 print 1.

**Why.** The probe runs 2,000 rounds of two `Thread.spawn` bodies, each driving a one-task `TaskGroup()`, and states that the executor makes two thread records in all. Two is the most it can make; it makes two only if in some round both threads hold a record at once. Nothing in the probe makes them overlap: when the first body finishes and gives its record back before the second claims one, in every round, one record serves all 4,000 tasks. Both outcomes are correct behaviour of the executor; the probe states one of them.

**The fix (test only):** make the overlap an event. Each round's two bodies meet at a gate before either ends (each announces itself with an atomic and waits, with a deadline per TESTING.md rule 3, until the other has announced), so both threads hold their records at the same moment in every round; the stated line stays "4000 tasks, 2 executor records" and is then exact. Keep what the probe is for (a record is REUSED once its thread ends: 4,000 threads, two records), and its conformance row's wording if it has one (the header cites one; check the ledger).

**Sweep (obligation 4):** the other probes and corpus programs that state a count which is only an upper or lower bound by timing: grep the run probes and `tests/corpus/` for thread or task counts in `// prints:` / `EXPECT` lines next to `Thread.spawn`, `TaskGroup(threads:`, record, worker or pool counts; for each, say whether the stated number is forced by an event or by timing. Fix those that are by timing; list the rest.
