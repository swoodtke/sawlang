## Description

**Frames of a function are large because sawc emits no `llvm.lifetime.start`/`end`: every scoped stack slot lives for the whole function.** Measured by SL-1158's sweep (Nov 18): a function with three `print`s of a Printable keeps three 512-byte StringBuilder buffers (1696 bytes of allocas) though no two are live at once; on the ESP32-S3 (small stacks, and esp-clang's call0 crash window at ~1-2 KB frames) that matters. Emit lifetime markers for a slot whose live range is a scope (format-argument scratch, block-scoped locals), so LLVM's stack coloring overlaps them; measure frame sizes before and after on the freestanding suite and the corpus. Related: SL-1158.
