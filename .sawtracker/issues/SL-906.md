Found by the Air (SL-903 c4; probes s6, s7, s9, t5 and s9old_* in its SL-897.p1 verdict directory). On MAIN, in every spelling (`match borrow`, `match borrow var` with `case Some(borrow var e)`, and §12's `match v.find(0) { case Some(var e) -> … }`): when the accessor has a `@synthesize(shared)` twin (std's `Vector.find`, `Vector.[]`), the scrutinee takes the SHARED form, but an arm's `var` payload binding WRITES through it. So `match v.find(0) { case Some(var e) -> { borrow let x = v[0] { e.n = 5 } } }` is accepted with `e` and `x` aliasing one element. Escalated: a reference into `e.items` taken through `x`, then `grow(&var e.items)`, is a heap use-after-free in safe code. Borrowck itself reports the window as 'holds `v` shared'.

**Mechanism (UNVERIFIED past the probes):** the least-privilege twin pick (§4) is made from the scrutinee's POSITION alone, never from what the arms bind. A binding that writes (`var`, or a write through a payload) needs the exclusive accessor, and the root charged exclusively.

**Obligation 4 sweep:** every place where a binding's mode is decided separately from the twin pick: a `match` arm (`var` payload, nested patterns, several arms with mixed modes), `if var`/`while var`, `for var`, a `borrow var` block over a twinned accessor, a forwarded `lend` (§2.7's lent-type pick), and a borrowing struct. Each case with a twinned accessor and an untwinned one, under ASan. The fix: ONE place where the binding modes of a position's bindings drive the twin pick and the root charge. Conformance row first (obligation 3).

**SL-906.p1 submitted** (1c7f2a944, on e11a6f01a).
- **The funnel:** `lend_mode` (typecheck `bodies.saw`, beside `lend_window`) is the ONE place a named lend's subject takes its use from its bindings' modes. A `match` over a borrowed or lent place whose arms bind `var`/`borrow var` at any depth is a WRITE, so the twin pick goes exclusive, MIR opens an exclusive window, and borrowck charges the root exclusively. Read-only places (`borrow let`, `&self`, `&T`, a `let` root) are refused with `mutability.immutable`. A subscript scrutinee with a writing arm takes the place role (the lost write fixed). An owned scrutinee keeps the mutable copy.
- **The backstop:** `verify_exclusive_refs` (MIR `verify.saw`) treats an exclusive ref through a shared one as an invariant. It exempts raw pointees, cell-carrying referents, and the `&self`-accessor receiver (removed with SL-903).

| probe | where the `match` sits |
|---|---|
