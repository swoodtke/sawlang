**User direction (Oct 29):** port Blade, the package manager written in Saw (`blade/`, 36 files, ~5,200 lines, FROZEN in v0.18.0's language and built by `sawc0` today), to the self-hosted `sawc`, as a rehearsal before the sawos batch update. sawos builds its root and 144 test packages through Blade, so a sawc-built Blade also removes that snag (SL-865 Q5).

**Shape:**
1. A read-only survey: build Blade with `sawc` against `std/`, bucket every diagnostic (language migration incl. `sawc fix`; new std; a sawc gap with its owner; a probable sawc bug with a repro), and size the port. Also: how Blade invokes the compiler (`SAWC`, `bin/blade` setting `sawc0`), and its own tests.
2. Unfreeze `blade/` in place (it leaves `tools/frozen_trees.tsv`), port it to the current language and std, and switch `bin/blade` and Blade's compiler invocation to `sawc`.
3. Blade's own tests, and building `libs/` packages and the freestanding suite's former Blade case through it, become a gate.

Language bugs found while porting are filed, never coded around. Child of SL-661 (the frozen trees' retirement).

**Survey done (p936; `.build/scratch/p936/report.md`).** The port is small and mechanical. Main's sawc rejects Blade with 66 unique diagnostics:
- **50 are std renames,** in 14 files and about 57 lines: `Path.as_str` → `as_string`, `ext` → `file_extension`, `Env.get`/`Env.cwd` Results, `Command.run` → `wait`, `String as UnsafePointer` → `c_string()!`, and a `std.data` import for `to_data`.
- **16 are one gap:** `std.cbor` and `std.serde` are absent. They sit in the CBOR half of `lock.saw` and in `tests/lock_cbor.saw`. serde is SL-833; no issue owns the cbor port yet.
- **No borrow-spelling migration:** `sawc fix` has nothing to rewrite.
- **No sawc bugs** surfaced in about 650 invocations.

**On a scratch copy, with the renames fixed and the CBOR half removed:**
- Blade builds with sawc and builds itself.
- `blade test` passes 21/21, and libs/ passes 10/10 with no source edits.
- `update` writes a byte-identical `Saw.lock`.
- The freestanding `bladepkg` case boots under QEMU on riscv32 and aarch64.

**Proposed units:**
- **U1, mechanical:** unfreeze blade/ and libs/, apply the renames, switch `bin/blade` and the Makefile to sawc, and use `--runtime`.
- **U2, the gate:** a Blade lane as a path-aware gate part and a battery stage, and un-skip `bladepkg`.
- **U3, optional:** an idiom pass.
- **U4, in sawos:** a `blade=` module mapping in sos_runner.

**Open decision for U1:** drop the CBOR half now and have the cbor port restore it as its pin (recommended), or hold U1 for SL-833 plus a cbor port.
