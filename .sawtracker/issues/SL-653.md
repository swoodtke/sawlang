**The orientation digest in CLAUDE.md misstates member visibility** (found by the Air, chat m562, while reviewing SL-652). It says "struct fields + extension methods are private-by-default outside the defining module (design 80)". LANGUAGE_SPEC's visibility section says otherwise. A field inherits its struct's tier: a `public struct`'s bare fields are public, readable and memberwise-constructible from anywhere, and only a field marked `private` is narrowed. An extension member carries its own visibility, and a bare one is module-private. Fix: the digest line states the spec's rule.

Approving SL-653.p1: CLAUDE.md's digest now states member visibility as LANGUAGE_SPEC's "Member visibility" section does. "A field's default is its declaring type's visibility", the fields of a `public struct` are public, and "`private` narrows a field back down". A bare extension member is module-private ("a declaration with no modifier is already module-private"). The spec's "Cross-module memberwise construction" passage gives the memberwise half. Exact tree on 61bb6c754: 6d29aff0.

A hair-split, not worth a revision: the spec says such a field is "readable and writable" cross-module, and the digest says "readable and memberwise-constructible". Construction and reading are what mislead an agent, which is what happened to me.

Approved and armed; it merges when the gate passes on the current base.
