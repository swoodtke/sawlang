**The orientation digest in CLAUDE.md misstates member visibility** (found by the Air, chat m562, while reviewing SL-652). It says "struct fields + extension methods are private-by-default outside the defining module (design 80)". LANGUAGE_SPEC's visibility section says otherwise. A field inherits its struct's tier: a `public struct`'s bare fields are public, readable and memberwise-constructible from anywhere, and only a field marked `private` is narrowed. An extension member carries its own visibility, and a bare one is module-private. Fix: the digest line states the spec's rule.
