**U7a, the walking skeleton.** sawc2 has no stage after drop elaboration, so nothing it compiles runs. This unit proves the whole path with the thinnest honest slice:
- monomorphization (SL:architecture §3.8);
- one target-description module that owns layout (§3.10);
- a textual LLVM IR backend (§3.10);
- `sawc2 build`, linking Stage 0's prebuilt Saw runtime (§3.11).

**First milestone:** `print("hello, world")` builds against the new std and runs.

**Then:** a differential lane of small programs, each built by sawc2 and by Stage 0, with their output compared.

**Scope:** whole-program builds only. Unhandled MIR constructs get clean, located refusals. Coroutine lowering, separate compilation and the VM are out of scope.

The brief follows as a comment. It's the first step on the critical path to self-hosting, which the lead's Oct 10 breakdown put at 5 to 8 weeks.
