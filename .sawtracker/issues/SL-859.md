## Description

**Lift the "v1 fences" that sawc2 already handles: a suspending `borrows` body, `consumes` that suspends, and their siblings** (the user, Oct 23: "can we loosen the suspending rules based on what sawc2 currently does, e.g. for borrows and consumes?").

Several suspension rules are v1 FENCES, limits of Stage 0's implementation written as language rules, not semantic rules:
- **conformance P08:** "a `borrows` body is `sync`. `lend` suspends the accessor and the caller resumes it at window close, which a coroutine frame cannot also be doing";
- **design 260 v1:** a `consumes` method may not both move a field out of `self` and suspend (`errors/consumes_suspending_field_move`), nor suspend when its type has a hand-written `deinit` (`errors/consumes_suspending_handwritten_deinit`).

sawc2 ACCEPTS all three corpus programs (they're `wrong` rows in `corpus_diff.tsv`). Accepting isn't proof of correctness, so each fence is lifted only on evidence.

**Unit 1 (a read-only sweep):**
1. **Inventory every v1 fence:** LANGUAGE_SPEC.md, the SL design docs (SL:coroutines, SL:borrowing, SL:architecture), designs/ 260 and kin, and the corpus rows whose directives expect a fence refusal ("v1", "not supported in", "fence"). For each: the rule text, its stated reason, and sawc2's verdict today.
2. **For each fence sawc2 accepts,** probe correctness with a matrix built from the fence's reason:
   - **A suspending `borrows` body:** suspend in the prologue, in the epilogue, or both; the lend unconditional or conditional; the caller a task, a pool task, or a sync wait that drives; cancellation during the window; two windows nested LIFO.
   - **`consumes` that suspends:** a field moved before or after the suspension; a hand-written `deinit` (it must run exactly once, after the moved field leaves); cancellation at the suspension; the task dropped unjoined.

   Every cell runs at -O0/-O2 under `leaks` and ASan, and states its print order (drop order, deinit count).
3. **Recommend, per fence:** LIFT (sawc2 is right everywhere: the spec text, the corpus program's directive and the conformance row change), FIX THEN LIFT (name the defect), or KEEP (a real semantic reason; say what it is).

Unit 2 (after the user rules on unit 1's recommendations) does the spec changes, the corpus flips and the pins.
