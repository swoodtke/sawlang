**Found by:** the Air, reviewing SL-505.p1 (U7a). U7a's backend is the first stage to run this MIR. Repros are in chat file f22 (m454): `census.saw`, plus the `Vector<String>`, `Box<Noisy>` and `Optional<String>.take()` programs. **Area:** MIR lowering, `compiler/mir/src/lower.saw` `lower_assign`.

**The defect:** `lower_assign` emits `emit_drop(p)` before every write to an owning target, and that includes a slot reached through a raw pointer. The spec (Unsafe surface: "a pointer place tracks no occupancy") makes `ptr[i] = move v` a placement into storage that holds no value, and the new std relies on that (`std/vector.saw`, `std/box.saw`).

Census, from `drops --dump` over the std the corpus reaches: `Box.make`, `Vector.push`, `Vector.set` and `Optional.take_payload`. In the last two the slot was just moved out, so the drop is a double drop.

Under U7a's backend:
- `Box<Noisy>.make` and `Vector<Noisy>.push` deinit garbage;
- `Vector.set` drops the old element twice;
- `Optional<String>.take()` prints garbage and then underflows the refcount;
- `Vector<String>` with a few pushes segfaults.

**Fix:** a whole-slot write through a raw-pointer index or `*p` emits no drop. Settle from the spec whether a field write through a pointer (`p[i].f = v`) drops. Keep the drop for `arr[i] = v` on a fixed array, which is tracked storage.

**Mechanism sweep:** check every MIR path that writes a place, not only plain assignment: compound assignment, `swap`, a payload write and a field projection rooted at a pointer.

**Pins:** run-lane programs with owning element types.

Being fixed in SL-505.p1's revision as a named MIR edit.

**Landed (with U7a):** `lower_assign` asks `pointer_step` whether a target is reached through a raw-pointer index or `*p`. If it is, the whole-slot store emits no drop, per the spec's placement writes ("No destination release"). A write to an owning part of a pointee (`p[i].f = v`) is refused as `slice.not-yet` (`mir/refuse/pointer-part`), since the spec doesn't settle it. Compound writes through a pointer never drop. `a[i] = v` on a fixed array, a write through `&var`, and a setitem through a window keep their drops. Pins: golden `placements`, `tests/std/mir/pointer_stores.mir` (the four std sites: `Box.make`, `Vector.push`, `Vector.set`, `Optional.take_payload`), and run-lane `owning_vector`, `owning_box` and `optional_take`.

Landed in SL-505.p1 revision 4, merged as 7e04eaa0 after the Air approved r2 and r4 (r4 is r2 rebased, plus two regenerated records). The gate passed on the applied commit c1308724.
